## Three things you do

**Capture.** Something happened: a client result, a conversation, an opinion. Tell the studio with `/capture`, or drop the file in `inbox/`. Don't tidy it up first.

**Decide.** The studio stops at two gates and waits for you: the brief before a draft, the piece after review. `/review` is where you say approve, ship, revise, hold or kill. The studio never publishes; you do.

**Review.** Now and then the studio proposes a change to your files, in `proposals.md`. Nothing about you changes without your yes.

## Your week

- Whenever it happens: capture. Two minutes.
- Once a week: `/status`, approve the briefs, decide on the pieces. About twenty minutes of your 4 hours; the rest goes to the pieces themselves and whatever you keep feeding it (Four client onboarding calls, recorded, not transcribed yet; LinkedIn posts, about 60, export in inbox/linkedin-posts.csv; A board-prep checklist I send clients).
- Every quarter: the proposals you've put off, your content plan, "continue my brand tree".
