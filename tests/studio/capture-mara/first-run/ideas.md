## I7 · Cash months, and why not "runway"
status: ready
raw: "FOUNDER: What's cash months, exactly? Runway? MARA: Same idea, I just don't like the word. Runway sounds like you're about to take off. Cash months is cash in the bank divided by what you burn net in a normal month. Money in minus money out. If you've got 400 in the bank and you net burn 100, you've got four months. I want every founder I work with to be able to say that number without opening a laptop."
