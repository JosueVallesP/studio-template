Voice memo, 2026-10-22, in the car.

Thing I keep noticing. The founders who get into cash trouble are not the careless ones. They're the ones with the best growth. Growth is what makes you hire, hiring is what eats the cash, and the P&L rewards you for the growth right up until the week you can't make payroll. I've watched it four times now, including the two in 2024. I think the honest version of my job is that I'm paid to be the person in the room who isn't excited.

Also, note to self, I should write down what the Monday call actually is, because I keep saying "the Monday call" like everyone knows. It's twenty minutes. We open the one page, we look at the next thirteen weeks, we look at which week is lowest, and we decide one thing. That's it. If there's nothing to decide we hang up early.
