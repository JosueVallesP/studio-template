## S1 · The 2019 plaza nobody sat in
status: draft
raw: "2019. A town about an hour from Guadalajara had a plaza that another firm redid in 2015. Granite paving edge to edge, a steel sculpture, maybe twelve meters, four trees. It won an award. The municipality hired us in 2019 because nobody used it. I went on a Tuesday in May and sat there from one to two in the afternoon. I counted. Nobody sat down. Forty-something people crossed it to get to the bus stop on the other side. The granite was too hot to touch. We took out a third of the paving, planted trees in a grid, put benches with backs under them, and moved the sculpture to the edge so it wasn't in the way. A year after we finished, the food carts were back and the old men were playing dominoes under the trees."
