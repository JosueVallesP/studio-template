# LinkedIn

Written by `strategy-brand` on 2026-10-21. `fmt-linkedin-post` reads this file for purpose, cadence and mix. Covers the feed, the profile, DMs and connection requests; the method treats them as one channel.

## Job
Get found and prove the thesis one leaf at a time, and turn a reader into a conversation. Why this channel does this job for Mara: it is the only channel she has (1,900 followers, `vehicle.md`), her reader is a business buyer and gets found here, and she won't do video, so written posts are her medium. The conversation half of the job matters because her goal rules out sales calls she doesn't want; a reply to a post or a DM is how she decides who to talk to.

## Reader here
- Segment: G1, bootstrapped SaaS founder, 2 to 10M ARR, 8 to 40 people, no finance hire yet
- What they're doing when they meet a piece: scrolling, often on a Monday or in payroll week (D1 WILL: "Payroll week still makes me nervous.")
- Stage of awareness: aware of the problem (they feel cash stress), not aware a weekly cash view is the fix. So pieces open with the desire or the moment, then the POV or the part of B1 that answers it.

## Formats
| Format | Share | What it carries (layer) | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Framework post: one part of B1 at a time (the three inputs, the Monday ritual, what to do when a week goes red, build it by hand first) | 40% | Branches | A2 spends most pieces on the body of work; B1 has four parts and each is a post the reader can act on |
| Opinion post: one POV and what it rejects, P1 to P5 | 30% | POVs | The reader believes the accountant has it covered; P1, P2 and P5 name that belief and reject it |
| Story post: S1, S2, S3, anonymised | 15% | Values and behaviors | Builds trust; each story carries a refusal (V1) or a hard thing said early (V2) |
| Proof post: R1, R3 as aggregates, with the method behind the number | 10% | Proof and vehicle | Proven position means results can lead from the start; aggregated only (X5) |
| Origin piece: T and its catalyst S1, pinned on the profile | 5% (one piece, then kept pinned and reposted when it ages) | Thesis and catalyst | Every post reaches a cold reader; the profile is the landing page |
| Targeted piece for one named founder or connector (written to their situation, no name) | counted inside the above, one every second week | Any layer | Branch A: hyper-personalised pieces for named targets carry most of the results |

No video (she won't do it). Carousels or visuals only where a part of B1 is a diagram; the one-page view itself is a natural visual.

## Cadence
- Pieces per week: 2, from the branch A2 default of 2 to 3, trimmed to her four hours a week of content time, and because the newsletter will take part of that time from month two
- Batching: Friday mornings (`vehicle.md`). Both pieces drafted and judged on Friday, posted on two different weekdays (a Monday fits the cash-call theme; the second is her choice)
- Raise to: 3 a week when two a week has held for a full quarter and the newsletter has its rhythm, and only if the Friday block still has room

## Mix this quarter
- Reach versus relationships: 70/30. Seventy percent of pieces are for the whole segment (reach inside the pond, never outside it); thirty percent are targeted pieces for named founders and connectors. Connection work and DMs below are relationships on top of that.
- Layer shares: Branches 40, POVs 30, Values stories 15, Proof 10, Thesis 5 (see `content-plan.md`)
- Off the table here: topic-popular posts (LinkedIn itself, productivity, leadership, workplace), everything on the anti-associations list in `brand-strategy.md` (fundraising X1, tax and compliance X2, generic productivity X3, AI-replaces-finance X4, any named client X5), and the word "runway" (W2: she says "cash months")

## Connections and conversations
- Who to connect with: founder-CEOs of bootstrapped SaaS, 2 to 10M ARR, 8 to 40 people (G1); the connectors who send her clients (bootstrapper community hosts, SaaS lawyers, payments consultants, from `brand-strategy.md` Relationships); peers who write for the same founder. The Dream100 and ICP lists are not in the canon yet; building them is the first task of the quarter and goes in the content plan.
- How many a day: 5, by hand, no automation. The branch default is 10 to 20; trimmed because her stated time is four hours on Fridays and requests need weekday attention. Blank requests or one plain line; no pitch.
- After they engage: a short thank-you DM in her voice (W3: dry, not cheerful), then nothing for two weeks, then one question about their cash months if they engaged twice. Wait at least 60 days before any ask.
- Collaborative invitations: an interview series with bootstrapped founders about how they watch cash (the dual harvest: research that becomes content and relationship). Not before the newsletter exists; proposed for the next quarter.

## Selling here
- Give until: 2027-04-30 at the earliest (six months) before any post names the retained offer O1
- How the vehicle appears: the Monday cash call as a character in the stories; C1 ("Reply with your cash months and I'll tell you what I'd look at first") at the end of a B1 piece once or twice a month; O2, the two-hour session, only in a stated burst with an end date, and only after the newsletter is running; the profile carries what she does and the pinned origin piece
- Never: a post that is an ad; a link in every post; a named client (X5); a post written to reach outside G1

## Signals
- Watch: replies and DMs that use her language ("cash months", "13-week view"); replies to C1 from founders inside G1; introductions and referrals from the connectors; conversations she wanted to have (a call she was glad to be on counts, a call she wasn't does not). Plus: which part of B1 draws the most replies, to feed the next plan.
- Ignore: followers, likes, impressions, reach outside G1
- Review: weekly for pieces; 2027-01-31 for this file
