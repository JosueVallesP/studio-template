## Job
Hold the reader's attention and go deep. Why this channel does this job for Mara: posts can't carry a whole part of the 13-week view (B1) with its inputs and the Monday ritual; a short issue can. The list survives the feed. For a 70/30 A2 plan the body of work needs somewhere to live that she owns, and she asked for it.

## Reader here
- Segment: G1, the same founder, after two or three posts made them subscribe
- What they're doing when they meet a piece: subscribed, reading on purpose; likely a Monday if the issue goes out then
- Stage of awareness: aware of the problem, moving toward aware of the fix once they've read a few issues. Issues can go further into method than posts do.
