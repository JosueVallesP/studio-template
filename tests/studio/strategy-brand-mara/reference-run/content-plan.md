## The reputation gap
Target association: "The cash person for bootstrapped SaaS." No reputation check exists yet, so the gap is the whole distance: 1,900 followers and 11 clients know her; the founders she wants to reach don't yet say "ask Mara about cash" unprompted (`outcomes.md`). This quarter's job is to make the 13-week view the thing her name brings to mind.

- Branch and pace: A, split 70/30 toward A2 (body of work first), from `brand-strategy.md`

The 30 is the targeted piece every second week plus the connection and DM work in the LinkedIn file. One plan, one weighting.

## This quarter's branches
1. B1, the 13-week cash view: it is the only confirmed branch, it answers both confirmed desires (D1, D2), it is the target association itself, and it carries the thesis (T) into something a founder can build on a Monday.
