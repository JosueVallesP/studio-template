## The path to the vehicle
A founder reads two or three posts that name their Monday (payroll nerves, the bank balance as the alarm), opens the profile, and finds the origin piece pinned (T, S1). The sideways move is C1, "Reply with your cash months and I'll tell you what I'd look at first", used at the end of a B1 piece once or twice a month, not in every post. A reply becomes a DM conversation. For some, the next step is the two-hour session O2 (which R3 says most founders finish with their own forecast); for the right ones it is O1. The selling rule: give first, for at least six months (to 2027-04) before any post names the retained offer; sell sideways through the method and the Monday ritual as a character in the stories; any direct window for O2 runs in a burst with a stated end date, and only once the newsletter exists. This also keeps her goal of never running a sales call she didn't want: the reply to C1 tells her who to talk to.

## Open questions
1. Four more clients against 3 to 4 at a time. O1 says she runs 3 to 4 retained clients at once; the goal says four more in twelve months. If "four more" means replacing clients who roll off, the 70/30 toward A2 is right. If it means growing to seven or eight, the pace should move toward A1 (more targeted pieces, more connection work) and the capacity line in O1 should change. Taken: 70/30 toward A2, on her own words about pace. What would change it: her answer.
2. The first gate, read literally. The method asks "has the person been this reader". Mara has been the person this reader hires, 11 times, not the founder. Taken: the gate passes on R1 to R3, because the test is about whether she can speak to the founder's Monday from inside it, and four years of running that Monday does that. What would change it: if she feels she can't write about the founder's side without inventing it; then the content leans harder on what she sees and says (V2) and less on what the founder feels.
3. When the newsletter starts. Taken: second month of the quarter, every two weeks, after about eight LinkedIn pieces exist to point at it. The other answer: wait a full quarter so the four hours a week go entirely into LinkedIn first. What would change it: how the first month's Fridays feel.
4. B2 "Three numbers under 10M" is a draft branch and P6 "Pricing is a cash decision" is a draft POV. Neither feeds content this quarter. If she wants to think out loud about either, a brief can say so. Otherwise they wait for confirmation and the next plan.
5. Connection requests per day. The branch default is 10 to 20 a day. Taken: 5 a day by hand, because her content time is four hours on Friday mornings and connections need weekday attention. What would change it: if she has ten minutes on other days.
6. Is a "bootstrapper community" a place she can post or be interviewed in? The canon names it as a sender but not what it is. If it has a feed or a newsletter, it may deserve a collaborative series (interview a founder about their cash months) and a channel file later.
