# Thesis

## T · Cash tells the truth before revenue does
status: confirmed
claim: In a bootstrapped software company, the cash position three months out tells you what the business really is; revenue is what you hope it is.
quote: "Revenue is the story you tell. Cash is the story that's true."
catalyst: Watching two clients with growing ARR run out of money in the same quarter (S1).
implies: Founders should run the company off a 13-week cash view, not the P&L.
from: V1, V2
log: 2026-09-14 confirmed
