# POVs

## P1 · The P&L is the wrong steering wheel for a bootstrapper
status: confirmed
rank: 1
claim: Bootstrapped founders should run the company off a 13-week cash forecast, not monthly P&L.
rejects: The accountant's monthly close as the main management tool.
evidence: S1, R1
log: confirmed

## P2 · Fractional means fewer hours, not less ownership
status: confirmed
rank: 2
claim: A fractional CFO who only reports is a bookkeeper with a title.
rejects: The "advisory hours" model of fractional finance.
evidence: R2
log: confirmed

## P3 · Most SaaS metrics are vanity below 10M
status: confirmed
rank: 3
claim: Under 10M ARR, three numbers matter: cash months, net revenue retention, and cost to acquire a paying customer. The rest is decoration.
rejects: Metric dashboards copied from venture-backed companies.
evidence: S2
log: confirmed

## P4 · Hiring ahead of cash is how bootstrappers die
status: confirmed
rank: 4
claim: The common failure isn't bad product; it's hiring two quarters before the cash can carry it.
rejects: "Hire ahead of growth" advice.
evidence: S1
log: confirmed

## P5 · Founders should do their own first forecast
status: confirmed
rank: 5
claim: A founder who has built one 13-week forecast by hand never reads a finance report the same way again.
rejects: Outsourcing all finance thinking to the finance person.
evidence: R3
log: confirmed
