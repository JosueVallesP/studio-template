# Podcast transcript (excerpt): "Eng Leadership Weekly", episode 48, Dana Whitfield

Host: So why cohorts? Why not just coach people one on one?

Dana: Honestly, I did one on one for years. It works, but it's slow and it's lonely for them. A new manager thinks they're the only one who doesn't know what they're doing. Put eight of them in a room and in the first session somebody says "I have no idea how to give feedback without it feeling like an attack" and everybody else goes, oh thank God. That's half the value right there.

Host: And the other half?

Dana: The company learns something. When nine managers all hit the same wall in week three, that's not nine personal problems, that's a missing piece in how you promote people. I tell the head of engineering what the walls were. I don't tell them who said what.
