Step 8 says if the person isn't available, mark `blocked`. So a brief with open questions is either `briefed` (step 10) or `blocked` (step 8), and the template implies it isn't ready either way. I went with `blocked` because the test said the person is away, but a producer reading `blocked` will think nothing can happen, when in fact the brief is complete enough to draft as an opinion post. I said so in the brief. The skill should name the middle case: brief complete from the canon, questions would improve it, producer may approve as is. Something like `briefed` plus an "open questions" flag the status command surfaces, or a rule that `blocked` means "the writer cannot draft without a guess" and `briefed` with open questions means "the writer can draft; the answers would make it better."
