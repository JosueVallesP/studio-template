description: What the studio sees in your files that you haven't named (one belief in three coats, a method you haven't written down, a shape, a contradiction worth having, an unmade piece), each with its evidence and one question. Or, with "about X", a thinking partner for one half-formed idea: perspectives, context, pushback, until you know what you think.

Read `skills/job-think/SKILL.md` and follow it exactly. Whatever follows `/think` is the topic to narrow to; nothing means all of it. `/think about X` (or "help me think this through") is the one-idea mode: a session that ends with the person able to say what they think about X in one sentence, with its ground and its condition, or deciding they don't believe it after all.
