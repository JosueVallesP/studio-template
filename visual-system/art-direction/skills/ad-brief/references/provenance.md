## The governing structural finding

**Every compliance mechanism across all seven channels is synchronous and in person.** Tabulated, each is either shipped before the shoot as a reference set, exercised live while the maker works, or a record of what happened. Nothing is an after-delivery conformance check against a written specification.

This follows from the medium rather than from carelessness, and it is the gap the skill exists to fill. The two mechanisms that transfer to an absent maker are the two least represented: a selection review against named rejection grounds, filmed once and only because a documentary happened to be present, and an in-frame inspection list whose four checks are properties of the image rather than of the set.

## Claims refused

- **A taxonomy made unfalsifiable by its own disclaimer.** The role structure is prefaced with the statement that the roles can move around, priorities and hierarchy differ by project, and if you see it differently that is fine. That makes it unarguable as stated, and it also doubles as a sales pitch: the closing section offers to be your creative director, producer and art director if you lack one.
- **"You cannot execute a concept without a creative director."** Asserted by the source selling creative direction.
- **A number of looks given with no reason.** Six to eight, stated in passing with no derivation.
- **"Angles that aren't typically shot at"** as a differentiation method. Not observable, and in tension with the better-reasoned fix-everything position.
- **Location does not matter because the photographs are carried by the lighting.** Asserted, and then contradicted by the same source specifying location modification at length.
- **Casting on comfort.** Choosing friends because it is easier and lower-pressure, from a source who lists the subject as a thing to specify.
- **Outcome guarantees**, the heaviest junk in the block. One source guarantees this will be your best shoot, says he has not had a bad shoot in ages because he does this, and claims that doing it puts you ahead of most photographers at your level. The guarantee is then hedged into unfalsifiability in the same breath, conceding you might personally disagree. **A guarantee that survives its subject disagreeing with it is not a claim.**
- **"The perfect mood board"** and work that will **"instantly change for the better"**. Both unearned by anything in the videos.
- **The presentation-polish layer.** Roughly three minutes on fonts and colour-coding, where the source concedes the information content is identical and argues for the styling anyway via an unspecified psychological effect, then via a claim that it leads to paid work. The legitimate point underneath, that a brief must be legible and sectioned, is real and kept. The styling layer is advice about appearing professional rather than about specifying an image, and for a brief read by a model it is actively wrong.
- **Gatekeeping on the creative-director label**, which is roughly a third of one video. An asserted age distribution for creative directors with no basis; ten years of experience as a threshold, derived from the speaker's own biography with no reason given for ten rather than three or twenty; and an argument by intimidation about being measured against famous performers. **That last is a claim about how he personally judges people, generalised into advice about what to call yourself, and it has no bearing on whether anyone can specify an image.** The accountability argument underneath is sound and is kept; the gatekeeping is not. He also reports doing the combined job he says cannot be done.
- **Credential as evidence.** A client list deployed to support method claims it cannot support. Worth contrasting with the same source's green-light mechanism, which stands entirely on its own logic and needs no client list.
- **A pricing tier presented as method detail**, plus an unrelated insurance ad at the close of a sponsored video.
- **"Great DPs should also be good editors."** Attributed, unargued, and plugging the speaker's own course. The following sentence does carry a mechanism, that you need to understand how shots flow together, so that is kept and the aphorism dropped.
- **"Everybody is photogenic, either you have learned how to look good on camera or you have yet to learn."** A disjunction covering every case, attached to a free posing guide.

**And ten stated standards that are not tests**, listed in the skill so they are not mistaken for them: as close to the soul as possible; if I look at the picture and I feel I know them; a sense of closed intimacy; it's going to be sick; angles that aren't typically shot at; making the space as mine as possible; looking professional; the model representing the brand; posing appropriately the right way; matching the aesthetic as much as possible.

**The pattern underneath those ten is the most useful thing in the corpus and no source states it.** Nearly every unobservable criterion here has a checkable mechanism sitting next to it: closed intimacy is untestable, putting up a black background is testable. So a spec line should name the mechanism rather than the feeling it is meant to produce. That rule is in the skill body, derived from filtering the sources' own criteria for observability rather than from any source's claim.

## Canon-source notes

The documentary carries more procedure than expected, and three items have no counterpart anywhere else.

**Two observable rejection grounds**, from the only filmed selection review: the composition is one already made before, and performance is visible with the pass condition being that nobody is performing. Both are checkable by a second person holding the frame, which is why they are the only canon criteria the skill carries. His other stated criteria, recognition on the viewer's side and proximity to the soul, are reliable-judge tests rather than observable ones and are not carried.

**Three named interventions when a session is not working**, all escalations of intensity rather than changes of plan: a deliberate shock delivered as loud encouragement to break a defensive posture; changing the background mid-sitting to change the emotional register while the subject stays put; and directing the subject's internal state rather than their body.

**A frame register.** He identifies a selected image by its exact contact sheet and frame number. No practical source mentions anything comparable.

**Preparation, contradicting the surface reading.** He has no shot list, board or call sheet and reads as the anti-planning source, but the film contains four unprompted statements that he prepares heavily, including third-party corroboration with a checkable instance, a pre-light with named retouching intent, and a prepared opening gesture chosen in advance that visibly produces the disclosure it was meant to produce. His sequence is also fixed and invariant: long-lens colour first, always, then monochrome, then a dark ground, then light. **That is a shot list; he simply never writes it down because it never changes.**

## Gaps: what no source covers

1. **Any written acceptance criterion.** Universal omission, and the largest gap for this skill.
2. **Aspect ratio.** Zero mentions in eight transcripts, from sources making magazine covers and commercial spots.
3. **Deliverable count, resolution, file format, colour space, delivery mechanism.** Zero mentions.
4. **Usage, licence, or where the image will run** as a written brief field.
5. **Budget as a written constraint.**
6. **Reshoot triggers.** No source names a condition under which work is redone. The only trigger in the corpus is a self-administered withhold.
7. **Image models.** Two mentions of AI in eight transcripts, neither about making pictures: one sponsor read, and the cinematographer rejecting it unprompted in his opening forty seconds. **Zero mentions** of prompting, text-to-image, any named model, style references, seeds or negative prompts. Every source assumes a maker who is a person, in a room, on a clock, who can be asked a question and will answer.

   The second-order consequence matters more than the absence itself: **the sources' weakest area is precisely what an image-model brief depends on most.** No reshoot triggers, no written acceptance criteria, no after-delivery conformance check, all survivable with live supervision, none survivable when regeneration is nearly free and supervision is impossible. The corpus supplies a strong specification inventory and almost no verification discipline. Everything in the skill's image-model section is labelled as transfer, and the four absent delivery parameters are supplied with no source support at all.

   One adaptation deserves separate note because it is structural rather than incidental: **the sources have no concept of a reference having a role.** A photographer reads a reference as an indication; a model reads it as a target. The corpus's three-to-ten loose references per shot type would average into mush, so for a model the count falls and each reference is assigned a role. No source frames references this way.
8. **A selection stage.** The practical sources end at capture. Only the documentary shows a candidate image argued over before it ships, which is remarkable, because selection is where a brief is actually satisfied or not.
9. **Adjacency for still sets.** The still sources never ask whether two images belong together, only whether each was captured. The cinematographer supplies the missing test.

## Moved here from the skill body

Detail that was in the operational file, carried source attribution or corroboration arithmetic, and had no procedural work left to do once the mechanism was written out.

- **Downstream use reasoned about but never written down.** One source reasons at length from where the image will end up: what the music sounds like, which brands will be approached, what advertising it will appear in. He never says to write any of it into a brief. That is the basis for usage being listed as a field a maker at a distance cannot infer rather than as a practice anyone demonstrates.
- **The resolution of a missed shot is political rather than productive.** Where a shot is missed after a green light, the source's account is that the director argues it out inside the client company. No productive remedy is described, which is why the skill substitutes a written acceptance criterion rather than an escalation path.
- **Assembling a target image from a library of existing frames.** The cinematographer builds his target by combining frames he already has. It is the closest thing to prompting anywhere in the material and it is arrived at with no model involved, which is the one piece of evidence that the transfer in the image-model section is not purely invented.
- **The one documented production failure.** Colours that could not be matched in the physical material. It is the only instance of a specified attribute proving unrenderable, and it is the basis for the skill's instruction to pre-commit what you drop when a model cannot render an attribute.
- **Contingency triggers are conditional rather than output-based.** A weather plan B, two or three scouted locations, and five to eight photographers queued so that a non-reply costs nothing. All three fire on a condition before the work exists, none on an inspection of the work, which is why none of them is a reshoot trigger.
- **The read-receipt mechanism.** Send the brief ahead of time and require everyone to confirm receipt, twenty-four to forty-eight hours out, with the stated reason that things change and you need time to replace someone. One channel.

## Known weaknesses

- **The canon tier is a documentary**, so its procedure is what a film crew happened to capture rather than what the subject chose to teach.
- **The best practical source is one channel.** The per-look schema, the location protocol, the call-sheet rules, the in-frame inspection list and the read-receipt requirement are all one voice.
- **The role boundaries rest on two short sources that disagree on structure**, one of which disclaims its own taxonomy and both of which are selling services.
- **The image-model section is argued, not sourced.** It rests on two mechanisms whose transfer is justified by their being frame properties rather than set access, which is a reasoned claim and not a demonstrated one.
- **One source's transcript is unreliable** in ways that affect quotation; figures from it were not carried.
- Not run on live client work.
