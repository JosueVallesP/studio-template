# dsn-image: provenance

Where everything in `SKILL.md` came from, how badly corroborated it is, which claims are one person's method rather than craft consensus, where the two independent voices actually conflict, and what none of them reach.

Read the corroboration section before anything else. This is the weakest-sourced skill in the plugin and the file exists mainly to keep that visible.

## Corroboration summary

Four sources, **two channels**. Three of the four are `orenmeetsworld`. The fourth is Louis Moss, and he covers moodboards and referencing only.

**Nothing in `SKILL.md` is corroborated by two independent teachers.** Not the one-thing rule, not action over composition, not the angle claims, not the brand sandbox, not the bell curve. Every one of those rests on a single person who uploaded three videos.

Total runtime is about 71 minutes, of which about 56 minutes is one person. For comparison, `dsn-poster` carries five sources across four channels and two of them are scholarly lectures. This skill carries nothing of the kind.

**What the three orenmeetsworld videos agree on among themselves.** This is one person being consistent across five months of uploads, October 2024 to March 2025. It is evidence that he believes it and uses it, not evidence that it is true. ## Where the sources disagree, and the decision rule

There are only two independent voices, so there is very little opportunity for disagreement. **Read the shortness of this list as a warning rather than as reassurance.** Three videos by one person cannot disagree with each other in any way that tells you anything, and the two real conflicts below both turn out to be the two independent voices contradicting each other directly, which is what genuine disagreement looks like in a file this thin.

**1. Is collecting what you like a legitimate way to build a reference library?**
Oren's method is accumulation. He tells viewers to build boards continuously and, "instead of Doom scrolling whenever you do it on whatever go and like spend time on your mood board apps ... just add things to those boards you like" [`Ku9bcxICrJQ` 00:17:43], so that references are on hand when a shoot appears. Moss names exactly this as the failure: "Most people reference by attraction. They see something they like and then they add it to their board. But that's not really referencing. That's just collecting" [`xJEYViuQifg` 00:10:22].
**Rule: Moss, but only for the board that drives a piece.** The two are describing different objects. Oren's accumulation produces a personal library, where unjustified saving costs nothing and breadth is the point. Moss's standard applies to a board that is about to constrain a project, where an uncaptioned image is a direction nobody agreed to. Keep the library loose, apply the caption test at the moment a board starts directing work. `SKILL.md` lists reference-by-attraction as an anti-pattern without noting this conflict, which is correct for the directing board and overstated for the library.

**2. Where should references come from?**
Oren is indifferent to the source: Cosmos, Are.na, "Pinterest really whatever it is you prefer" [`Ku9bcxICrJQ` 00:04:22], and he works from Pinterest by name [00:11:43]. Moss argues the opposite, that Instagram and Pinterest are algorithmic, "if I'm searching something, you're searching something, we're probably going to get the same thing", and that Are.na's boards are built by people being deliberate so "the standard to me feels a lot higher" [`xJEYViuQifg` 00:12:05 to 00:13:10].
**Rule: Moss on the risk, nobody on the remedy.** Neither demonstrates anything. Moss's claim is asserted, and he is a working art director whose value rests partly on curation being hard, though he does not say so and the professional-interest reading in `SKILL.md` is an inference rather than anything in the transcript. What survives is narrow and matches what `dsn-composition` found about starting points: a board assembled from one week of one feed will produce category-shaped work by construction. Where you look is a real variable. Which platform solves it is unsettled.

**3. How much volume is too much?**
Moss caps references hard [`xJEYViuQifg` 00:03:18]. Oren presents 20 or 30 concepts in a brainstorm [`oIzPT05pvY8` 00:09:24]. **Not a real disagreement.** Oren is counting concepts in a session meant to be narrowed by other people, Moss is counting references on a board meant to constrain. Different objects.

## Claims in SKILL.md that do not trace to a transcript

The most useful section in this file. Each of these is either unsourced, overstated against what the source actually says, or labelled with a source that does not carry it. None are wrong on their face. All of them are ours rather than theirs.

1. **"Four words naming one quality"**, twice labelled **(Moss)** in principle 4 and in the caption test. Moss asks for specificity, not brevity. Keep the discipline, drop the attribution or relabel it derived.

2. **"Under fifteen" and "over fifteen"** as board thresholds. No source. Covered above.

3. **"All four sources treat the image as the whole piece and never as one element in a layout"**, the stated ground for principle 3. **Overstated.** It holds for the three orenmeetsworld videos. 6. **"Every judgment in all four videos is 'does this look good'"**, in the agent-capability section. **Very nearly true and worth keeping**, with one exception on the record: `CNYN0wFkzMo` 00:06:22 cites a view count. 10. **The whole of "Type over a photograph", the Mode B review order, step 7 of Mode A, and the poor-photo ladder.** All derived, and `SKILL.md` says so for the ladder and the type section. ## What the legibility pass removed from SKILL.md

`SKILL.md` was rewritten to the skill standard. Bracketed source IDs, source names, the corroboration arithmetic and the three-label scheme were all moved here. Nothing was deleted.

**The old label scheme and its replacement.** `SKILL.md` carried three inline labels: **(one practitioner)** for orenmeetsworld-only material, **(Moss)** for the single independent voice, and **(derived)** for reasoning. All three collapse into the standard's single **(thin)** marker, plus a plain-language `## Confidence` section stating that this is the plugin's weakest independence, that most of the file is one practitioner's method, and that nothing is corroborated by two independent teachers. The mapping is: every former **(one practitioner)** and **(Moss)** rule is now **(thin)**; former **(derived)** material is either shown as arithmetic in place, or marked **(thin)** where it was an assertion with no mechanism.

**The fourteen audited defects, and what happened to each.**

1. Four-word caption limit, mislabelled **(Moss)**. Removed. The caption test now asks for a specific named quality as a noun phrase, with worked examples, and sets no word count.
2. "Under fifteen" and "over fifteen" board thresholds. Removed from both the anti-patterns and the volume test. The volume test now fails on the gap between reference count and captioned count, and states explicitly that no reference count threshold is given because none is supported.
3. "All four sources treat the image as the whole piece", the justification for principle 3. Removed; false of Moss. Principle 3 now derives from canvas area instead: a full-bleed image on 1080 x 1350 covers 1,458,000 pixels against at most 348,192 for a type block, so an unassigned image defaults to level one by area.
4. "He has a professional interest in it", on the algorithm claim. Removed. Inference, and source attribution.
5. "Human-curated boards hold a higher standard", attributed. Removed. Replaced with the narrow residue: where you look is a real variable, which platform fixes it is unsettled, and no claim that one source is higher quality should be trusted.
6. "Every judgment in all four videos is 'does this look good'". Removed. The Confidence section now states plainly that nothing in the file rests on any measurement of an image against an outcome.
7. "Nothing on photographing anything other than people and apparel". Corrected. The gap list now reads food, interiors, architecture, documentary and still life outside commerce, and acknowledges product-on-white coverage.
8. "Monochrome removes the two things that date an amateur photo fastest". The frequency claim is gone. The mechanism is now stated as what it actually is: greyscale discards the colour channels, so there is no hue left to be wrong, and this removes two failure modes and nothing else.
9. "Unnameable is the commonest failure" and "winning on 5 and losing on 1 is the commonest wrong choice". The first is gone. The second is retained with the frequency explicitly labelled an impression and the ranking labelled the instruction.
10. Type over a photograph, Mode B order, Mode A step 7, the poor-photo ladder: all now carry their derived status at the point of use, and the Mode B order carries its reasoning (cheap decisive checks before expensive arguable ones).
11. Partial reveal. Retained as a concept, marked **(thin)** alongside scale ambiguity.
12. Ten-second read. Retained, marked **(thin)**, and stated as a short fixed interval chosen for this skill with no measurement behind it.
