# Audit: dsn-composition

Independent check of `SKILL.md` against the transcripts in
`~/research/design-plugin/transcripts/` only. The source set used: the five
`composition__` files (`PKfZ1gnVJ44`, `yUvGHNzqG7M`, `GT-K0CrrgfU`,
`r6LPNRVhGKA`, `aQI95mVeDXw`) and the five `principles__` files
(`FpwZTl3dCZE`, `I4C-Smqsf7Y`, `HmgzcaH-uv8`, `WLI5ckPofrk`, `9EPTM91TBDU`).

`references/provenance.md` was not used as evidence. It was written by the same
agent that wrote the skill, so it is self-attestation.

Counts: 8 mislabelled, 6 unsupported, 4 derived but unlabelled, 1 citation
pointing at the wrong place. Fixes applied inline where the correction is
grounded in a transcript; everything left alone is marked "reported only".

## Mislabelled attributions

The skill reserves "all three channels" for its only agreement with evidential
weight. Four of the eight findings below are that phrase used where the three
channels do not agree.

### 1. "All three channels require exactly one primary" (principle 1)

Only one video states it. Satori: "one of the most effective ways to provide a
sense of balance right out of the gates is to choose a single focal point for
your design" [yUvGHNzqG7M 00:01:55].

Satori's deepest guide states the opposite: "a design can have multiple
different leverage points depending on the brief" [PKfZ1gnVJ44 00:21:35], and
on the Nike layout it names a second one, "the neon green logo, which acts as
the leverage point of the smaller proximity group in the lower left"
[PKfZ1gnVJ44 00:20:48].

Kittl does not require one either. On a film poster he ranks five: "this is
first this is second this is third this is fourth maybe this right here is
fifth" [aQI95mVeDXw 00:12:38].

Envato defines a focal point as "the main subject or main element we want the
viewer to look at first" [r6LPNRVhGKA 00:01:40] and never forbids a second.

**Fixed:** attribution corrected, the counter-statement added, the rule kept
as the skill's own choice.

### 2. "All three channels name it" on asymmetric weight (Path, balance and friction)

Two channels name it, not three.

- Envato: without the small dark hill "you would almost feel like the artwork
  was falling or shifting to the left side" [r6LPNRVhGKA 00:10:22]. Envato's
  other guide repeats it: a lack of balance "will make the poster feel like
  it's falling toward one side" [9EPTM91TBDU 00:02:22].
- Kittl: "a lack of balance can make a design feel like one side is too heavy"
  [aQI95mVeDXw 00:05:17].
- Satori names asymmetrical balance only as a technique that already works:
  "the designer has used asymmetrical balance by having one large image and
  then three smaller images" [I4C-Smqsf7Y 00:00:45]. No composition-set Satori
  video names the falling failure or an anchor requirement.

**Fixed.**

### 3. "All three channels teach it" on gaze and limb direction (Path cues, item 3)

Two channels, not three.

- Satori: "the athlete's gaze, or maybe a part of their body, points towards
  the logo" [PKfZ1gnVJ44 00:00:41]; "fingers pointing or fists punching or eyes
  looking in One Direction" [yUvGHNzqG7M 00:16:05].
- Envato: of Botticelli's Venus, "the other figures around her are looking at
  her so this is also a great example of imaginary leading lines"
  [r6LPNRVhGKA 00:02:30].
- Kittl's movement section teaches curved elements, curved type and rays, and
  a figure breaking out of a circle [aQI95mVeDXw 00:25:03]. A search of that
  transcript returns no gaze, eyeline, pointing limb or line of sight anywhere.

**Fixed.**

### 4. "All three channels say three, in different vocabulary" (principle 2)

Two do.

- Satori: "the hook, the secondary detail, and the finisher"
  [GT-K0CrrgfU 00:03:03].
- Envato: focal point, then "the rest of the elements sort of come secondary"
  [r6LPNRVhGKA 00:06:58], then "this is just extra information" [00:07:50].
- Kittl counts to five and does not stop at three [aQI95mVeDXw 00:12:38].

**Fixed.**

### 5. "No more than two cue types on one path ... Satori's own warning about its layered-flow method"

Satori's warning is about the number of competing paths, and it gives no
number at all: "Some designers might use too many competing paths and the
design might end up feeling chaotic" [PKfZ1gnVJ44 00:03:52]. The cap of two
cue types per path is not in the source and is attributed to it as stated.

**Fixed:** Satori's actual warning kept and quoted, the cap relabelled as
derived.

### 6. "the usable part of Kittl's 60/30/10 split ... its only worked example cannot satisfy them"

Kittl works two examples. The first is presented as satisfying the split:
dark purple at sixty, neon blue at "about 30", neon pink "making up about 10"
[aQI95mVeDXw 00:30:39]. Only the second concedes a miss: "our 10 which maybe
debatably is a little bit more than 10 percent" [aQI95mVeDXw 00:32:03]. That
second passage also labels two different colours "60", which is caption damage
rather than a third worked case.

**Fixed:** "only worked example" corrected to the second of two, with the
quote.

### 7. "A tall narrow frame supports only a vertical journey; a square supports horizontal or radial" (principle 4)

The dependency itself is at the cited timestamp: "a lot of the movement and
flow on a graphic design should initially be dictated by the kind of dimensions
of the document you're working on" [I4C-Smqsf7Y 00:13:06]. The mapping the
skill builds on it is not what that source shows.

- The tall narrow document in that source produces a diagonal, not a vertical:
  "the flow or the movement here is of a slight diagonal from top right to
  bottom left and that's mainly because of the shape of the document itself
  notice how narrow and Tall it actually is" [I4C-Smqsf7Y 00:12:23].
- A vertical journey for a narrow page is stated, but in a different video:
  "A4 is quite narrow lastly ... I'm thinking of a downward Journey from top to
  bottom" [yUvGHNzqG7M 00:07:03].
- The square example yields horizontal only [I4C-Smqsf7Y 00:13:06]. Radial
  balance appears at [I4C-Smqsf7Y 00:13:51] on an unrelated design and is
  never tied to a square frame.

**Fixed:** "only" dropped, the vertical claim given its real citation, the
diagonal counter-case noted, radial marked as not tied to a square by any
source.

### 8. "asserted by both sources that teach it and derived by neither" on the rule of thirds

Envato asserts the reason: "the point of intersection on each of these lines is
where the viewer's eyes will go to naturally first" [r6LPNRVhGKA 00:14:02].

Satori teaches thirds and never states that reason. All it says is that the
method "helps you position key elements in a way that feels natural and
engaging" [PKfZ1gnVJ44 00:17:47]. It mentions no intersections and no eye
behaviour. So the eyes-land claim is one source asserting, not two.

**Fixed.**

## Unsupported

### 10. "Every pixel value below is derived from it" (Applied to a post visual or carousel)

False in two directions. `reference/canvas-assumptions.md` gives canvas sizes
of 1080 x 1080 and 1080 x 1350 and the 3x and 4x factors, and nothing else
numeric. The counts that follow in the skill are not in it: two levels rather
than three, four to nine words, six columns, L1 spanning four, six archetypes,
three to five list items.

Worse, the one raw pixel figure below that line is "a piece built at 1200px and
hoped" (The ultimate test). 1200 is in neither `canvas-assumptions.md`, which
gives 1080, nor any transcript.

This is the same defect found in `dsn-image`: a pixel value presented as coming
from the shared file that is in neither the file nor the sources.

**Fixed:** the claim corrected to say what actually comes from the shared file,
the rest labelled as constructions, and 1200px marked illustrative.

### 11. "Commonest failure in client content" (anti-pattern: shouting match)

A frequency claim. No source in the set counts failures or ranks them by
frequency. **Fixed:** relabelled as an observation.

### 12. "The budget is one irregularity total across grid break, pattern break and style collision" (principle 5)

Satori's grid-break passage gives no count and no cross-category budget. It
says only to make "a few boxes of typography and some imagery that are falling
in line with the grid columns and rows, but then have a design element that
totally breaks the rule" [PKfZ1gnVJ44 00:10:04]. The accompanying claim that
"two breaks read as absence of a grid" is also unsourced.

**Fixed:** marked as constructions, Satori's actual statement quoted.

### 13. The applied-section counts

"It supports three levels where a square supports two", "four to nine words",
"six columns ... L1 spanning four", "six named archetypes", "three to five
items, because the ladder cannot distinguish six". None of these is in any
transcript or in `canvas-assumptions.md`. The three-to-five figure has partial
cover from Envato's rule of odds, "it is preferred to have an odd number like
three in up to five" [r6LPNRVhGKA 00:14:57], but Envato's stated reason there
is avoiding clutter, not a hierarchy ladder.

**Fixed** by the single label added at the head of that section, rather than by
marking each number, so the procedures are untouched.

### 14. "Four levels in a feed image collapse into two" (principle 2)

No source states a collapse count or anything about feed rendering. The
sources are silent on platform rendering. **Fixed:** marked derived.

### 15. "This is where carousel templates fail" (longest-real-content test)

Frequency claim, no source. **Fixed:** relabelled as an observation.

## Derived but not labelled as derived

### 16. "Odd groups rank themselves; even groups do not"

The tie-the-eye-cannot-break mechanism is the skill's reading. Envato gives
only the clutter reason [r6LPNRVhGKA 00:14:57], and the skill is right that it
does not survive the examples, since both place the middle subject dead centre
(the third of five females "placed directly right at the center" [00:14:57],
the second of three magnolias "at the very center" [00:15:47]). But the
replacement mechanism is presented as fact.

**Fixed:** labelled derived.

### 17. "Blob count" test

No composition-set source states it. The nearest thing is Satori's squint test
for grouping, and that is in the hierarchy source set
[hierarchy__5yjYe9aDSBM 00:13:20], not in any of these ten files.
**Fixed:** labelled derived.

### 18. "Flip test"

Mirroring the frame is in no source in the set. **Fixed:** labelled derived.

### 19. "inform, sell, shock or be remembered" (Mode A step 2)

Satori's purpose question names informing and selling [yUvGHNzqG7M 00:05:49]
and demonstrates shock [00:06:23]. "Be remembered" is not in that list.
Reported only: it is one word inside a sourced sentence and removing it would
change the procedure.
