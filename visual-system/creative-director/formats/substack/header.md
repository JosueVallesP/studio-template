# Substack header image

**For:** the image at the top of a post. It sets the tone and shows as the preview when the post is shared, so it does two jobs: frame the essay for someone already reading, and earn the click from someone who isn't.

**Size:** confirm against Substack's current guidance and record it here with the date. It must also survive the crop used for social previews.

## Parts

1. **One image or one idea,** not a summary of the essay.
2. **Text:** optional. If present, the essay's title or its core claim, in the brand's headline style.

## Read speed

At a glance in a social preview; the reader decides whether to open in a second or two.

## What generic looks like here

A stock photo matched to a keyword in the title; a model-made surreal "concept" image that needs the essay to explain it.

## Open questions

- Current recommended size and the preview crop.
- How it renders in email clients and in dark mode.
