# LinkedIn framework card

**For:** one idea, drawn so it makes complete sense in the feed with no post around it. The post and the card each stand on their own.

**Size:** 1080 × 1350 pixels (4:5 portrait), the tallest image the feed shows without cropping. Confirm against LinkedIn's current guidance. A brand may set its own size in `direction.md` (the Engraved Swirl brand uses 1500 × 1800, which is 5:6); in keep mode the brand's size wins, and the review notes the difference.

## Parts

1. **Headline:** states the idea in a sentence a reader could repeat. Large enough to read at feed size.
2. **The visual:** the shape the idea needs (see `taste/rules/diagram-form.md`), taking most of the canvas.
3. **Labels:** only where the shape can't carry the meaning, in the reader's words.
4. **Name or mark:** small, in a fixed position the brand sets, so the card is credited when it's shared without the post.

## Read speed

The headline and the shape should land in about two seconds, at a glance while scrolling. The labels reward a second look.

## What generic looks like here

A familiar template shape with interchangeable labels; a headline that teases; a palette that matches everything else in the feed. See `taste/rules/generic-tells.md`.
