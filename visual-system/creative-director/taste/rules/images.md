# Images

**The rule: an image starts from something of the person's.** A story they told, the piece the image goes with, a photograph they took, or a new photograph they send. Their own photographs come first.

## Why it matters

An image that starts from nothing of theirs could belong to anyone. It also puts a place or a moment in front of readers that the person never lived.

## What it requires

- An image from an image model is allowed only when it starts from one of the four sources above.
- A brand needs two kinds of photograph: the person's own, and brand photographs that show the brand's world with the person not in them. When the person has no such photograph, a brand photograph may be generated from the scene in one of their own. Its line in `sources.md` names that photograph.
- A generated place is never given a real place name.
- Every photograph and every generated image has one line in the brand's `photos/sources.md`: the file, how it was made, and what of theirs it starts from.
- A stand-in used to show a treatment is recorded as a stand-in and never published.
- Every image shows craft and taste. None looks like a stock picture or an image model's default style.
- A caption never names a place or a date the person has not confirmed.

## How to check

Open `photos/sources.md` and find the line for each image in the piece. An image with no line, or with a line that names no source of theirs, fails.
