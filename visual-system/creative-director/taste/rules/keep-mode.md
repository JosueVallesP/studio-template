# Keep mode

**The rule: in keep mode, the existing brand is the authority, and the agent's job is to protect it.**

- Use only the tokens in `system/tokens.json`. No new color, font, weight or spacing value, even one that "fits."
- Match canon. When a piece has a close precedent in `canon/`, follow it.
- When the brand doesn't cover a case (a format it has never used, a new kind of image), pick the most conservative option the existing system allows, mark it in the review as "not covered by the brand," and ask the approver before it ships.
- Never "improve" the brand. Note ideas for improvement in the brand's `context.md` under "Waiting on a person," and leave the work as the brand defines it.
- Every change to `direction.md`, `system/` or the locked list needs approval, however small.
