# The durable bar

Adapted from the compound engineering plugin's `ce-compound`.

A lesson earns its place only when it holds judgment that isn't easy to recover from the brand files, the rules or the work itself, and losing it would plausibly cause the same mistake, a real risk, or a lot of rediscovery.

**The counterfactual:** if this lesson disappeared, would someone working on the next brand, reading only the rules and the brand folder, be likely to repeat the mistake or redo the work? If not, it fails.

## Passes

- A pattern seen more than once, with log lines as evidence.
- A person overruling the rules for a reason that would apply again.
- A tool behavior that cost a round of work and isn't in its `hands/` notes.

## Fails

- One rejection with no reason given.
- Something a rule already says. (Then the problem is that the rule didn't fire. Note which step should have loaded it.)
- A preference specific to one brand. That belongs in that brand's `direction.md` as a proposed change, not in learnings.
- Effort or size. A long, hard piece doesn't make a lesson by itself.

## Evidence

Every lesson cites the log lines or review findings it came from. Never write a reason nobody gave.
