# Showing directions

Read this at Step 4 in new and rebrand modes, before you ask for approval. A person cannot choose a look from a paragraph. When this step is skipped, the choice takes many rounds, and each round rebuilds work the first round should have shown.

## What gets shown

**Three directions.** Each one is built on a different contradiction that passed every test in Step 3. If only two pass, show two. Never pad the set with a direction that failed a test.

**The same content in every direction.** Pick one line of theirs, one framework of theirs and one small story or figure of theirs, and use those three in every direction. The person then compares looks and not words. Every word comes from `brief.md`, `references.md` or the person's own material. Never write a line for them.

**Eight boards per direction, in this order.** One row per direction on one canvas.

| Board | What it shows | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mood board | The art, a photograph, the colours, the type, the mark and three plain words for the feel | 1600 x 1350 |
| On a wall | The art framed in a room. If it would not hang well, the direction is not art yet | 1600 x 1350 |
| Art print | The art alone, with a title | 1080 x 1350 |
| Post | The art with their one line | 1080 x 1350 |
| Framework | Their framework drawn in this direction's own shapes | 1080 x 1350 |
| Illustration | Their story or figure as one picture | 1080 x 1350 |
| Photograph | How a photograph is treated | 1080 x 1350 |
| The feed as a whole | Nine tiles together | 1080 x 1350 |

## How to build them

1. **One piece of art per direction, with very few things in it.** Decide the art first. The post, the print, the wall and the feed all come from it.
2. **Put the kept things in every direction.** Read `equities.md` and the brief's "what stays" before drawing. A mark or symbol the person calls personal appears in all three.
3. **Ask what a kept symbol means, once, and say they may decline.** The meaning often decides the direction. If the answer arrives after the first round, run the mark round below.
4. **Use their own photographs, and brand photographs without them in the frame.** Follow `<plugin>/taste/rules/images.md`. A stand-in is labelled as a stand-in in your message and never sent as a real photo of theirs.
5. **Draw with code that can be kept.** Write the art as functions in a script, starting from `<plugin>/kit/vis.py`. `/cd-system` turns the chosen direction's script into the package. Art drawn by hand in a canvas cannot be repeated by the next agent.
6. **Look at every board before you show it.** Render each one and check for overlaps, clipped text and labels on top of the art. Say plainly what you did not check.
7. **No rationale on a board.** The reasons go in your message: each direction's contradiction in one sentence, its weak spot, and which one you recommend and why.

## The mark round

Run this when the person explains a kept symbol, or says the mark matters more than the first round showed.

For each direction the person liked, add one more row where the mark is at the centre of the idea and not a sign-off in a corner. Keep the same eight boards and the same content. Keep the first rows on the canvas so the person can compare.

## After the person replies

- Log their reply in `log.md` in their words.
- Remove a direction only when they reject it. A direction they liked but did not choose stays on the canvas as the record.
- Do not add new directions unless they ask for one.
- The chosen direction goes into `direction.md`. If they are still choosing between two or three, `/cd-system` can package each one so they can judge real pieces.

## A canvas other people can open

Before you change a canvas that is shared by link, ask the studio owner. A change shows at once to everyone who has the link.
