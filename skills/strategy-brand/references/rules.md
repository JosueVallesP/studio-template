# Small rules

These keep two runs on the same canon landing in the same place. None of them is about one person.

## Trimming cadence to capacity

Costs, until the person tells you theirs:

- One feed post, from brief to judged draft, with the person's review: about one hour of the person's time.
- One targeted piece for a named person (research plus the piece): about two hours.
- Connection requests by hand, with the thank-you DMs they produce: about ten minutes a day for 10 to 20 requests; five minutes for 5 to 10.
- A newsletter issue built from that fortnight's posts: about two hours.

Start from the route default, cost it against the hours the person gave (`strategy/outcomes.md`, Capacity; or `vehicle/vehicle.md` constraints if the interview recorded it there), and cut in this order until it fits: the newsletter's frequency, then connection requests per day, then ordinary posts, then targeted pieces last. Write the arithmetic in one line in the channel file so the person can correct the costs.

A person who writes on one day a week can still send connection requests on other days; ask in step 8 rather than assume either way.

## Gate 1 in one line

Pass when the canon shows the person has lived the reader's situation, or has fixed the reader's problem for people like the reader enough times to see a pattern. Fail when neither. Cite the IDs.

## Gate 2 in one line

Pass only on the `reads` field of the segment in `audience.md`. Empty field means ask, and mark the channel provisional.

## Enemy tie-break

The highest-ranked confirmed POV whose `rejects` line the target association depends on. A second POV only when it's the same enemy from another side.

## One confirmed branch

Is enough for a quarter. Name a draft branch as a hypothesis the quarter tests, marked as such, or leave the second slot out.

## Anti-associations without a stop list entry

Write them with "no X ID yet" and put the stop list entry in `proposals.md`. The person confirms it there.

## The quarter

Thirteen weeks from the plan's start date. Judge on the last day of it.

## Permission

Carry the `permission` field of every story and proof entry the plan uses into the channel file's mix section (public, anonymize or private), so the format skills never have to look it up.

## Where the reader reads

The primary channel is where the reader is. Where the person already has an audience breaks a tie only. When they differ, say so in the channel file and let the person decide.
