# The thesis session

One session, about 20 to 30 minutes. It ends with the thesis handed back for a yes. Read `../SKILL.md` ("The method" and "The shape of every session") first; this file holds only what's particular to the thesis.

## What a thesis is

- **Is:** the one claim the whole body of work exists to prove. It's where the person layer (values, POVs, philosophy) meets the goal (what they need to be known for).
- **Isn't:** a tagline, or a mission. A mission says what they do ("I help founders build their brands"). A thesis says what they claim is true ("Founders lose more deals to being forgettable than to being expensive").
- **The evidence it leaves:** every POV supports it or branches from it, and proving it would take years of work.

So this session doesn't ask for the thesis either. The studio drafts it from the layers below and the goal; the person pushes back until it holds.

## It needs the philosophy and the goal

Don't draft a thesis without a confirmed philosophy and a confirmed goal (`canon/vehicle/vehicle.md`). Without the philosophy, a thesis is a guess about what the person believes; without the goal, it may be true and still useless for what they want. If either is missing, say which and suggest that session instead.

When a person arrives with a clear big idea already, write it into `canon/person/thesis.md` as the first draft (status draft), and test each POV against it as it lands. The thesis session itself still waits for the philosophy and the goal.

## Drafting

Draft two or three versions. For each, show where it came from:

- the philosophy it rests on,
- the POVs it holds together (by ID),
- the goal it serves (what they need to be known for).

Make the versions differ in a way that means something (narrower or broader, a different audience, a different claim), not in wording only. Then ask which is closest and what's wrong with it.

## The questions

The thesis itself isn't asked for. These fill the fields around it, and the first two ask about events.

| Field | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| catalyst | "When did you first see this? What happened?" Then: "What made you decide to do something about it?" The answer is a story; file it. |
| falsifier | "What would you have to see to stop believing this?" |
| for | "Who most needs to hear this?" Read it against the segments in `canon/strategy/brand-strategy.md`. |
| changes | "What changes for someone once they believe it?" |
| implies | "What should they do differently?" |
| branches | "Which of your methods or frameworks back this up?" May be partial at confirmation. |

## Pushback

| The answer | The pushback |
| --- | --- |
| A description of their job | "That's what you do. What do you claim is true that makes it worth doing?" |
| A tagline | "Could someone disagree with it? Who?" |
| Too broad to prove | "What would it take to prove this? If the answer is nothing in particular, it's too broad." |
| No catalyst ("I've always believed this") | "When did you first notice it? What were you working on?" |

## The handback

> **Your thesis**
> Founders lose more deals to being forgettable than to being expensive.
> It rests on your philosophy that trust comes from what you're willing to give up, it holds P1, P3 and P5 together, and it's what your goal needs you known for: the person founders call before a big sale.
> The moment you saw it: the 2023 deal you lost to a pricier firm the client "just remembered".
>
> Would you put your name on this on your homepage today? Tell me what's off if not.

## The gate

The thesis is confirmed when it traces to the confirmed philosophy and the confirmed goal, it has a catalyst backed by a story, and the person says they'd put their name on it. `claim`, `for` and `catalyst` must be in their words or a sharper version they accepted. The layer is done when the fuller test in `../references/tests.md` passes (falsifier, at least two branches).

## What it makes possible

The body of work, the strategy and the manifesto. Once the thesis and the goal are both confirmed, run the two-directions test in `../references/tests.md`.
