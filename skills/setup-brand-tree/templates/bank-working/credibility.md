# Credibility bank

What makes a reader trust this person on what they say. The idea bank holds what they think; this bank holds what stands behind it. Capture, `/shape`, `/post` and the interviews fill both at once: a story about a client win can be an idea in one bank and a result in the other, each pointing at the other. Numbers stay exactly as given: never rounded, never filled in. Nothing about another person goes `public` until the person says so.

Kinds:
- `result`: an outcome with a number, or a plain before and after (revenue, clients, saves, time, years kept).
- `client`: who they've done the work for, and what the work was.
- `said-about`: what a client, peer or reader said about them or their work, word for word.
- `before-after`: a specific state before their work and after it, both sides concrete.
- `track-record`: years, roles, companies, how many people or projects, done the same way for a long time.
- `recognition`: talks, press, books, awards, invitations, being quoted or hired by a known name.
- `artifact`: a real piece of their work they could show (a memo, a deck, a test, a framework sheet, a screenshot).
- `vouched`: a known person or company who would stand behind them, or a relationship that opens doors.
