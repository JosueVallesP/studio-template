# calibration 1A: the studio's draft and the person's version

## The studio wrote

When I go through a call transcript looking for post ideas, what I'm doing is a lot like clipping a long video interview into shorts.
You're not looking for the best moment in the call. You're looking for a moment that still makes sense when you cut it out and hand it to someone who wasn't there.
Most of what we say in a long conversation leans on something said ten minutes earlier. Read it cold and it means very little, the same way a random verse from the Bible means very little to someone who doesn't know the passage around it.
So before I write anything, I ask a few questions about the idea. Would a stranger get it with no context? Does it help someone outside that one situation? Is it more than common sense? Can they do something with it?
If any answer is no, it's not a post yet. It might still be a good idea, but it needs more work before it can travel on its own.

## The person wrote

When I go through a call transcript looking for content ideas (whether it's for myself or for clients), what I do looks a lot like clipping long-form videos into shorts.
I'm not really looking for "the best moment" or "the most insightful" idea in the call. I'm just looking for ideas that would still make sense on their own, without any other context.
Specifically, I ask myself:
Would a stranger get it with no context?
Does it help someone outside that one situation?
Is it more than common sense?
Can they do something with it?
If the answer is no, the idea still needs some work.
It might be a good idea, but it's just not ready yet.

## What changed

- "hand it to someone who wasn't there", the Bible paragraph and "travel on its own" cut: images where the point was already clear · rule: H1
- "You're not looking for" → "I'm not really looking for": the speaker stopped sliding into a generic "you" · rule: H13
- the four test questions set one per line, no bullets · rule: W9
- "it's not a post yet" → "the idea still needs some work. It might be a good idea, but it's just not ready yet.": softer, with the stance words a talker uses · rule: W3
