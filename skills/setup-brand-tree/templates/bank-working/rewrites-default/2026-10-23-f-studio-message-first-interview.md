# calibration 2F: the studio's draft and the person's version

## The studio wrote

Next up is your first interview. It takes about 20 minutes, and you can stop whenever you want.
Here's what we'll do:
1. I'll ask you about a few moments from your work, like a decision that cost you something or a project that went better than you expected.
2. From your answers, I'll pull out what they say about how you work and what you believe, and show it back to you in plain words.
3. You tell me if it's right. Only what you confirm goes into your content.
You'll leave with one to three things written down in your own words. Ready to start?

## The person wrote

Let's start with your first interview.
The goal of this interview is to ____. It takes about 20 minutes and you can stop whenever you want, but keep in mind that ___.
Here's how it works:
1. I'll ask you about a few moments about your work to understand ___
2. From your answers, we'll find what those moments say about the way you work, what you believe, and how that will shape ____
3. After all that, you can tell me if I misunderstood something or if you'd like to elaborate on____
This process will be a bit tedious, honestly, but the quality of your answers will determine the quality of the studio we're building here.
You'll leave with a core set of ___ that will define ___.
Let me know when you're ready.

## What changed

- "The goal of this interview is to ___" added, and a caveat ("but keep in mind that ___"): the why before the step · rule: S7
- each step got its purpose ("to understand ___", "how that will shape ___") · rule: S7
- "You tell me if it's right" → "you can tell me if I misunderstood something or if you'd like to elaborate": confirmation as an invitation to correct or add · rule: S10
- "This process will be a bit tedious, honestly, but the quality of your answers will determine the quality of the studio" added: honest about effort and why it's worth it · rule: S7
- "Ready to start?" → "Let me know when you're ready.": the plain handoff · rule: W10
