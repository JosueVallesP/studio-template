# calibration 3H: the studio's draft and the person's version

## The studio wrote

Card: A piece of content is great when it's: 1. Relevant to you 2. Part of what you're building toward 3. Easy to follow, with nothing left to guess 4. Useful: it helps someone see or do something differently
Caption: A piece doesn't need to be perfect to be great. If it's yours, it fits where you're going, people get it the first time, and it actually helps someone, it's ready. The visuals, the analogies, a clever line…all of that is a bonus. You'll learn more from how people respond than from another round of edits.

## The person wrote

Card: A piece of content is great when it's: Relevant to you / Relevant to your business / Fully understandable / Useful (meaning it helps someone see or do something differently)
Caption: A piece of content doesn't need to be perfect to be great. If it's yours, it fits where you're going, people get it the first time, and it actually helps someone, it's ready. Don't wait to ship it. The visuals, the clever line, the right words…all of that is extra. You'll learn a lot more by posting 100 ready pieces than 0 perfect pieces.

## What changed

- "Don't wait to ship it." added: a direct imperative · rule: piece
