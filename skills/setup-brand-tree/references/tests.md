# Tests: when each layer is done

Each entry has a **gate**: the condition for `confirmed`, set out in its layer's file under `../interviews/` and summed up here. A layer is done when it passes its test, not when you've asked enough questions. Run the test with the person, tell them the result plainly, and if it fails, say what's missing and go back to fill it.

Mark a layer `done` in `progress.md` only after it passes.

A test marked "later, by the person" needs someone outside the studio. It is never a condition for `confirmed` or `done`; offer it once, and record the result when the person brings it back.

## Values

**Gate (each value):** at least one costly choice behind it, told as a story; every field filled; the person recognizes it as theirs.

**Done when:** there are one to three values, each has every field and a rank, and the decision test passes.

**Decision test:** ask the person for three real decisions from their past, ideally hard ones (a job, a client, an offer, a public stance). Then, for each decision, check whether the values would have predicted what they chose.

- If all three line up, the test passes.
- If one doesn't, ask about it. Either a value is missing, the ranking is wrong, or a listed value is aspirational (who they want to be, not who they are).
- If the list predicts nothing, start the layer again from their decisions.

## Behaviors

**Gate (each behavior):** it's observable, it repeats (two instances or more, one dated), and it ties to a confirmed value. Until the value is confirmed, it stays a draft with the candidate value in its log.

**Done when:** every value has at least one behavior or refusal that proves it, and each one has a specific story or result behind it.

**Check:** each behavior has a dated moment or a result behind it. If it doesn't, it's an intention, not a behavior.

**Colleague check (later, by the person):** ask someone who worked with them for a month whether they'd confirm each behavior.

## POVs

**Gate (each POV):** the claim, the ground and the condition are all present, and a reasonable peer could reject the claim.

**Done when:** at least 5 are confirmed (the minimum canon's default, in `templates/progress.md`), and each one is disagreeable, names what it rejects and names its condition.

**Disagreement test:** for each POV, ask, "Name a real person or a common source in your field who would disagree." If they can't, it's probably a truism.

**Condition check:** any POV without a condition stays at draft, with confidence forming.

## Philosophy

**Gate:** it explains at least three confirmed POVs (its `explains` line), and the person says it's the thread.

**Done when:** the gate holds, every lens has an answer or a flagged gap, and no confirmed POV contradicts it.

**Check:** read each confirmed POV next to the philosophy. If one doesn't follow from it, either the POV needs a condition, the philosophy is missing something, or the POV belongs on the stop list. Ask the person which.

## Thesis

**Gate (confirmed when):** it traces to the confirmed philosophy and the confirmed goal (its `from` line), it has a catalyst backed by a story, and the person says they'd put their name on it. `claim`, `for` and `catalyst` are in the person's words or a sharper version they accepted. The `branches` field may still be partial; this is what the minimum canon needs.

**Done when:** it's one sentence, names who it's for and what changes for them, has a catalyst backed by a story, has a falsifier, and at least 2 branches hold it up.

**Filter test:** pick three topics the person has posted or talked about. Ask, "Does this connect to your thesis?" If most of their best material doesn't connect, the thesis is too narrow or the wrong one.

**Zoom-out test (later, by the person):** give one of their best pieces to someone who doesn't know the thesis and ask them to guess it. If they can't, the thesis may not be what the person actually stands for.

## Branches

**Done when:** at least 3 exist, each with its parts, limits, mechanism type, the desires it answers, and plain definitions of its key terms.

**Check:** ask the person to explain one branch in two minutes using only the parts listed. If they need something that isn't written down, add it.

## Stop list

**Done when:** at least 5 entries, each with a reason (the minimum canon's default, in `templates/progress.md`).

## Stories

**Done when:** every value and every top-ranked POV has at least one story, and every story has a permission marked.

## Vehicle

**Done when:** the goal has all four answers (what should happen, what they need to be known for, what to do, what to learn), every open offer has all its fields, and every result carries a permission.

## Brand strategy

**Done when:** every segment is described in the segment's own words, not the person's, and the target association passes the two-directions test.

**Two-directions test:** run it once the thesis and the goal are both confirmed. Write two answers side by side:

1. What the person layer says they can honestly be known for (from the thesis, top POVs and branches)
2. What the goal says they need to be known for

Then:

- If they overlap, the overlap becomes the target association.
- If the goal needs a reputation the person layer can't support, tell the person plainly. Either the goal is wrong for them or the vehicle is the wrong way to reach it. Change the goal or the vehicle, never the canon, because content built on beliefs they don't hold won't sound like them.
- If the person layer supports something the goal leaves out, ask whether that points to a better goal or a missing offer.

Then check the audience side: does the target association answer a desire a segment already has (D IDs)? If it answers none, it may be true and needed, but readers won't notice it. Look for the desire it's closest to, or adjust the wording so it speaks to one.

When the person says yes to the overlap, set the `status:` line under "Known for" in `brand-strategy.md` to `confirmed`. Run the test again whenever the goal changes.

**Reputation check (later, by the person, once or twice a year):** the person asks a few clients, peers and connectors what they think of first when they hear the person's name. Record their exact words in `brand-strategy.md`, compare them with the target association, and note the gap in `progress.md`. The gap is what the content isn't doing yet.

**Check:** for each segment, ask, "Is this something a client actually said, or how you'd describe them?" Replace anything that's the person's words with real client phrasing.

## Audience

**Done when:** each segment has a stage of awareness and at least 3 desires, each with real quotes and sources behind it, the person's own scores, and 3 WILL and 3 WON'T statements.

**Checks:**

- **Source check:** any desire without a real quote stays draft.
- **Score check:** the person scored before seeing AI scores. If the two disagree by more than 3 points on any measure (a starting default), ask why. The person's score wins, but the reason often reveals something.
- **WON'T check:** before a WON'T statement feeds content, a real client must have said or shown something like it. Unconfirmed ones stay draft.
- **Pairing check:** every WILL and WON'T sits under one desire. None is shared across desires.

## Content plan

**Done when:** it names the reputation gap, the quarter's branches with a reason for each, at least one desire paired with each branch, and the layer mix with a reason.

**Cohesion test (for every piece later):** does it make the target association more likely to stick, by repeating it or going deeper into it?

## Voice

**Done for the minimum canon when:** there are at least three W rules the person confirmed, each with a before and an after example. The ten default rules (`status: default`) don't count; a default rule counts once the person says yes to it and it becomes `status: confirmed`. That is enough for the minimum.

**Fully done when:** at least 10 rules with before and after examples and at least 5 approved samples.

**Blind test (later, by the person):** mix three of their own pieces with three drafts written from the canon. Ask someone who knows their work to pick out the drafts. If they can, ask how, and turn the answer into a new rule.

## Core summary

**Done when:** the person has read it and confirmed it.

Write it only from confirmed entries. Keep it to one to two pages: the thesis, the philosophy's thread, the target association, the values in rank order, the top POVs, the branches in rank order, the segments, the stop list and the main voice rules. Cite IDs.
