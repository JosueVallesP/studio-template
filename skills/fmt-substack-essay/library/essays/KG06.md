Yes, you read that right. Trillion.

To help you wrap your head around that number: you could take a full trillion off his net worth and he would still be the wealthiest man on the planet. INSANE.

He has since fallen back to billionaire status, but the glass ceiling is now broken, and in the next decades, we will probably see more and more people enter the trillionaire club.

Everyone sees this level of staggering inequality, and the immediate reaction is usually moral outrage:
