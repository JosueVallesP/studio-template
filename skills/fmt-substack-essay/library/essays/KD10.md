## Essay

One way I grow my newsletter is through swapping promotions.

Recently I spoke to 3 creators with similarly sized audiences. But before we worked together, I signed up for their emails. When I promote brands, it is my reputation on the line. I don’t want to send you somewhere crap just so I can get more readers.

Unfortunately, I had to turn down all 3.

Why? Because their content was AI-generated.

It felt like I was reading something straight from Claude. And I do not want to associate with people who do not put time and effort into speaking with their audience.

This is just one example of how the ‘AI opportunity’ is ruining opportunities.

There’s a whiplash to the AI craze, and we’re just starting to see the sting.

Social media flags fake stuff. AI-written books are being witch-hunted. Services are starting to promote the fact that they are not AI-driven.

Everything you gain from writing with AI comes at a heavy price.

Because as the world becomes increasingly fake, the true reward is for being real.

People want to do business with real people. This is true of peer-to-peer connections, but also with your customers and clients. They are being bombarded by AI-generated swill. Everything sounds suspiciously the same. When you are different, when you are human, you are like a lighthouse in the sea of noise.

People flock to you because they know you are worth their time.

Your best clients. Your exciting opportunities. Your coolest connections.

Everything is downstream from your writing. It is the magnet that attracts success. Put out crap and don’t be surprised when the results start to stink.

If you want to thrive in this AI-driven age, you must double down on being real.

On being unique, useful, and undeniably you.

The internet will be divided by real people and robots.

Think about where you’d like to land,

Kieran
