## Life is a Miracle

I don’t know what you define a miracle as, but the reality is that the odds of a universe with physical constants that even allow atoms to exist are infinitesimal.

The odds of those atoms forming self-replicating molecules? Even lower.

The odds of those molecules evolving into conscious beings who can reflect on that process? Astronomical.

So yeah. Miracle.

My favourite definition of life is “locally reversing entropy” which blew my mind the first time I heard it

Entropy is the natural tendency of the universe to move toward disorder.

Everything is constantly falling into more and more chaos until eventually the heat death happens, and we all disappear.

Life , however, creates and maintains order. Cells repair themselves. Organisms grow. Ecosystems self-regulate.

Biologists call this homeostasis: the ability of living things to keep internal balance even as everything around them changes.

In nature, this simple feedback system keeps incredibly complex organisms alive.
