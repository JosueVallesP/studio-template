## Essay (free preview only; the rest is paywalled)

BOY: The Thessalonian you’re fighting, he’s the biggest man I’ve ever seen. Achilles mounts the boy’s horse. BOY: I wouldn’t want to fight him. ACHILLES: That’s why no one will remember your name.

Our world is binary, no our universe is binary, I’m not talking about quantum mechanics here even though that might apply to some extent, I’m talking about how we move through the world, and we do that with choice.

You either ask the person out or you don't. You either start the company or you don't. You either get on the boat or you stay on the shore, I could go on and on and on, there is no half decision, 47% commitment type of choice and when I first watched the movie Troy, what Achilles said made me realize two things

- Nothing worth doing is easy.

- There are only those who do and those who do not do.
