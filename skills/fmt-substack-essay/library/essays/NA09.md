## Essay

Intelligence and peace. We assume the first buys the second.

Look around. The smartest people you know are often the most tormented. The sharpest minds run the darkest simulations. Intelligence, left unsupervised, becomes a machine for manufacturing problems that do not exist yet.

That is the uncomfortable joke inside this question. We treat smart as the highest compliment, and yet the supposed prize of all that intelligence, a good life, quietly fails to arrive.

Because intelligence answers how. It never answers whether it is enough.

Smart Wins Arguments. Wise Wins Life.

A brilliant mind can optimise a career it never questioned. It can win a game it should never have entered. It can construct a flawless argument for why now is not the time to be present, to rest, to enjoy what it already has. The smarter the mind, the better the excuses.

Happiness is a different exam. It does not test processing power. It tests what you have chosen to want. A person of average intellect who wants what he already has will outscore a genius who cannot stop wanting more.

This is not an argument against intelligence. Dumb and happy is not the offer. The offer is to point the intelligence at the real problem. Most people aim their smartest thinking at their work and run their inner life on factory settings.

Turning Smart Into Happy

- Use your intellect to audit your desires, not just serve them. Ask where each one came from. Most were installed by someone else.

- Solve happiness like an engineering problem: inputs, outputs, iterations. Sleep, sunlight, movement, fewer inputs, honest relationships. It is not mysterious. It is maintenance.

- Stop rehearsing disasters. Simulation is a talent. Aimed at the future’s worst cases all day, it is a curse.

- Ask the question in the quote, honestly, once a year. Let it sting.

Intelligence is the engine. Peace is the destination. It is entirely possible to drive the fastest car in the city, in circles.

Being smart is a tool. Being happy is a decision about where to point it.
