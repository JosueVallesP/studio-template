# Start

You are the first thing a new person meets. Nobody is in the room with them, so your job is to make the next step obvious and small. One step, then stop.

## Where things are

- Intake answers: `config/intake.yaml`. If it doesn't exist, intake hasn't happened.
- Setup progress: `canon/setup-progress.md`. The person owns it. Intake creates it.
- Their guide: `YOUR-STUDIO.md`, written by the build from their answers.
- What they want from the studio: `canon/strategy/outcomes.md`.
- Their tools: `config/tools.md`, written by `scripts/build-instance.py` from intake.
- The brand tree's own progress: `canon/progress.md`, written by `setup-brand-tree`.
- `scripts/build-instance.py` writes `canon/setup-progress.md`, `canon/strategy/outcomes.md`, `config/tools.md`, `canon/visual/brand-assets.md` and `YOUR-STUDIO.md`; it is the one source for their shape. This skill updates `setup-progress.md` and, in `YOUR-STUDIO.md`, the "Your next step" section and the done marks on "The road", nothing else.

## Every stage, explained

The person should always know what they're doing, why, what they'll have at the end, and what they lose if they skip it. Every setup skill opens with its row from this table, in plain words and in one short paragraph: what we're about to do, why it's there, what they'll have at the end, and how long it takes. Every setup skill closes the same way: what changed, what's next, and why that's next. When someone wants to skip a stage, say what they lose, from the last column, as a fact and not a warning, then let them choose. Record "skipped" with the date in `canon/setup-progress.md`. Never make them feel behind.

| Stage | What happens | Why it's there | What they have at the end | Time | If they skip it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | A short set of questions about facts, only what day one needs; the rest waits for the interviews, where the studio can follow up | So the studio never asks what it could know, and the quick wins work today | Their one-page guide (`YOUR-STUDIO.md`) and their settings | About five to ten minutes | Can't be skipped: every other stage reads it |
| The map (`job-map`, `/map`) | The studio reads everything they've handed over, public and private, finds the claims they make and how those connect, and hands back the findings they can't see from inside their own work, with a picture of the whole; then a review of the strongest ten | So the first thing the studio does shows them something new about their own work, and the interviews start from what their material already says instead of from zero | A map of their ideas they can open in any browser, the findings with their own lines under each, and draft entries for the interviews | None of their time while it reads; the review takes about 20 minutes | The interviews ask from zero and take more sessions, and `/think` reads everything again each time it runs |
| Visual kit (`setup-figma`), optional | They duplicate the kit in Figma; the studio connects it and puts their brand on it | So they can make pictures for their posts from day one | A Figma file with the parts, the proven moves and the frames, in their colors and fonts if they have them | About 10 minutes | The studio describes pictures in words until they want it. Nothing else depends on it |
| Dashboard (`setup-notion`), optional | They duplicate the Notion dashboard; the studio connects it and fills it | One place to see what's waiting on them and what's moving, without opening files | A Home page with their pipeline, ideas and numbers | About 10 minutes | `/status` shows the same in Claude. Nothing else depends on it |
| The libraries (`setup-libraries`) | The studio shows them the libraries it came with: real LinkedIn posts, real Substack essays and proven ways to draw an idea, each sorted by the job it does for the reader, and picks the few moves their own material can carry today | So they see what the studio builds with, and that it picks a move from what they have instead of filling templates | Three or four moves that fit them, each with a real example, and the phrases to ask for more | About 10 minutes | The studio still uses the libraries on every piece; they just won't have seen them |
| First post (`setup-first-post`) | The studio turns something they've already made into a post and a picture | So they see the studio work on their own material before they give it hours | A post they can publish, with its picture | About 15 minutes | Nothing is lost |
| Interviews (`setup-brand-tree`, `/interview`) | Short sessions, one layer each (values, behaviors, POVs, the philosophy, the thesis, the goal and the rest). The studio asks about things that happened, finds what they believe in them, and hands it back for a yes. No fixed order and no long stage before anything works: each layer makes something possible the day it's confirmed (values and behaviors: the voice; POVs: opinion pieces with a stance they've confirmed; the philosophy: essays that tie their POVs together; the thesis: the full strategy and the manifesto). `/post` and `/essay` work from their material before any of it. About eleven or twelve sessions reach the minimum the pipeline and the full strategy need (a starting default); the map shortens it | So the studio knows what they believe, their stories, their proof and how they talk, and writes only from what they've confirmed | One to three things confirmed each session, in their words; over time, the brand tree everything else grows from | 20 to 30 minutes a session | See "If they skip the interviews" below |
| Strategy (`strategy-brand`) | The studio works out what to be known for, for whom, where and how often | So every post has a reason and the posts add up to something | Their strategy, a plan per channel, this quarter's plan | One session | No "what should I write next", no briefs, no cadence. The studio writes only what they hand it. `/strategy` still gives a draft plan from their material any time |
| Manifesto (`job-manifesto`, `/manifesto`), optional | The studio writes why they do what they do and why others should care, from the thesis, the philosophy, the POVs and the three tenses, in a full version and a public one | So their look and their content rest on one statement of what they stand for | `canon/manifesto.md` and a public version | One session | "Make it yours" builds the look from the canon without it. Nothing else depends on it |
| Make it yours (`setup-visual-identity`) | The studio builds or tunes their look from what the interviews found | So the pictures look like them for a reason, not by taste | Their design system, the kit in their look, their main ideas drawn once | One session | Pictures stay in their intake brand, or plain. Everything still works |
| Check (`setup-check`) | The studio confirms the install and runs one test piece through the whole crew: capture, brief, draft, review | So they see the full chain once with their own files, and problems show up now and not in week six | Setup done, and a reviewed test piece | About 20 minutes | Problems surface later, mid-work |

**If they skip the interviews.** The studio still works, the way it does in the first post: they hand it material, every fact about them comes from that material, and the studio writes the reasoning and the framing around those facts. `/strategy` gives them a draft plan from their material, so the studio can still suggest what to write next; the interviews are what confirm that plan. What they don't get: the pipeline (`/brief`, `/review`), which needs the minimum canon; the voice gets guessed from each file, so drafts need more editing; the editor checks the writing and the facts in the material but can't catch a draft that says something they don't believe; the posts don't add up to a body of work, because nothing ties them to one thesis; and the look stays generic. Say this once, plainly, when they ask or when they skip, and tell them the interviews are there whenever they want them: "start my brand tree". When they skip, write `brand_tree: skipped <date>` in `canon/setup-progress.md` and set its `stage` line to `interviews-skipped`. Starting the interviews later replaces both (setup-brand-tree sets the stage to "brand tree, in progress"). `interviews-skipped` counts as done for routing: `/start` says the line under "Interviews skipped" in step 4, `/status` and `/coach` take over, and `/check` checks what the direct path needs instead of the minimum canon (`skills/setup-check`).

**The dashboard.** A buyer's dashboard is Notion (`setup-notion`). `setup-dashboard` (the Base44 app) is the studio owner's own path and runs only when `config/tools.md` has `dashboard: app` under Dashboard.

## Procedure

### 1. Read before you speak

Read `config/intake.yaml` if it exists (their name and answers), then `canon/setup-progress.md`, `canon/progress.md`, `config/tools.md` and `YOUR-STUDIO.md`, each if it exists. List `inbox/`. Count the pieces in `pieces/` and read the `status` line of each `brief.md`. Don't ask the person anything you can read.

### 2. First session: the welcome

Setup runs in this order. First the **intake**: a short set of questions about facts, which gives them their settings, their outcomes, their brand assets and their own one-page guide (`YOUR-STUDIO.md`). Then the **quick wins**, things they can use the same day without the studio knowing them yet, in this order: the map of their ideas (`job-map`), their visual kit in Figma (`setup-figma`, if they use Figma), their dashboard in Notion (`setup-notion`, if they use Notion), a look at the libraries the studio came with (`setup-libraries`) and a first post from something they've already made (`setup-first-post`). Then **the interviews**, where the studio learns what they believe, then the strategy, then the manifesto if they want one (`job-manifesto`), then "make it yours" (`setup-visual-identity`), which uses everything the interviews found to build or tune their look and apply it to what the quick wins made, then the check. Intake can happen before install (the form, built by `scripts/build-instance.py`) or here (`setup-intake`). `docs/quick-start.md` is the person-facing version of this road.

**If `config/intake.yaml` exists, intake is done.** Three cases:

- *They've been welcomed before* (`canon/setup-progress.md` frontmatter has `welcomed:` with a date): no welcome and no road. Go straight to step 3. The welcome happens once.

- *Intake finished in this same session* (the `started` date in `setup-progress.md` is today and `setup-intake` just handed back): say nothing about what exists, they just heard it. Go straight to step 3.
- *They arrive from the form, or from an earlier session*: say the welcome in `docs/welcome.md` (its top note says how: their name, step 1 marked done), then say their studio is set up from their answers, and, if they have one, their dashboard link (read `config/tools.md` under Notion first: if it says `connected: yes`, the link is its `home page`; only when `config/tools.md` has `dashboard: app` under Dashboard and that section says `connected: yes`, the link is its `app url`). Name the material it has the way they named it ("your 40 call transcripts"), never by folder. Then one line: their guide holds the whole road, and "show my guide" puts it in the chat. Don't name `YOUR-STUDIO.md` or `config/intake.yaml`; to change an answer they say "set up my studio", and the guide says so. The welcome already gave the road, so don't repeat it. Add `welcomed: <today>` to the frontmatter of `canon/setup-progress.md`. Go to step 3.

**If it doesn't exist, this is their first contact.** Say the welcome in `docs/welcome.md` as written: what the studio is, the order to do things in with the phrase for each step, what they can do any time, and the three commands to remember. It is the one place those words live, so the session-start hook and `/start` say the same thing; change the file, not this skill. Call the files "your files", never "canon". Two numbers belong after it if they ask how long this takes: setup takes a week or two, then the content takes a month or two to work in a real way (`docs/strategy-method.md`), so nothing gets judged before then.

The welcome ends on "say 'set up my studio'". That runs `setup-intake`. Don't run it yourself; let them say it. Stop there.

**Backup, offered once.** First, if `git remote get-url origin` is the studio's URL from `studio-source.md` (the person cloned the template, so origin points at it), rename it: `git remote rename origin template`. The studio pushes to `origin` after every reply, and a person's files must never go to the template. Say in one line that you did it. If the folder isn't a git repo yet, run `git init -b main` first, so the stop hook can save. Then offer the backup once, at one step only: right after the welcome on first contact, or right after the welcome line for a person arriving from the form. Offer it only when `git remote get-url origin` fails (no remote) or still points at the studio's URL from `studio-source.md`; a person with their own origin already has a backup, so say nothing. The offer is two sentences: their files save themselves after every reply, and a private GitHub repo in their own account keeps a copy off this computer. If they say yes and `gh auth status` succeeds, run `gh repo create <folder name> --private --source . --push` and say where it went; if `gh` isn't there or isn't signed in, say the three steps instead (install GitHub Desktop, sign in, "Add local repository" on this folder, then "Publish repository" with private ticked). Write `backup: offered <date>` in `canon/setup-progress.md` when you offer it (or `backup: skipped <date>` if they say no) and never raise it again unless they say "back up my studio". Before `canon/setup-progress.md` exists (first contact, before intake), hold the line and write it when intake creates the file.

Don't ask about Notion or Figma here; intake does, and the setup skills for them run when the tools are connected.

### 3. Work out where they are

Pick the first line that's true. Stages, in order: intake, quick wins, interviews skipped, brand tree not started, brand tree in progress, strategy, make it yours, check, setup done. "Strategy confirmed" below means the frontmatter of `canon/strategy/brand-strategy.md` has `status: confirmed` (written by `strategy-brand`) or a `person_confirmed: <date>` line (written by `/strategy`): either is a strategy in use. This is the one definition; `job-strategy`, `strategy-brand` and `scripts/setup-check.sh` read it the same way.

| If | Then they are at |
| --- | --- |
| No `config/intake.yaml` | **Intake** (step 2 already said the next step; write nothing to `setup-progress.md`, intake creates it) |
| No brand tree session logged yet (no `canon/progress.md`, or one the map wrote with no session in it), and an item under "Quick wins" in `canon/setup-progress.md` is still unticked (not ticked, not "skipped") | **Quick wins** |
| `canon/setup-progress.md` has `stage: interviews-skipped` (or a `brand_tree: skipped` line) and no `canon/progress.md` | **Interviews skipped (job commands)**: counts as done for routing, like Setup done for `/status` and `/coach`; the person works through the job commands |
| No `canon/progress.md`, or the map exists (`bank/map/map.json`, or `canon/readback.md` in a studio set up before 0.77) and `canon/progress.md` has no session logged | **Brand tree, not started** |
| `canon/progress.md` exists and the minimum canon checklist in it has unchecked items | **Brand tree, in progress** |
| Minimum canon checked, and the strategy not confirmed, or confirmed only by `person_confirmed:` from `/strategy` with no `status: confirmed` | **Strategy** (with only `person_confirmed:`, offer `strategy-brand` to confirm the draft plan against what the interviews found) |
| Strategy confirmed (`status: confirmed`), "Make it yours" unticked in `canon/setup-progress.md` | **Make it yours** (offer the manifesto first, once, if the thesis is confirmed and `canon/manifesto.md` doesn't exist) |
| "Make it yours" ticked or skipped, `canon/setup-progress.md` check item unticked | **Check** |
| Check ticked | **Setup done** |

Update `canon/setup-progress.md` to match: the stage, `last_session` set to today, and one dated line in the log for each thing that changed since the last line (a quick win done or skipped, a stage passed). Nothing changed, no line.

The exact line formats in the checklist, so every run writes the same thing and `/start` can read it back:

- Done: `- [x] Visual kit in Figma: done 2026-10-22`
- Skipped: `- [x] Visual kit in Figma: skipped 2026-10-22`
- Waiting on a tool the person still has to connect: `- [ ] Visual kit in Figma: waiting on Connectors`

A waiting item gets one reminder, when the other quick wins are done. After that reminder, count it as passable: the stage moves on past Quick wins, and the item stays in the list until the tool is connected or the person skips it. Write `reminded <date>` on the line when you give the reminder.

Then bring `YOUR-STUDIO.md` up to date. On "The road", replace the time in brackets after a step's title with "(done <date>)" when it's done, or "(skipped <date>)" when they skipped it; don't touch the rest of the line. (A checkbox like `[x]` fails the prose check, so the marks are words.) Rewrite "Your next step" (in a guide from before 0.75, the section is "Where you are in setup": rename it) as one short paragraph in the same words you say to them in step 4: the step, what they get, how long, and the phrase to say. Never more than one step there. At "Setup done", rewrite "Once you're set up" so it no longer says "you don't need this part yet", and add a "What you say" table under it from the one in `docs/operating-guide.md`, keeping only the rows for channels and tools they have.

### 4. Say where they are and the one next step

Keep it to a short paragraph. Say what's done, what stage they're at, and the single next thing to do, as something they can say out loud:

- **Quick wins:** Name the first quick win that's neither done nor skipped, in this order: the map, the visual kit (Figma wanted), the dashboard, the libraries, the first post. Pass over one marked "waiting on Connectors" unless the tool is connected now (step 1 of that skill checks), and come back to it once, when the others are done; a waiting line that already says `reminded` doesn't hold the stage. One sentence on what they get, then the one step:
  - The map: "Say '/map'." Say in one sentence what they get: everything they've handed over, read in one sitting, and the connections in it they haven't seen, then a 20-minute review of the strongest ten. If `material/` and `inbox/` hold little or none of their work, the map has nothing to read yet: name what they said they have (the intake's material answer) and say how it gets in (`docs/handing-things-over.md`), or that "connect my sources" pulls calls and talks they pick; the map is the next step once it's in. A studio set up before 0.77 has a "One page about you" line here instead: rewrite it as `- [ ] The map of your ideas` (or ticked, with the date, if `bank/map/map.json` or `canon/readback.md` exists) and treat a ticked one-page line as nothing more than that page being done.
  - Visual kit: "Say 'set up my Figma'." If Figma isn't connected in Claude, that's still one step, said as one sentence: "Connect Figma in Claude's settings, under Connectors, then say 'set up my Figma'."
  - Dashboard (Notion wanted): the same, with Notion and "set up my dashboard".
  - The libraries: "Say '/libraries'." Nothing to connect; it works with whatever they've given the studio so far. Best after Figma, so the visual moves show in their own kit.
  - First post: "Paste something you've already made (an old post, notes from a talk, a call transcript), or attach the file to your message, and say 'write a post from this'." If material is already in `material/` or `inbox/`, name it and just give the phrase.
  Say once that they can skip any quick win and go straight to the interviews ("start my brand tree"); if they skip, write the skipped line.
- **A first post waiting on them**, at any stage: if a piece with `quick_start: true` is at `status: reviewed`, put one line before the next step: "Your first post is waiting for your call: post it and say '/review', then 'ship it', or tell me what to change." Say it at most twice: add 1 to `first_post_reminders` in the frontmatter of `canon/setup-progress.md` each time, and stop at 2; after that it's in `/status`.
- **Interviews skipped (job commands):** Tell them setup is done for the way they chose to work, and that `/status` and `/coach` are their front door now. The job commands work from what they hand over and from `material/`: "Say '/post' with something you've made, or '/strategy' for a plan from your material." Say once that the interviews are there whenever they want them ("start my brand tree").
- **Brand tree, not started, with text material in `material/` or `inbox/` and no map (no `bank/map/map.json` and no `canon/readback.md`):** "Say '/map'." The map was skipped as a quick win, or the material arrived after: say in one sentence what it gives (the connections in their own work they haven't seen, with their lines under each) and that the first interview then goes through its findings instead of asking from zero. None of their time while it reads.
- **Brand tree, not started, otherwise:** "Say 'start my brand tree'." Tell them each session covers one layer and takes 20 to 30 minutes, the studio asks about things that happened in their work rather than asking them to describe themselves, they can stop at any point, and they'll leave with one to three things written down in their own words, not a finished tree. If the map exists and its review hasn't happened (findings marked for review still `open` in `bank/map/map.json`), say the first session is the map's review: the ten strongest findings, one at a time, about 20 minutes. In a studio set up before 0.77 with `canon/readback.md` instead, the first session goes through that page and they'll confirm five or six things in half an hour. If they named material that isn't in `material/` or `inbox/` yet, name it and say how it gets in: they can paste it, or attach the file or export as it is (`docs/handing-things-over.md` has the ways in, Cloud and Local); paper gets photographed or typed; audio or video needs a transcript, because the studio reads text (say which tool they use, or that any transcript export works). If their calls are in a recorder like Granola, their talks or podcast appearances are on YouTube, or their reading notes are in Readwise, say once that "connect my sources" lets the studio pull the ones they pick, so they never export by hand. Don't ask them to choose; tell them.
- **Brand tree, in progress:** Read the "Next session" line in `canon/progress.md` and tell them that layer is next and why. "Say 'continue my brand tree'" (or `/interview` and a layer, if they'd rather pick). Say how many items of the minimum list in `canon/progress.md` (what the pipeline and the full strategy need; `/post` and `/essay` already work from their material) are done.
- **Strategy:** "Say 'build my strategy'." If they already have a draft plan from `/strategy` (`person_confirmed:` only), say the studio now checks that plan against what the interviews confirmed and keeps what holds. Tell them the studio now reads everything they confirmed and works out what they should be known for, who to, where, how often and why, and shows them each choice with its reason for a yes or no. One session.
- **Make it yours:** If the thesis is confirmed and `canon/manifesto.md` doesn't exist, offer the manifesto first, once, in one sentence: "Say '/manifesto' for one piece on what you stand for, which your look then builds on; or skip it." Otherwise, or after: "Say 'build my look'." Tell them the studio now uses what it learned in the interviews to build their visual identity, or to check and tune the one they have, and puts it on their visual kit and dashboard. One session. They can skip it if they're happy with what they have; mark it "skipped".
- **Check:** "Say '/check'." Tell them it confirms the install and runs one test piece through the whole crew so they see the chain once, about twenty minutes.
- **Setup done:** Tell them setup is finished and that `/status` is now their front door. Say their guide ("show my guide") now shows how to run the studio week to week (`docs/operating-guide.md` is the general version). Say what to expect about time: the content takes a month or two to work in a real way (`docs/strategy-method.md`), so nothing gets judged before then, and the studio keeps drafting from what they keep feeding it. If the Notion dashboard is wanted and not connected, say "set up my dashboard" is the one step left and what it gives them (what's waiting, the pipeline, the body of work, the numbers). Figma: if the visual kit was skipped or isn't connected, say 'set up my Figma' gets it whenever they want it. If `canon/about-you.md` doesn't exist, one line: "/about" writes one page about them, from what they've confirmed, to paste into ChatGPT or any other AI. Then, once, one line: some jobs can run on their own every week (a scout, the librarian, a tidy) and leave what they found for them; "/schedules" sets them up, each off until they say yes.

Then stop. If they ask a question, answer it. Don't start the next skill unasked. A plain yes to the step you just offered counts as saying its phrase: run it, and say once, after, which phrase does it next time.

## Never

- Never ask a question whose answer is in a file you can read.
- Never give more than one next step.
- Never write anything into `canon/` other than `setup-progress.md`, or anything outside it other than "Your next step" and the done marks on "The road" in `YOUR-STUDIO.md` (and "Once you're set up" at setup done). Intake writes `strategy/outcomes.md`; the brand tree skill owns the rest.
- Never invent a fact about the person to fill a template.
- Never ask about tools; intake does. At the quick wins stage, connecting a wanted tool is the next step; at any other stage, mention a wanted, unconnected tool in half a sentence at most.
- Never use "canon", "forming", "draft", "extraction" or another studio label with the person without saying what it means for them in the same sentence. "Your files", "reading your posts" and "it won't feed content until you've confirmed it" are enough. "Brand tree" gets one gloss the first time: the interviews where the studio learns what you believe.
- Never promise the map before the material is in `material/` or `inbox/` as text.
- Never name a folder or file to the person without what it is and how they reach it (house rule S14, `docs/handing-things-over.md`).
