# The dual harvest plan

Follow this structure and fill it only with what came from the session and the person's files. Every claim about a guest carries its source, nothing about a guest is invented, and every line of every invitation is true on the day it would be sent.

Length: 900 to 1,400 words plus the five invitations. A working document, built to act on this week.

Written to the person, in second person, in their words, following the house rules (`skills/craft-house-rules`): full sentences, active voice, plain words, no em dashes, no copywriting devices, no hype. Run `python3 scripts/check-prose.py` on it before showing it.

---

## Template

```markdown
# Your dual harvest plan
<today's date> · Series: <series name>

## The system in one paragraph

<Three or four plain sentences: the series, who round one is for, what every invitation produces whatever happens, and the rule that no selling happens inside the collaborations.>

## The series

**Name:** <the series name>
**Format:** <what it is and the honest time ask per guest>
**The core questions:**
<The five or six questions, numbered, with a note on which change for each guest.>
**What it shows about you:** <One or two sentences tying it to the thesis or branch it proves, with the ID, or the person's words when there's no canon.>

## Round one: the five

<Per person, in send order:>

### 1. <Name> · <context> · <how warm>
**The specific thing:** <the true, particular observation the invitation is built on, with its source>
**The invitation:**
> <the judged version, 60 to 110 words, ready to send>
**Piece:** <pieces/<yyyy-mm-dd>-invite-<guest-slug>/>

<Repeat for all five. Invitations one and two use launch language; a note under three to five says to add real guest names once earlier guests have said yes, and to keep launch language if they haven't.>

## The transition plan

**The natural next step:** <the closing question inside the format, with the rule: the written piece stands complete on its own.>
**Follow-up shapes:** <the two or three drafted shapes, each anchored to "something they actually said", to be written for real from each guest's answers.>
**Your line:** <their no-selling boundary, in their words.>
**The generosity rule:** <their version of the one-third, no-ask rule, with two concrete examples for them: an introduction between two guests, a useful resource sent with nothing attached.>

## Decisions

<Five or six. Each in this shape:>

### <Number>. <The decision, as a sentence they could say out loud>

**Why:** <What in the session it comes from.>
**First move:** <One action, small enough to do this week.>
**You'll know it worked when:** <Something you can see, not a feeling.>

## Dates and rhythm

<The send order with the first date on a named day. The rule for the next sends: on reply or after one week. The production promise: questions out within two days of a yes, the piece published within a week of the answers. The follow-up: two weeks, then a month, then the door stays open.>

## The tracker

<The columns, as in tracker.csv: name, invitation date, response, interview status, publish date, transition tried, outcome, next touch. The monthly question: which specific lines and which kinds of guests are turning into conversations?>

## What's still thin

<An honest list: specific lines still thin, guest facts waiting to be checked, the series subject if it was chosen with low confidence, anything in an invitation to check before it goes out.>

## After round one

<Three or four lines: ten invitations before judging the method, round two reaches for the names round one earned, the published pieces are what lasts, and new names go back on the relationships list as the series meets them.>
```

---

## Which decisions to include

Five or six, each traced to the session.

**Send the first invitation by the date.** The warmest name, on a named day this week. The first move is sending it, not polishing it.

**The production setup.** Where the questions go out from and where the pieces publish, decided now, so a yes never waits on logistics. First move: write the question document for guest one today.

**The checking pass, if needed.** Any guest fact or specific line marked to check gets checked before its invitation goes out.

**The generosity acts.** Two no-ask moves named now, with who they're for: the introduction to make, the resource to send. First move: make the introduction this week, because it takes ten minutes and it's the plan's proof of good faith.

**The tracker.** One row per guest, started today, in `tracker.csv`.

**Round two's reach.** One or two bigger names, parked with a note on what round one has to produce before their invitations make sense. This decision gives ambition a place to live that isn't round one.

## Rules for the decisions

- Trace every decision to its source in the session, or cut it.
- Make the first move small enough for this week.
- Make the signal something you can see: a sent message, a published piece, a tracker with rows.
- Every invitation in the document passes the truth test on its send date, and the document says so where the guest-naming note appears.
