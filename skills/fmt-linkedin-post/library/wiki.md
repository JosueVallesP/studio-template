# LinkedIn library wiki

Everything in this library, as entries that link to each other: 57 idea briefs, 18 hook moves, 9 structures, 9 moves inside the body, 6 closes, 10 post types and 357 example posts.

## How the pieces fit

A post starts from an idea. The [[idea-briefs|idea briefs]] help you find one and tell you what material to gather. A post type gives the method for that kind of post. The hook move opens the post, the structure orders the body, the moves inside the body do the work in each part, and the close ends it. Every entry below links to the posts that use it and to the briefs those posts belong to, so you can enter from any side.

The steps for writing a post are in `../SKILL.md`.

## Counting notes

- The counts on hook move entries use each post's primary hook move.
- A post has one brief. 11 posts have none.
- The post type on a brief page is the type the idea usually becomes. The posts carry no type tag.
