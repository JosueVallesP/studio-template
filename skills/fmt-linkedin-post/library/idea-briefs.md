# Idea briefs

An idea brief names the idea under a post that worked, so you can write your own version in any field. This page lists all 57 briefs drawn from the 357 posts in `posts/`. It is part of the [[wiki|library wiki]].

## How to use this page

1. Pick a way in. "By what you have" answers "what can I write?" "By what the reader leaves with" answers "what does my content need more of?"
2. Read the "need" line of each brief under the heading you chose. Pick the briefs where you have what the line asks for.
3. Open the brief. Fill in "the post in one sentence" and answer its questions. Your answers are the raw material for the post.
4. Read two or three of its example posts in full before you write.

For agents: pick a brief from the "need" lines on this page, then read the brief page and at least two of its example posts. A brief feeds the moves stage of `../SKILL.md`. It gives the idea and the material to gather. The hook moves, structures and types still decide the shape. Every post file carries a `brief` line in its frontmatter that links back to its brief.
