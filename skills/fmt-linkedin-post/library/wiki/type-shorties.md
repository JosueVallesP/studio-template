# Shortie

A post type. Back to the [[wiki|library wiki]].

## The method

The full method lives in `types/shorties.md`: [[shorties]]. It has its own gates, steps and judge checks.

## Briefs that usually become this type

- [[the-gripe|The gripe]] (6 posts). Need: something specific that bothers you, and a reason beyond taste.
- [[mock-the-pose|Mock a pose your field keeps striking]] (5 posts). Need: a pose people in your field will recognize in one line.
- [[what-your-reader-needs-to-hear|What your reader needs to hear]] (5 posts). Need: a pressure your readers feel, and a reason it's optional.
- [[one-idea-in-one-line|One idea in one line]] (8 posts). Need: a claim you'd defend, short enough to quote.
- [[a-small-scene|A small scene from outside work]] (6 posts). Need: a small moment with detail, and one thought it gave you.
