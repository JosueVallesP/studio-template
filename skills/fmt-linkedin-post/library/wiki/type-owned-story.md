# Owned story

A post type. Back to the [[wiki|library wiki]].

## The method

The full method lives in `types/owned-story.md`: [[owned-story]]. It has its own gates, steps and judge checks.

## Briefs that usually become this type

- [[your-worst-stretch|Your worst stretch and what it built]] (4 posts). Need: a hard period with dates, money and what you did next.
- [[a-decision-that-made-no-sense|A decision that made no sense on paper]] (3 posts). Need: a real choice where you gave something up.
- [[how-you-got-in-the-room|How you got in the room]] (5 posts). Need: a person or room that seemed out of reach, and what you did.
- [[how-you-got-here|How you got here, year by year]] (5 posts). Need: dates, numbers and a few real setbacks.
- [[you-got-what-everyone-wants|You got what everyone wants]] (2 posts). Need: a goal you reached that your readers still chase.
- [[what-you-got-wrong|What you got wrong for years]] (4 posts). Need: a real mistake with a cost, and the change you made.
- [[a-story-that-carries-a-value|A story that carries a value]] (10 posts). Need: a real event with a turn in it.
- [[a-small-scene|A small scene from outside work]] (6 posts). Need: a small moment with detail, and one thought it gave you.
- [[this-week-at-work|This week at work]] (3 posts). Need: a moment from a call, a meeting or a client file.
- [[what-they-said-to-you|What they said to you]] (3 posts). Need: a line from a client or boss, word for word.
- [[a-rule-you-keep|A rule you keep]] (5 posts). Need: a rule you follow, and the moment or reason behind it.
- [[you-tried-it|You tried it]] (4 posts). Need: a small test you ran, with a clear setup and result.
- [[stepping-away|Stepping away from what everyone uses]] (4 posts). Need: a tool or habit you used for real and have stopped.
