## Briefs that usually become this type

- [[this-weeks-news-through-your-lens|This week's news through your lens]] (8 posts). Need: a fresh event, and knowledge from your field that explains it.
- [[a-change-and-what-to-do|A change, and what to do about it]] (6 posts). Need: a specific change, your read on it and a next step.
- [[outside-evidence|Outside evidence for what you've been saying]] (9 posts). Need: a study, report or respected voice you can cite.
