# Op-ed

A post type. Back to the [[wiki|library wiki]].

## The method

The full method lives in `types/op-ed.md`: [[op-ed]]. It has its own gates, steps and judge checks.

## Briefs that usually become this type

- [[your-own-model|Your own model of how something works]] (5 posts). Need: a model you use with clients, with a name for each part.
- [[the-question-you-get-most|The question you get most]] (3 posts). Need: a question people keep asking you.
- [[from-the-other-side-of-the-table|From the other side of the table]] (3 posts). Need: time spent in the seat your reader is trying to reach.
- [[two-kinds|Two kinds, and how to tell them apart]] (4 posts). Need: a real divide you've seen between people in your field.
- [[a-belief-your-experience-contradicts|A belief your experience contradicts]] (14 posts). Need: common advice in your field, and evidence of your own against it.
- [[fix-a-word|Fix a word your field misuses]] (8 posts). Need: a word clients use loosely, and your definition.
- [[the-real-cause|The real cause of a common complaint]] (5 posts). Need: a complaint you hear often, and what you find underneath.
- [[what-used-to-win|What used to win and doesn't now]] (4 posts). Need: a before and after you lived through.
- [[how-it-really-is|How it really is]] (5 posts). Need: facts or first-hand detail that contradict a common picture.
- [[criticize-your-own-industry|Criticize your own industry]] (3 posts). Need: standing inside an industry, and a specific problem with it.
- [[the-gripe|The gripe]] (6 posts). Need: something specific that bothers you, and a reason beyond taste.
- [[the-shortcut-and-its-cost|The shortcut and its cost]] (3 posts). Need: a shortcut you've seen used, and a case where it went wrong.
- [[the-calm-read|The calm read on the hype]] (4 posts). Need: reasons for doubt or calm about something everyone is loud about.
- [[speak-for-your-profession|Speak for your profession]] (8 posts). Need: standing in a profession, and the exact things outsiders get wrong.
- [[the-practice-your-field-dropped|The practice your field dropped]] (4 posts). Need: a practice you still keep, and the job it does.
- [[an-unresolved-tension|An unresolved tension in your field]] (2 posts). Need: two demands of your work that pull against each other.
- [[what-your-reader-needs-to-hear|What your reader needs to hear]] (5 posts). Need: a pressure your readers feel, and a reason it's optional.
- [[what-they-have-in-common|What they have in common]] (4 posts). Need: several real cases that share one trait.
- [[a-bet-on-the-future|A bet on the future]] (8 posts). Need: a prediction specific enough to be wrong, and your reasons.
- [[someone-should-build-this|Someone should build this]] (4 posts). Need: a gap you've seen first-hand, and who it's for.
