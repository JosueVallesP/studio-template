# Arbitraged content

A post type. Back to the [[wiki|library wiki]].

## The method

The full method lives in `types/arbitraged-content.md`: [[arbitraged-content]]. It has its own gates, steps and judge checks.

## Briefs that usually become this type

- [[one-line-that-changed-your-work|One line that changed your work]] (7 posts). Need: advice or a question someone gave you, and what you did with it.
- [[notes-from-someone-ahead|Notes from someone ahead of you]] (8 posts). Need: real access to a person, a room or a report.
- [[a-masters-principles|A master's principles, distilled]] (16 posts). Need: a book or body of work you know well.
- [[an-old-document-that-still-holds|An old document that still holds]] (6 posts). Need: a real memo, letter, test or ad you can quote word for word.
- [[work-you-admire|Work you admire, taken apart]] (5 posts). Need: one specific piece of work, and the parts that make it work.
- [[a-lens-from-another-field|A lens from another field]] (2 posts). Need: a law or model from outside your field that fits your work.
