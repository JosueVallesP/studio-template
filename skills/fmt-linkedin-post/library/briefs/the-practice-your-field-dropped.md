# The practice your field dropped

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind. It also gives: the feeling of being seen.

## The idea

People in your field stopped doing something and forgot what it was for. Say what it was for.

## The post in one sentence

We stopped [practice]. It was for [purpose].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

People who still do it feel seen. People who dropped it get a reason to pick it back up. The post argues about purpose, so it doesn't read as nostalgia.

## What you need

- A practice you still keep.
- The job it does.
- One case where it paid off.

## Questions to answer

- What did people in your field do ten years ago that they now skip?
- What did it do that the replacement doesn't?
- When did it last save you?
- Why did people stop?

## How it fails

- You never name the purpose.
- The field dropped the practice for a good reason.
