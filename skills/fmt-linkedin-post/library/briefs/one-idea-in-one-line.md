# One idea in one line

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind.

## The idea

You have one claim you can say in a sentence or two. Post it and stop.

## The post in one sentence

The post is the sentence.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A short claim is easy to read, agree with and repeat.

## What you need

- A claim you'd defend.
- Standing that makes it worth hearing from you.

## Questions to answer

- What do you say so often that people quote it back?
- Can you say it in under 40 words?
- Does it hold without explanation?

## How it fails

- Anyone could have posted it.
- It needs the context you cut.
