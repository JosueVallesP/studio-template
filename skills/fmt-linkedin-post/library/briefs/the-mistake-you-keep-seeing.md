# The mistake you keep seeing

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use. It also gives: a changed mind.

## The idea

You watch many people do the same job. Name the mistake you see again and again, and give the fix.

## The post in one sentence

After [sample], the mistake I see most is [mistake], and the fix is [fix].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers check themselves against the mistake. Your sample (deal reviews, clients, hires) makes the claim believable.

## What you need

- A view over many cases.
- A specific mistake.
- What the people who avoid it do.

## Questions to answer

- What mistake do you see every month?
- Why do smart people make it?
- What do the people who avoid it do?
- How many cases have you seen?

## How it fails

- No sample stands behind "most people."
- Nobody makes the mistake you describe.
- You give no fix.
