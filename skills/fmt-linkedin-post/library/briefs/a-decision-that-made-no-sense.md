# A decision that made no sense on paper

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a sense of who you are.

## The idea

You made a choice that looked wrong on paper. Tell why you made it and what happened.

## The post in one sentence

I [made this choice] when everyone said not to, and [this] happened.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The reader wants to know how the bet turned out. It shows what you value when it costs you.

## What you need

- A real decision with something given up.
- Your reasons at the time.
- The outcome.

## Questions to answer

- What choice did people warn you against?
- What did you give up?
- What happened after?

## How it fails

- The decision cost nothing.
- You hide the parts that went badly.
