# A master's principles, distilled

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: something to use.

## The idea

Take a master of your craft, or a book that shaped you, and distill the principles.

## The post in one sentence

[Master or book], in [number] principles.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The body of work is the proof. You save the reader the hours and show your taste.

## What you need

- A source you know well.
- The principles in your words.
- One fact that shows why this source deserves attention.

## Questions to answer

- Whose work do you return to?
- What rules run through all of it?
- Which one do you use most?
- Which principle do people misread?

## How it fails

- A book report.
- Principles copied with no reading of your own.
