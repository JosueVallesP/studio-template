# A bet on the future

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you see where things are going. The reader leaves with: a read on what's happening.

## The idea

Say where you think things are going and what you're doing about it.

## The post in one sentence

I think [this will happen], because [reasons].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A clear bet is easy to remember and to check later. It shows conviction.

## What you need

- A prediction specific enough to be wrong.
- Your reasons.
- What you've staked on it.

## Questions to answer

- What do you expect in two years that others don't?
- Why?
- What are you doing now because of it?

## How it fails

- A safe forecast.
- No reasons.
- Nothing staked.
