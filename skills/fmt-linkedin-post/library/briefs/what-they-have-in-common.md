# What they have in common

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: someone to look up to. It also gives: a changed mind.

## The idea

You've noticed something that the people or things you admire share. Name it and show the cases.

## The post in one sentence

The [best people or things] I know all [share this].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A pattern across cases reads as a finding. The reader checks it against people they know.

## What you need

- Several real cases.
- The shared trait.
- Why it matters.

## Questions to answer

- What do the best people you know have in common?
- Who are three examples?
- Why does that trait lead to the result?
- Who doesn't fit the pattern?

## How it fails

- One case called a pattern.
- A trait so broad that it fits everyone.
