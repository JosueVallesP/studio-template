# The gripe

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: the feeling of being seen.

## The idea

Something in your working world bothers you. Say it plainly and say why.

## The post in one sentence

[This] bothers me, because [reason].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers who feel the same see someone say it at last. A clear stance shows what you stand for.

## What you need

- A specific thing that bothers you.
- A reason beyond taste.
- Standing to complain.

## Questions to answer

- What made you angry at work this month?
- Why does it matter beyond your own annoyance?
- What would you rather see?

## How it fails

- A rant with no reason.
- You punch at people with less power than you.
