# One line that changed your work

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a sense of who you are. It also gives: something to use.

## The idea

Someone gave you a line or a question that changed how you work. Quote it and unpack it.

## The post in one sentence

[Person] told me "[line]," and here is what it means in practice.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The line is short enough to keep. Your unpacking shows how to use it.

## What you need

- The exact line and who said it.
- What it means in practice.
- A change you made because of it.

## Questions to answer

- What's the best advice you ever got?
- What does each part of it mean?
- What did you start doing differently?

## How it fails

- A famous quote with no link to your work.
- You repeat it and add nothing.
