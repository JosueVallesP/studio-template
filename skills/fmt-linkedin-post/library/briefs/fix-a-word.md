# Fix a word your field misuses

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind.

## The idea

People in your field use a word loosely or wrongly. Say what it means, or give a thing its true name.

## The post in one sentence

People say [word] and mean [loose meaning]. It means [your meaning].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A better definition changes what people do. Two terms set side by side are easy to remember and repeat.

## What you need

- A word people misuse.
- Your definition.
- An example that shows the difference.

## Questions to answer

- Which word do clients use that means something else to you?
- How do you define it?
- What does someone do differently with your definition?
- Where did the loose meaning come from?

## How it fails

- The argument changes nothing in practice.
- Your definition is as vague as theirs.
