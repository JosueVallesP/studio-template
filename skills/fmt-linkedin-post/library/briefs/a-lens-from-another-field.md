# A lens from another field

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: something to use. It also gives: a changed mind.

## The idea

Borrow a law or a model from another field and use it to explain yours.

## The post in one sentence

[Law or model from another field] explains [problem in yours].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The borrowed idea makes a familiar problem look new. It also shows your range.

## What you need

- A model you understand well.
- A real fit with your field.
- What it tells the reader to do.

## Questions to answer

- What idea from outside your field do you use at work?
- Where does it fit, and where does it break?
- What follows from it?

## How it fails

- A loose analogy.
- You've misread the source.
