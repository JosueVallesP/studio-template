# Criticize your own industry

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind. It also gives: proof you can do the work.

## The idea

Say what's wrong with your own industry, as someone who works in it.

## The post in one sentence

I work in [industry], and [this] is wrong with it.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Criticism costs an insider something, so readers believe it. Buyers learn what to watch for.

## What you need

- Standing in the industry.
- A specific problem.
- A way for a buyer to protect themselves.

## Questions to answer

- What does your industry get away with?
- Why does it last?
- What should a buyer ask before hiring someone like you?
- What does saying this cost you?

## How it fails

- It reads as an attack on rivals to sell yourself.
- No specifics.
