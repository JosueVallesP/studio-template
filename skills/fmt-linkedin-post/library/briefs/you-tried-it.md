# You tried it

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have data or ran a test. The reader leaves with: proof you can do the work. It also gives: a sense of who you are.

## The idea

You ran a small test on yourself, your team or your neighbors. Tell what you did and what happened.

## The post in one sentence

I wondered [question], so I [did this], and [this] happened.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The reader wonders what would happen. You went and found out.

## What you need

- A test with a clear setup.
- What happened.
- What you'll do next.

## Questions to answer

- What did you wonder about?
- What exactly did you do?
- What happened, and what surprised you?

## How it fails

- No clear result.
- A test designed to sell something.
