# What they said to you

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: the feeling of being seen.

## The idea

A client, a boss or a stranger said something that shows how they see your work. Quote it and answer it.

## The post in one sentence

A [client or boss] said "[line]." Here is what that shows.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The line is real, so the misunderstanding is real. Your peers have heard it too.

## What you need

- The line, word for word.
- What it reveals.
- What you wish they understood.

## Questions to answer

- What's the strangest thing a client asked you?
- What does it show about how they see your job?
- What's the true picture?

## How it fails

- A made-up quote.
- Contempt for the person who said it.
