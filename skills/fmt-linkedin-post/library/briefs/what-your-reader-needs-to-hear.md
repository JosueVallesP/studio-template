# What your reader needs to hear

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: the feeling of being seen.

## The idea

Your reader feels pressure to live or work a certain way. Tell them it's fine not to, and why.

## The post in one sentence

It's fine to [thing your reader feels they can't do], because [reason].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The pressure is real and rarely questioned. Relief is a strong reason to share a post.

## What you need

- A pressure your readers feel.
- A reason it's wrong or optional.
- Your own case, if you have one.

## Questions to answer

- What do your readers feel guilty about?
- Who set that standard?
- What's true for you?

## How it fails

- Comfort with no reason behind it.
- A pep talk any account could post.
