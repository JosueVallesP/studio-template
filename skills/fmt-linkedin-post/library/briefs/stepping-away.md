# Stepping away from what everyone uses

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have data or ran a test. The reader leaves with: a sense of who you are.

## The idea

You're stepping away from a tool or habit that everyone uses. Say why, and what you'll do in its place.

## The post in one sentence

I've stopped [using this], because [reason].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Many readers have thought about doing it. You did it and can report.

## What you need

- Something you used for real.
- The reason you're stopping.
- What replaces it.

## Questions to answer

- What have you stopped using?
- What did it cost you while you used it?
- What did you notice after?

## How it fails

- Preaching.
- You quit something you barely used.
