# You got what everyone wants

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a changed mind. It also gives: someone to look up to.

## The idea

You reached a goal your readers are chasing. Tell them what it's like there.

## The post in one sentence

I got [the thing people chase]. Here is what it's like.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Only someone who arrived can report back. The sober view saves readers from chasing the wrong thing.

## What you need

- A goal you reached, with proof.
- What surprised you.
- What you'd chase now.

## Questions to answer

- What did you get that you once wanted badly?
- How did it differ from what you expected?
- What would you tell someone still chasing it?

## How it fails

- A complaint about success.
- A pitch for your product where the honest report should be.
