# Your own model of how something works

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use.

## The idea

You've built your own way to see a problem: layers, circles, levels or a formula. Draw it in words.

## The post in one sentence

I think of [problem] as [number of parts], and here is what each part is.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A model gives the reader a tool for thinking that they can reuse. It carries your name when they pass it on.

## What you need

- A model you use in client work.
- A name for each part.
- One case where the model changes what someone should do.

## Questions to answer

- How do you explain this problem to a new client?
- What are the parts, and what do you call them?
- What does someone do differently once they see it this way?
- Where does the model stop working?

## How it fails

- The model renames things people already know.
- It has parts but no use.
- It reads like a formula made up for the post.
