# This week at work

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a read on what's happening. It also gives: proof you can do the work.

## The idea

Something happened in your work this week that shows a bigger problem or change.

## The post in one sentence

This week [this happened at work], and it shows [bigger thing].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A fresh scene proves you're in the work. It turns an opinion into a report.

## What you need

- A moment from a call, a meeting or a client file.
- The bigger thing it points to.

## Questions to answer

- What happened this week that surprised you?
- What was said or sent?
- What does it tell you about your field?

## How it fails

- The moment is invented or dressed up.
- The jump from the moment to the claim is too big.
