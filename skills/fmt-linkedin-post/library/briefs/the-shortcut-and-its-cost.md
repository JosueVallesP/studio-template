# The shortcut and its cost

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind.

## The idea

A shortcut is spreading in your field. Show what it skips and what that costs.

## The post in one sentence

People now skip [work] by using [shortcut], and it costs them [this].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers are tempted by the shortcut. You show the price before they pay it.

## What you need

- A shortcut you've seen used.
- The work it replaces.
- A case where it went wrong.

## Questions to answer

- What are people in your field skipping now?
- What did that work produce that the shortcut can't?
- Where have you seen it fail?
- What is the shortcut good for?

## How it fails

- Fear of new tools with no case behind it.
- You never grant what the shortcut is good for.
