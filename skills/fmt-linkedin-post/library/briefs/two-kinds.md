# Two kinds, and how to tell them apart

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: a changed mind. It also gives: something to use.

## The idea

Split the people in your field into two kinds and show the signs of each.

## The post in one sentence

There are two kinds of [people in your field], and you can tell them apart by [signs].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers place themselves. The signs make the difference visible in daily behavior.

## What you need

- A real divide you've seen.
- Signs of each side, taken from things people say or do.

## Questions to answer

- What separates the people who do this well from the ones who don't?
- What does each side say in a meeting?
- Which side do you stand on, and why?
- Which side were you on once?

## How it fails

- One side is a cartoon.
- The signs are opinions, with no behavior the reader can spot.
