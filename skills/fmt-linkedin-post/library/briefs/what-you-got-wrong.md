# What you got wrong for years

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a sense of who you are. It also gives: something to use.

## The idea

Admit something you did wrong for years, and say what you do now.

## The post in one sentence

For [how long] I [did this]. It cost me [this], and now I [do this].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers making the same mistake listen to someone who made it. The admission costs you a little, which makes it credible.

## What you need

- A real mistake with a cost.
- Why you made it.
- The change.

## Questions to answer

- What did you do for years that you'd never do now?
- What did it cost?
- What made you stop?

## How it fails

- A humble brag.
- A mistake with no cost.
