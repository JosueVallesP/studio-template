# The question you get most

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use.

## The idea

People keep asking you the same question. Answer it once, in full.

## The post in one sentence

The question I get most is [question], and here is my full answer.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The question is proven demand. Your answer shows how you think, and you can point people to it for years.

## What you need

- A question you hear often.
- An honest answer, even when the honest answer is "I don't."

## Questions to answer

- What do people ask you most?
- What do you tell them in private?
- Which part of your answer surprises them?
- Who asks it, and what do they hope you'll say?

## How it fails

- Nobody asks the question.
- You give the safe answer.
