# Someone should build this

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you see where things are going. The reader leaves with: something to use. It also gives: a read on what's happening.

## The idea

You see something nobody is doing. Describe it and how you'd do it.

## The post in one sentence

Someone should build [this] for [who].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers hunt for ideas. A concrete one with first steps gets saved and passed on.

## What you need

- A gap you've seen first-hand.
- Who it's for.
- The first steps.

## Questions to answer

- What do you wish existed?
- Who would pay for it?
- How would you start?

## How it fails

- An idea with no buyer.
- A plug in disguise.
