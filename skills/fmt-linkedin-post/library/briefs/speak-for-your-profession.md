# Speak for your profession

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: the feeling of being seen.

## The idea

People outside your job keep judging it wrongly. Tell them what they get wrong, on behalf of everyone who does the job.

## The post in one sentence

To [outsiders]: here is what you get wrong about [my job].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Your peers share it because it says what they can't say to their boss. The people you address read it because you named them.

## What you need

- Standing in the profession.
- The exact things outsiders say or ask for.
- For the stronger version, a volume of recent conversations.

## Questions to answer

- Who tells you how to do your job?
- What exactly do they ask for?
- What does it cost?
- What should they do in its place?

## How it fails

- A complaint without specifics.
- You never tell the outsider what to do.

## Variations

You can also speak for the people you serve, such as a client who was treated unfairly (L132).
