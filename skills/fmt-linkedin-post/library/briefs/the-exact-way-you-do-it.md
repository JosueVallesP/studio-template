# The exact way you do one thing

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use. It also gives: proof you can do the work.

## The idea

You do one task well and often. Show the steps in the order you run them.

## The post in one sentence

Here is how I [do one task], which got [result].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A reader can copy a procedure on Monday. The detail also proves you do the work yourself.

## What you need

- A task you've repeated enough to have a fixed way.
- A result that shows the way works.
- The small details that people usually skip.

## Questions to answer

- What do you do the same way every time?
- What result does it get?
- Which step do people skip, and what happens when they do?
- What does the reader need to have before the first step?

## How it fails

- The steps are generic and anyone could have written them.
- No result backs the method.
- The steps could run in any order, which makes it a list of tips.
