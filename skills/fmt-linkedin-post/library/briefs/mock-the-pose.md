# Mock a pose your field keeps striking

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: the feeling of being seen.

## The idea

Your field keeps striking a pose. Mock it by writing the honest version or by playing it straight.

## The post in one sentence

The honest version of [pose] would say [this].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Everyone has seen the pose and nobody has said so. The joke makes the point without a lecture.

## What you need

- A pose people will recognize in one line.
- A sense of what the honest version would say.

## Questions to answer

- What do people in your field perform?
- What would the honest version say?
- Can you stay in character to the end?

## How it fails

- You target a person where you should target a behavior.
- You explain the joke.
