# A list worth keeping

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: something to use.

## The idea

Hand over a list of things outside the post: people, books, tools, things worth the money.

## The post in one sentence

Here are the [number] [things] I'd point you to.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The reader leaves with something to go and get. People save lists.

## What you need

- Items you've used or vetted.
- A reason for each one, or a clear standard for the list.

## Questions to answer

- What do people ask you to recommend?
- What's on your short list?
- Why each one?

## How it fails

- A list you didn't vet.
- No standard for what made the cut.
