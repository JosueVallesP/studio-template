# A known person's odd decision

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: someone to look up to. It also gives: a changed mind.

## The idea

A known person made a choice that looked strange. Tell it, show what came of it, and tie it to a principle you hold.

## The post in one sentence

[Person] did [strange thing], and [this] happened.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The odd choice opens a question and the result closes it. The reader keeps the principle because the story carried it.

## What you need

- A true story you can check.
- The odd choice, the pushback and the result.
- A principle of yours that it supports.

## Questions to answer

- What story do you retell to make a point?
- What was strange about the choice?
- What result followed, with numbers?
- What do you believe that this shows?

## How it fails

- The story is unverified.
- The principle doesn't follow from the story.
- Everyone has heard the story.
