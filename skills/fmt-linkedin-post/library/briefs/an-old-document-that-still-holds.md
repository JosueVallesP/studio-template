# An old document that still holds

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: something to use.

## The idea

Show a real memo, letter, test or ad from your field's past and let it speak.

## The post in one sentence

In [year], [who] wrote [document]. Here it is.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The reader sees the thing itself. Its age shows that the idea lasts.

## What you need

- A real document you can quote word for word.
- Who wrote it, when, and one striking fact about it.

## Questions to answer

- What old document from your field do you keep?
- What makes it worth seeing?
- Why does it still hold?

## How it fails

- You paraphrase where you should quote.
- You can't verify it.
