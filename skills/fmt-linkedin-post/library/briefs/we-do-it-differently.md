# We do it differently

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have data or ran a test. The reader leaves with: proof you can do the work.

## The idea

Your company does something against the standard playbook. Show it with the numbers.

## The post in one sentence

We [do this against the norm], and here are the numbers.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The numbers prove the other way works. Readers see that they have a choice.

## What you need

- A practice that goes against the norm.
- Results.
- The reasoning.

## Questions to answer

- What do you do that others in your market don't?
- What numbers back it?
- What does it cost you?

## How it fails

- A brag with no reasoning.
- The practice is common.
