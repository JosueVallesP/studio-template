# What used to win and doesn't now

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind. It also gives: a read on what's happening.

## The idea

Something that worked in your field no longer does. Say what changed and what works now.

## The post in one sentence

[This] used to work. It stopped because [change], and [this] works now.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers feel their results dropping and don't know why. You explain it and give them the next move.

## What you need

- A before and after you lived through.
- The cause of the change.
- What you do now.

## Questions to answer

- What worked three years ago that doesn't now?
- What caused that?
- What replaced it in your own work?
- What shows that it stopped working?

## How it fails

- Nostalgia with no next move.
- You claim the change and show no sign of it.
