# A belief your experience contradicts

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind.

## The idea

Your field repeats a piece of advice. Your experience says otherwise. Say so and show the evidence.

## The post in one sentence

Everyone says [advice]. In my experience, [what you've seen].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The reader holds the belief, so the post is about them. Your evidence gives them a reason to change their mind.

## What you need

- A belief people state out loud.
- Evidence of your own: cases, numbers, years.
- What to do in its place.

## Questions to answer

- What advice in your field makes you wince?
- What have you seen that contradicts it?
- When is the advice right?
- Who gives this advice, and why does it sound right?

## How it fails

- Nobody holds the belief.
- The evidence is one anecdote, stretched.
- You never say where the advice holds.
