# If I were in your seat today

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use.

## The idea

Say what you would do today if you were in the reader's position, move by move.

## The post in one sentence

If I were [in this seat] today, here is what I'd do.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

It turns your experience into a plan for one specific person. The frame lets you be direct without lecturing.

## What you need

- Real experience of that seat, or of advising people in it.
- Specific moves, in order.

## Questions to answer

- Whose seat can you speak to?
- What would you do first, second and third?
- What would you skip that people think is required?
- What has changed since you were last in that seat?

## How it fails

- You've never been near that seat.
- The moves are vague.
