# The calm read on the hype

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a read on what's happening.

## The idea

Everyone is excited or scared about something new. Give the calm read.

## The post in one sentence

Everyone is loud about [thing]. Here is what I expect to happen.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers are tired of loud takes. A steady voice with reasons stands out.

## What you need

- A hyped thing.
- Reasons for your doubt or calm.
- What you expect to happen.

## Questions to answer

- What is your field overreacting to?
- What do the loud voices leave out?
- What do you think happens in a year?
- What would change your mind?

## How it fails

- Cynicism with no reasons.
- You dismiss the thing without knowing it.
