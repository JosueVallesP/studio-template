# Outside evidence for what you've been saying

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something is in the news. The reader leaves with: a read on what's happening. It also gives: proof you can do the work.

## The idea

A study, a report or a respected voice confirms what you've been saying. Show it.

## The post in one sentence

[Source] found [finding], which backs [what I've been saying].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A third party carries weight that your own word can't. It tells your followers they backed the right view.

## What you need

- A real source you can cite.
- The finding in plain numbers.
- Your point, stated before or after.

## Questions to answer

- What have you been saying for a while?
- What new evidence supports it?
- What does the evidence leave open?

## How it fails

- You stretch the finding.
- No link or source.
