# A small scene from outside work

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a sense of who you are.

## The idea

Something small happened with your family, on a flight or at dinner. Tell it and say what it showed you.

## The post in one sentence

[Small thing happened], and it made me think [this].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A small true scene is easy to read and easy to feel. It shows who you are outside work.

## What you need

- A scene with detail.
- One thought it gave you.

## Questions to answer

- What small moment stayed with you this week?
- What did you see or hear, exactly?
- What did it make you think?

## How it fails

- A diary entry with no thought.
- A forced business lesson.
