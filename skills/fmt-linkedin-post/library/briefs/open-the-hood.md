# Open the hood on your setup

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use. It also gives: proof you can do the work.

## The idea

Show the real setup behind your work: the tools, the spend, how a new tool fits into your week.

## The post in one sentence

Here is the exact [stack, spend or week] behind [result or role].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

People want to see what someone in your seat uses, with real names and numbers. Specifics like these are rare, so readers save them.

## What you need

- A setup you run today.
- Names, prices or counts.
- The job each piece does for you.

## Questions to answer

- What does your stack or budget look like, line by line?
- What did you build or automate last week?
- What changed in your work because of it?
- What did you try and drop?

## How it fails

- A list of tool names with no job next to each.
- A claim of results with nothing shown.
- A product plug dressed up as a setup.
