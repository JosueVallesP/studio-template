# Someone worth knowing

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: someone to look up to.

## The idea

Introduce a person your readers haven't heard of, or the unknown early years of one they have.

## The post in one sentence

You should know about [person], who [path in one line].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A whole path, told fast, shows how success gets built. Readers like to discover someone.

## What you need

- A person with a path worth telling.
- Facts you can check, in order.
- What you take from it.

## Questions to answer

- Who do you admire that your readers don't know?
- What did they do before the part everyone knows?
- What does their path show?

## How it fails

- A list of achievements.
- No view of your own.
