# Your worst stretch and what it built

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a sense of who you are. It also gives: someone to look up to.

## The idea

Tell the worst stretch of your working life and what it built.

## The post in one sentence

In [year] I was at [low point]. Here is what I did next.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers in their own bad stretch see a way through. The detail earns a trust that polish can't.

## What you need

- A hard period with specifics: money, dates, what you did next.
- What you carry from it.

## Questions to answer

- What was your worst year?
- What did you do the week after?
- What do you do today because of it?
- What detail from that time do you still remember exactly?

## How it fails

- The low point is mild.
- The lesson is bigger than the story supports.
