# One small fix for today

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use.

## The idea

Give one small thing the reader can do in a few minutes, with a before and an after.

## The post in one sentence

Do [this small thing] and [this] gets better in [this many minutes].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Trying it costs so little that people do it, and a fast result earns trust.

## What you need

- A fix you use yourself.
- A before-and-after example.
- How long it takes.

## Questions to answer

- What's the quickest improvement you know?
- What does it look like before and after?
- How does the reader check that it worked?

## How it fails

- The fix is too big for one sitting.
- You give no example.
