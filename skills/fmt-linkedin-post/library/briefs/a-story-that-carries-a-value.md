# A story that carries a value

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a sense of who you are.

## The idea

Tell a story from your life that shows something you believe.

## The post in one sentence

[When], [this happened], and it showed me [value].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A story lets the reader reach the value themselves. They remember the scene long after the lesson.

## What you need

- A real event with a turn in it.
- A value it shows.
- Restraint about the moral.

## Questions to answer

- What story do you tell friends more than once?
- What changed in it?
- What does it show about how you work or live?
- Where does the story turn?

## How it fails

- The story has no turn.
- The moral is bolted on.
- The story serves a product pitch.

## Variations

This includes the time you were in the room for something well known (L005).
