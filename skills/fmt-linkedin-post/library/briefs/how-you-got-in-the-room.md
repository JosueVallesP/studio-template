# How you got in the room

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: someone to look up to. It also gives: something to use.

## The idea

Tell the unusual way you reached a person or a room that seemed out of reach.

## The post in one sentence

I reached [person or room] by [doing this].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers want access too. A move that worked once, told with detail, feels possible.

## What you need

- A specific person or room.
- What you did.
- What came of it.

## Questions to answer

- Who did you reach that you had no business reaching?
- What exactly did you send, say or do?
- What would you tell someone to copy?

## How it fails

- The access came from luck or connections that you leave out.
- You give no detail of the move.
