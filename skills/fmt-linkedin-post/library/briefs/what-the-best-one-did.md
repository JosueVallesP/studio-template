# What the best one did differently

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use. It also gives: someone to look up to.

## The idea

Think of the best person you managed or worked with. Show what they did that others didn't.

## The post in one sentence

The best [role] I worked with got [result] by doing [this] differently.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The reader gets a model to copy, and the result that person produced is the proof.

## What you need

- One standout person with a result you can state.
- Their specific behaviors, set next to what average people do.

## Questions to answer

- Who was the best you've seen at this job?
- What did they do in a normal week that others skipped?
- What did it produce?
- Can you name them, or do you need to keep them anonymous?

## How it fails

- Praise with no behaviors.
- Traits ("she was driven") where the post needs actions.
