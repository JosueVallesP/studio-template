# How it really is

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind.

## The idea

Outsiders hold a picture of a job, a market or a way of working. Show them how it is, with numbers or details.

## The post in one sentence

People picture [X]. Here is how it is: [facts].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The gap between the picture and the facts is the surprise. Detail from inside settles it.

## What you need

- A common picture.
- Facts or first-hand detail that contradict it.

## Questions to answer

- What do people assume about your job or market?
- What numbers or details would surprise them?
- Why does the wrong picture last?
- Where do your facts come from?

## How it fails

- Nobody holds the picture.
- The facts have no source.
