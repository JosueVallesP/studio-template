# Announce it by the gap it fills

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have something to launch. The reader leaves with: proof you can do the work.

## The idea

You're launching something. Lead with the gap it fills and why you're the one to fill it.

## The post in one sentence

I'm launching [thing] because [gap I saw].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers care about the problem before the product. Your history with the problem is the proof.

## What you need

- Something real to announce.
- The gap, from your own experience.
- Who it's for and what to do next.

## Questions to answer

- What are you launching?
- What did you see that made you build it?
- Who is it for, and who is it not for?

## How it fails

- Features come first.
- No ask at the end.
- Hype with nothing shipped.
