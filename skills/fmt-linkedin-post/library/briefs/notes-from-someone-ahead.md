# Notes from someone ahead of you

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: something to use.

## The idea

You spent time with someone ahead of you, or with their thinking. Share what you took from it.

## The post in one sentence

I spent [time] with [person or source]. Here is what I took from it.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The reader gets access they don't have. The other person's standing carries the proof.

## What you need

- Real access: a conversation, a trip, a talk, a report.
- Takeaways in your own words.
- Your read on what matters most.

## Questions to answer

- Who did you learn from this month?
- What did they say that changed your mind?
- What will you do with it?
- What did you disagree with?

## How it fails

- A summary with no judgment from you.
- The access is thin and the takeaways are generic.
