# A rule you keep

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: a sense of who you are. It also gives: something to use.

## The idea

You hold a rule in your work. State it and tell where it came from.

## The post in one sentence

My rule is [rule], and it started when [moment].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A rule is easy to remember and copy. The story behind it shows you earned it.

## What you need

- A rule you follow.
- The moment or reason that created it.
- What it costs you to keep.

## Questions to answer

- What do you never do, or always do?
- When did that start?
- What does keeping it cost?

## How it fails

- A rule you don't keep.
- A rule with no cost.
