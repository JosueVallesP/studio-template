# An unresolved tension in your field

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind.

## The idea

Your field wants two things that pull against each other. Name the tension and say where you stand.

## The post in one sentence

My field wants [A] and [B], and they pull against each other.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

People in the field live with it daily and rarely see it named. An open question draws real replies.

## What you need

- Two demands that conflict.
- Your experience of both.
- An honest position, even an unfinished one.

## Questions to answer

- What two things does your work demand that fight each other?
- Where have you felt it?
- How do you handle it today?

## How it fails

- The tension is fake and the answer is obvious.
- You take no position.
