# Work you admire, taken apart

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you read, heard or saw something. The reader leaves with: something to use. It also gives: proof you can do the work.

## The idea

Pick one piece of work you admire and explain why it works.

## The post in one sentence

[This piece of work] works because of [these parts].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers learn to see what you see. Your taste becomes visible.

## What you need

- A specific piece: a campaign, an email, a logo, an act.
- The parts that make it work.

## Questions to answer

- What made you stop and look this month?
- What exactly did they do?
- What would a typical team have done?

## How it fails

- Praise with no analysis.
- You pick something everyone already praised.
