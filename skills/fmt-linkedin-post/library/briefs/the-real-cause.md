# The real cause of a common complaint

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you disagree with something. The reader leaves with: a changed mind. It also gives: something to use.

## The idea

People complain about a problem and blame the wrong thing. Name the real cause.

## The post in one sentence

People blame [X] for [complaint]. The cause is [Y].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

The reader arrives with the complaint. You move the blame to something they can fix.

## What you need

- A complaint you hear often.
- The cause you find when you look closer.
- The fix.

## Questions to answer

- What do clients complain about in the first call?
- What do you find when you dig?
- What do they need to change?
- What do people blame?
- How do you know the cause is real? Give a case or a count.

## How it fails

- The real cause is as vague as the complaint.
- You blame the reader and offer no fix.
