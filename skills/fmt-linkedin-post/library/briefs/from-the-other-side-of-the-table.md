# From the other side of the table

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have years of practice. The reader leaves with: something to use. It also gives: a changed mind.

## The idea

You've sat where your reader's buyer, boss or loved one sits. Tell them what it looks like from there.

## The post in one sentence

As [the person on the other side], here is what I see when you [do the thing].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers can't see themselves from the other chair. You can give them that view.

## What you need

- Time spent on the other side.
- What you ignored, what you noticed and what worked on you.

## Questions to answer

- What seat have you held that your readers are trying to reach or sell to?
- What do people send or say that you never look at?
- What did work on you?
- What did you believe before you switched seats?

## How it fails

- You guess at the other side.
- You complain and give the reader nothing to change.
