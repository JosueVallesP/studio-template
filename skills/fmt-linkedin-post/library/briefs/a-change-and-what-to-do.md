# A change, and what to do about it

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something is in the news. The reader leaves with: a read on what's happening. It also gives: something to use.

## The idea

One event or change shows where things are heading. Say what it means and what to do.

## The post in one sentence

[This changed]. It means [your read], so do [this].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Readers want to know if they need to act. You give them the meaning and the move.

## What you need

- A specific change: a hire, a launch, a rule, a platform update.
- Your read.
- A next step.

## Questions to answer

- What changed?
- What does it tell you about where things go?
- What should your reader do this month?

## How it fails

- A big forecast from one small event.
- No next step.
