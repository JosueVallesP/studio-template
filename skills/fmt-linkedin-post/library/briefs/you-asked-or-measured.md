# You asked or measured

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: you have data or ran a test. The reader leaves with: proof you can do the work. It also gives: a read on what's happening.

## The idea

You surveyed people or looked at your own numbers. Report what came back.

## The post in one sentence

We asked [number of people], or measured [what], and found [finding].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

Nobody else has your data. A finding beats an opinion.

## What you need

- A real sample and its size.
- One finding that surprises.
- What to do about it.

## Questions to answer

- What data do you have that others don't?
- What surprised you in it?
- What should someone change because of it?
- How did you collect it?

## How it fails

- A tiny sample presented as fact.
- Findings with no consequence for the reader.
