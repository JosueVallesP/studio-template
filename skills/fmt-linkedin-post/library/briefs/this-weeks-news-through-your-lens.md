# This week's news through your lens

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something is in the news. The reader leaves with: a read on what's happening.

## The idea

Something happened this week. Explain it with what you know from your field.

## The post in one sentence

[This happened]. Here is what it shows about [your field].

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

People are already talking about it. Your lens tells them something the headlines don't.

## What you need

- A fresh event.
- Knowledge that explains it.
- A point that outlasts the news.

## Questions to answer

- What happened this week that touches your field?
- What do you see that reporters miss?
- What principle does it show?

## How it fails

- You repeat the news.
- The link to your field is forced.
