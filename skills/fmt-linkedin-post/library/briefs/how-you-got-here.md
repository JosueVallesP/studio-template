# How you got here, year by year

Part of [[idea-briefs|Idea briefs]]. Start here when: something happened to you. The reader leaves with: someone to look up to. It also gives: a sense of who you are.

## The idea

Lay out your path year by year, with the failures left in.

## The post in one sentence

[Year]: [what happened], for each year from the start until today.

This says what you hand the reader and why it comes from you. If you can't fill it in yet, gather more material before you write.

## Why it works

A timeline compresses years into a minute. The bad years make the good ones believable.

## What you need

- Dates and numbers.
- At least a few real setbacks.

## Questions to answer

- What happened each year?
- Which years went wrong?
- What does the whole path show?

## How it fails

- A list of wins.
- No setbacks.
