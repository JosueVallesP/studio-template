## Post

When is a Ferrari NOT a Ferrari? 

Surely if a car is made by Ferrari and has the Ferrari badge on it, it must be a Ferrari?

Apparently not. In the last 24 hours millions of fans of the brand have taken to social media to protest that the new Ferrari Luce absolutely doesn't fit with their perception of what a Ferrari is. 

If the Luce was launched by VW, Toyota or even Apple there would not have been any uproar. The opposite could have happened - people might have championed the car as an absolute triumph with almost any other marque emblazoned on it. "Wow the new Volvo Luce looks incredible!" is something I can totally imagine someone saying. 

So what is it about this release that grates against people sensibilities? 

In my opinion the essence of the Ferrari brand is "the fulfilment of a childhood dream". In order for a Ferrari to be real you have to imagine it on a poster hanging proudly above the bed of a 12 year old boy. 

You have to imagine a pair of teenagers craning their necks and pressing their heads against the window as a Ferrari overtakes their family car on the highway. 

A Ferrari needs to look like the well dressed cousin of an F1 car. Anything less than this and you've lost the essence of what people expect from the brand. 

Every business has an unspoken essence. Your customers expect you to be something or embody something. If you do it well they love you for it but if you stray from those expectations all hell can break lose. 

Ferrari has almost flawlessly lived up to expectations for the best part of a century now. People are upset because they love the brand, what they think it stands for and what they feel it says about them. All of that comes undone when they see something that so clearly doesn't match expectations.
