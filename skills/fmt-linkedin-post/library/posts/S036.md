## Post

12 things I believe about building high-performing leadership teams:

1. When your team frustrates you, assume the problem is you.
 
2. As a leader, your #1 job is to unlock performance. That's it. Not to be liked, not to be needed, and definitely not to have every answer. Go through your team one by one and ask: what does this person need to be at their best? Am I giving it?

3. Almost every scale-up problem is a clarity problem. Can everyone name the priorities, what success looks like, and who owns what? Repeat the answers until you are bored.

4. Every yes costs something. 

5. You should hire and take advice from people unlike you.

6. Delegating is NOT disappearing. Tell every senior hire when you will get hands-on, before you do it.

7. 3 days a month spent planning prevents about 15 days of firefighting.
 
8. Do not move up to vision and outside relationships until your leadership team can run the company without you.
 
9. Meet your leaders weekly, because habits revert within a month.

10. Keep your moods off the team. Period.

11. Hire senior help right after you raise, not a year later when the problems show.

12. Building a leadership team is performance management, not a wellbeing service. Let people who will not grow with the company go quickly.
