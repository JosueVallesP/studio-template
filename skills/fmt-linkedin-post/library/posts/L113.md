## Post

I'm incredibly bullish on IRL

Intimate dinner parties. Music festivals. Group adventures. Camping trips. Stargazing. Saunas. Sunrise hikes. Game nights. Book clubs.

The pendulum will swing back to craftsmanship, curation, texture, rituals, long conversations, and undivided attention.

The more AI takes over, the more we will hunger for what it cannot give us: real humanity.

Nothing will ever replace the experience of breaking bread with other people.
