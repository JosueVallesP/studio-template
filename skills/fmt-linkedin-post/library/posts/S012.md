## Post

There’s a panic attack you can’t see in this photo.

Yesterday, Jenny and I flew from Santa Barbara to Denver. 

About 10 minutes into the flight, I saw Jenny wave down a flight attendant with more urgency than she usually lives with. The woman sitting next to her was having a panic attack and didn’t think she could survive the rest of the 2-hour flight. 

Jenny jumped into action and compassion in a way that reminded me what a lucky person I am to know her never mind be married to her. 

She got the woman a bag of ice to help her physically reset her vagus nerve, got her medicine from her carry-on bag overhead and then talked to her for two hours straight. 

Hands and feet of Jesus. 

The amount of love Jenny poured out on this complete stranger who was terrified was amazing to watch from across the aisle. 

I get to be on stage. I’m the one who has the Instagram following, my name is on books and a small handful of people think I’m neat. But the truth is, even though most of the time you don’t see it, Jenny is the real rockstar at the Acuff house. 

I don’t know what mom and dad were praying for their daughter yesterday morning, but their prayers were answered. 

She sat next to Jenny Acuff.
