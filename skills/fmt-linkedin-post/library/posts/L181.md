## Post

I hear this so often from founders and CEOs...

They look at their People team and say: "Fix the culture." 

But I mean, let's be honest: that's just not how it works.

I've made plenty of hiring mistakes myself, and if there’s one thing I’ve learned, it’s that you can’t delegate the "soul" of your company. 

You can build the best systems in the world, but if the leadership isn’t walking the talk, it’s all just noise.

Here's what HR can do:

- Build great hiring systems
- Design fair compensation
- Create growth programs
- Measure engagement
- Train managers
- Track performance

Here's what HR can't do:

- Force executives to listen
- Make leaders show up
- Change toxic behaviors
- Fix broken promises
- Repair lost trust
- Override bad examples

The best HR team in the world can't outwork a CEO who doesn't care.
