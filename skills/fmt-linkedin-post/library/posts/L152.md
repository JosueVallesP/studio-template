## Post

When I had to restart my show, I reached out to about 150 people asking for help. 148 said yes. Then there was this one dude....

This guy's a big podcaster and someone I had considered a casual friend. 

His response was essentially:

"You're not doing anything for me anymore, so I have no reason to help you."

Not even dressed up as a polite excuse. Just pure transactional honesty.

At first I was shocked. Then I started telling people the story.

Almost everyone had their own version of this with the SAME GUY.

They'd spent hours helping him with a launch, proposal, introduction, presentation, whatever... Then later asked for one small favor and got the same answer.

The relationship had served its purpose (for him) and he was done.

I used to be angry about it. Now I mostly feel sorry for the guy.

He might be successful, but his reputation sucks. Those hundreds of tiny interactions stacked up.

Here's the lesson in all this. 

If someone helped you, help them back.

If you can't, be gracious.

The bar isn't very high.

People remember who showed up when there was nothing obvious in it for them. And they definitely remember who decided the relationship no longer had any ROI.
