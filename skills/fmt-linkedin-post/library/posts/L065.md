## Post

Cancelling a meeting with someone on your team is one of the most disrespectful things a leader can do.

So my rule is simple:

Put the meeting in your diary and don't cancel it. Keep it light if you want to. You don't need an agenda or a deck. Just show up.

If you genuinely have to move it, move it inside the same week. Not "I'll come back to you with a new time." 

And the person you cancelled on should be the first person you reschedule, not the last.

Your team isn't going to read your strategy memo to figure out if they matter to you. 

They're going to look at your calendar.
