## Post

In 2026, the race will split into two categories.  

VC Backed AI Native:
- Expected hyper growth.  Growth ain't a goal.  It's a commandment.
- Experimental ARR facing GRR collapse.  Explosive on a chart.  Fragile IRL.
- Gross margins that are “fine for now” and hand-waved away as a future problem.
- Dependent on hype cycle and enterprise enthusiasm
- Small chance at $1B ARR
- Race to get to exit or secondary before you have to prove the business model
- VC investors whipping you into a frenzy 
- "Unicorns are dead.  I'm all in on SuperNovas (or a new moniker my team came up with last night)
- Brutal intensity but maybe you'll end up like Fyxer, Manus, Elyse, Lovable and get to the finish line
- Expected ARR Growth: 
-- 0-$100M - 300%
-- $100M+ - 200%
- Time Horizon: Get out ASAP before the music stops
- "This time it's different"

Non-AI SaaS / Everyone Else (including me):
- Growth that’s intentional, modeled, and meant to survive contact with reality
- ARR that compounds quietly, supported by actual customers and real CS motion
- Goal is Rule of 40-60
- Gross margins you can explain without squinting (65–90% and proud of it)
- Iced out of the hype cycle
- No meaningful chance at $1B ARR
- Long term focus
- "Long term games with long term partners"
- Slow and steady wins this particular race (which isn't the same race the AI teams are running)
- Expected ARR Growth:
-- 0-$100M - 40%
-- $100M+ - 20%
- Time Horizon: 10+ years
- "I'll just keep building a good business. (And sleep at night)"

If you're in the first category, enjoy the ride.  It might burn you out.  But you also might win.  

If you're in the second category, find your composure and design your business around steady compounding every day.

Personally, I'm in the second category.

Community is the antithesis of AI native.  

It's real relationships
Real people.
And real experiences.

It doesn't respond well to hyper growth pressure (and often breaks when forced).

It does respond well to a long term steady vision, a focus on human connection and meaning, and an emphasis on craft.  

There's no judgement.  

We all have to grow where we're planted.

And mine is one that builds over years and decades vs quarters.  

What game are you going to play this year?
