## Post

Everyone is doubling down on AI. And most everyone hates the results.

We used Wynter to survey 50 B2B mid-market marketing leaders. 56% are increasing their AI budget in Q1.

But when you read the fine print, the mood isn't excitement. It’s frustration.

Actual quotes from the survey:

"Often AI turns out slop."
"My team is just bumbling through it."
"It's all garbage."

The disconnect is massive.

We have AI SDRs sending robot emails. AI content that sophisticated buyers smell instantly. AI ad variations that are uninspiring.

The problem isn't the technology. The problem is that we are trying to automate the thinking instead of the grunt work.

B2B buyers have excellent BS detectors. Your AI-generated email might save you 20 minutes. But if it costs you the deal, you optimized for the wrong thing.

Efficiency at the cost of effectiveness is just a faster way to fail.

Use AI to execute. Do not use it to think for you.
