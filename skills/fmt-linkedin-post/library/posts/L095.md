## Post

I once hired an agency to make a funny video ad for Ahrefs.

They sent me a script. I read it and thought: "this doesn't seem funny."

Their response?

"Tim, this is American humor. Trust us, the actors will improvise on set and it'll be way funnier than the script."

It wasn't.

The resulting video they sent me was "okay" at best. And in the end they never even completed the project (but that's a whole different story).

So I never got my funny Ahrefs video. But I never stopped wanting one.

A few months ago I stumbled on the work of Deadpan 💀 🎬 — a B2B agency that specializes in funny ad videos. 

And I immediately invited the founder, Luke Winter, on the Ahrefs Podcast to pick his brain on that topic. What followed was half discovery call, half masterclass on how funny B2B videos are actually made.

Here's what we covered:

🔸 Why humor is the cheapest way to stand out in a LinkedIn
🔸 Why you should optimize jokes for recognition, not cleverness
🔸 How to make your video instantly recognizable (mascots, thought leaders, visual identity, etc)
🔸 How one comedy campaign closed a "whale client" within weeks
🔸 Why a bad video won't actually damage your brand
🔸 Why ChatGPT will never write a funny script for you
🔸 Why relying on improv means the script wasn't good enough.
