## Post

Fuck it, let’s announce something big - I’ve built a marketing course. 

For over a year now I have been quietly planning a course bringing together my experience as a strategist, a lecturer, and yes, a creator.

There is something big missing from marketing education. 

A gap between knowing the fundamentals - which haven’t changed - and what the reality is actually like out there for a marketer today.

The viability of traditional linear ‘campaign-thinking’ is being obliterated by ever-expanding channel mixes, ever-increasing creative output, and the requirement to work through armies of mediators: influencers, partnerships, community activations, collaborations.

The New Fundamentals of Brand is my answer: a systems-level approach to brand that teaches us how to orchestrate coherent outcomes in situations where we no longer have total control. 

Throughout May, along with my co-founder Gabrielle Grist we ran a pilot cohort of 50 brilliant students spanning 10 countries, agency and client-side, junior to very senior, independent outfits up to multi-billion dollar corporations. Sidenote, I am so very proud of you all. 

We will be taking these accumulated learnings onboard to a full launch of both a B2C and B2B offer in the second half of the year (we already have some flagship partners which will be announced in due course). 

If you’d like to be added to the waitlist, you can find more info in the comments. Or DM me if you're interested in putting your business through it.

It’s a bloody mess out there. Let’s make some sense of it.
