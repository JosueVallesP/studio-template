## Post

I’m actually embarrassed to admit this…

I think the advice I’m giving gets mixed up sometimes.

It’s my belief that in order for your personal brand to stand out, you need to have a contrarian belief.

An opinion, thought, or action that is different from what everyone else in your space is doing.

But here’s the thing, most people think that means I’m telling them to be controversial.
There’s a massive difference here.

Contrarian = going against a dominant belief because you genuinely see the world differently. This is grounded in how you actually think.

Controversial = taking a position designed to provoke, shock, or create friction. The intent is the reaction, not the truth.

Anyone can say wild shit that gets people to stop and react.

But if that’s what you build your brand around.. you create something you can't sustain.
You just end up with content that doesn't reflect how you think or what you actually believe.

Aim to be contrarian, NOT controversial.

(if your contrarian take ends up being controversial for some, that’s fine…the key is what you’re optimizing for)

Hope this clears things up.
