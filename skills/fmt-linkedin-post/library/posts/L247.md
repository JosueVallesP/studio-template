## Post

Fix your LinkedIn in under 20 minutes

All you need is Claude.

1/ Download the profile PDF

Your profile feels different to your audience than it does to you. Having Claude look at the PDF gives you a chance to see it the way they do.

2/ Upload it into Claude

Everyone has a different goal. Don't tailor your profile for recruiters if you’re posting to find investors.

Upload the PDF with this prompt: "I want to optimize my profile to [insert your goal]. Assess: Headline, About, Banner, Featured. Tell me what's clear, what's missing, and how to improve each section."

3/ Define the "why you"

Find your answer to 3 questions: who you help → what you solve → why people should trust you.

Likes and views won't matter if you're reaching the wrong audience.

4/ Rewrite your About section

Your profile is less about you and more about the people you help or want to help. Use this prompt:

"Rewrite my About section so it focuses on who I help and what outcome I create. Keep it skimmable. End with a CTA to [insert your offer]."

I keep updating mine as my goals evolve.

Add your personality here! No boring AI slop!

5/ Check your proof gaps

Your headline, about section, and banner can pull people in, but they won't convert unless they show real proof.

Ask Claude: "What proof would make this profile more trustworthy?" Then add details like client results, specific numbers, stories etc.

6/ Find patterns in your winners

Your top posts can tell you exactly what's working, but they won't help you grow unless you pull the patterns out. Go to "Show all analytics" → "Impressions" → choose the timeline → "Export"

Upload the PDF with this prompt: "Identify the top performing patterns in my content. The top performing hooks, formats, topics, and writing style."

7/ Understand your audience

Your best posts address the common objections and frustrations your audience has. Talk to your target audience, record the calls and upload the transcripts to Claude with this:

"Review these call transcripts and identify the 20 most common pain points. Focus on the top frustrations and fears that keep them from buying, their unmet needs and recurring patterns."

8/ Turn the insights into content

The hard part of writing is figuring out what to write. Once the patterns from your analytics are clear, that work is already done. Start writing with this prompt:

"Based on my top performing format + hook + style, help me write a LinkedIn post on [insert customer pain point / your topic]."

9/ Pass the 5-second rule

Now upload your updated profile PDF to Claude and ask, "Would a stranger understand what I do in 5 seconds?"

The gaps are usually smaller than you think.

Claude just shows you where they are.
