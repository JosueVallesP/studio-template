## Post

In 1947, Disney cartoonist Carl Barks created the character Scrooge McDuck to epitomise the wealthy. 

He cemented the image of someone hoarding wealth, swimming in gold coins, detached from reality and oblivious to large scale problems in society. 

This image stuck and most people think this is how someone like Elon Musk has $600B net worth. 

In reality, the complete opposite is true. 

Elon’s wealth is almost completely tied up in companies he has started. He owns shares in Tesla, SpaceX, xAI, NeuralLink, Boring Company and other tech innovations. 

Far from swimming in gold coins, he is drowning in massive commitments. His net worth is predicated on his ability to attract and retain the world’s greatest engineers to invent and scale ground breaking technology. He has had to constantly raise money and has almost run out of cash several times. 

Far from being care free, the levels of stress he has endured for the last 30 years would break most humans. Far from being detached from problems, he has sought them out and created solutions to them. 

Entrepreneurs who are reported to have a massive “net worth” aren’t swimming in cash, care free and detached from reality. 

They are building something of value, immersed in complex problems and in full contact with what’s really going on.
