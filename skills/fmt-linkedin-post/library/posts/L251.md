## Post

In 2022, you could go viral with a pancake recipe. Today, the value-post you spent 3 hours on barely gets 500 views. 

Maybe you remember the good ol' days: 

- Carousels got tons of impressions 
- Hand-raiser posts meant DMs in your inbox 
- Subject matter content got the attention it deserved

Then AI came in. 

It stole your best copy tricks and made them available for everyone. 

The feed got noisy and your prospects became overwhelmed. 

I noticed this too. 

I used to wait for leads booking a discovery call. For them to reply to my "DM me INFO". 

They did. But I wanted more predictable revenue.

So instead of relying on LinkedIn content alone, I started taking people off platform: 

1. LinkedIn became my distribution channel
2. Waitlists brought people to my offer 
3. Story + value emails led to conversion

Resulting in selling out group programs, products, workshops, and other offers.

A dead simple "funnel" I overlooked for 2 years. 

Then I started implementing this with my 1:1 clients and getting the same results (one just drove $30k last month). 

People say: "don't take people off platform, the algo will punish you". 

Well, I've got news for you: 

The algo has been punishing you for a long time. 

LinkedIn content hasn't lost its power. 

You just have to use it differently.
