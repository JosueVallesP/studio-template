## Post

I'm on a flight. It's 9:28 AM.

After watching the landscape pass from ocean to city to mountain to desert underneath us in a matter of a few minutes, I looked away from my window to see something absolutely insane: Only one other passenger on the entire flight, seven rows up, has their window open. Every other single window on this flight is closed.

I'm a pretty optimistic guy, but this is one of the more depressing things I've seen regarding the state of humanity in recent memory. We're so disconnected from the wonders of the world around us that we'd all rather shut our windows and our eyes than spend a few moments marveling at seeing the Earth from 35,000 feet up in the air.

If you make things, it's never been more important to fill your work with wonder and joy and humanness. Curiosity has always been our species' most powerful and consequential trait. But it's feeling in shorter and shorter supply by the day.

It's on all of us to get more people opening their windows again.
