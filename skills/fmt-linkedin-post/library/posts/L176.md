## Post

25 things I wish I knew about LinkedIn when I started:

(5 years and 1,000+ posts later) 

1. Some of the best relationships start in the DM’s
2. People you’d never expect are closely watching 
3. The ‘rules’ and ‘hacks’ have a very short shelf life
4. Making friends matters, prioritize relationship building
5. Some formats that work for others, might not work for you
6. Every action you take creates a micro-experience of yourself for others 
7. LinkedIn = the ultimate microcosm of our bigger societal structures
8. There’s always more to learn but don’t burn yourself out
9. People are pretty predictable 
10. Use other’s ideas as inspiration
11. Systems make a (big) difference 
12. The content you try hard on will flop
13. The post you write in 30 seconds will do great
14. Content is the best long term business investment ever 
15. There’s more than enough business on here for everyone
16. The best time to start was yesterday but better now than never 
17. Creating content is the best way to distill your knowledge & expertise 
18. Sometimes internet strangers are more supportive than your closest circle 
19. Being vulnerable & sharing your story doesn’t get easier you just get used to the discomfort 
20. Don’t be afraid to promote, be direct & sell your offers, it’s literally how people know how to hire you 
21. But give more than you take 
22. Watching someone put themselves out there will always move & inspire me 
23. Someone will quite literally hire & pay you off the back of a well-written post 
24. If you are one of the 1% of users actually showing up to post, you’re mentally tough 
25. Learning how to communicate complex ideas simply is the ultimate skill to develop

Of course these are just compiled from my own personal experiences.

But I’ve found them to be more true than not…

My biggest takeaway?

A little effort (everyday) really goes a long way.
