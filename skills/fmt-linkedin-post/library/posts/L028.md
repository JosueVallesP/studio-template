## Post

Here’s my formula for content that builds trust (screenshot this post)

(Painful Customer Problem + Your Unique Solution) x (Contextual Credibility + Proven Wrapper) = Content that Builds Trust

Painful Customer Problem → the things you’re solving over and over in your business for customers…introduce those problems in your content.

Your Unique Solution → then add in your unique solution to that problem.

Contextual Credibility → people need a reason to believe what you’re saying is legit, general credibility is good, contextual credibility is great. aka talk about the specific thing you’ve done that directly relates to the problem you’re solving.

Proven Wrapper → These are the packaging styles and formats you see outperforming others on platforms.

The key here: please look OUTSIDE your space to find these “wrappers.”
If you don’t, you just end up looking like everyone else in your space
