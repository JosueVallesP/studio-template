## Post

The best way to become a great designer is to be completely obsessed with looking at work. 

Just relentlessly find good work, dissect it, catalogue it, and remake your own versions of it over and over again until you can do it with your eyes closed.

Only then—armed with a deep visual library and thousands of quality reps—can you find your distinctive style that will separate you from the pack.
