## Post

There's a Brazilian saying: "Quem não chora, não mama." 🇧🇷 

Literally: the baby who doesn't cry doesn't get fed.

A month ago I posted that I was sad I didn't get picked for LinkedIn's Next Gen Community. Broken heart emoji, "I have fomo", the whole thing. Peak dignity.

…then Esther Akinola slid into my DMs. "I'd love to have you on the programme!"
So yes. I cried on the internet and it worked lol

I used to think that if I just did good work, the right people would eventually notice. Most of us were taught that wanting something out loud is a little embarrassing. Very uncool. Better to wait and be discovered.

But people can't give you what they don't know you want. 🩵 

So, officially: I'm part of LinkedIn's Next Gen Community, a hand-picked group of creators shaping conversations with LinkedIn News. Kick-off done, first content challenges in my inbox, and I'm already overthinking all of them 🫠

Thank you Esther!
