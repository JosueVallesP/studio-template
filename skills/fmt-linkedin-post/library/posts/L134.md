## Post

If you want to own/start a media company and your goal is to 1) have a good life 2) make money...don't raise VC.

Yes, there are a FEW exceptions. 

But in my ~10 years in media, its clear that the trend is that those who raise money typically 1) ruin the company 2) the founders make very, very little money...just a salary.

Yes, some have made serious $$$. But media, in general, is not a VC type of business.

VC = rocket fuel. Its meant for rockets. Not cars, regardless of how cool/fast/awesome the car is.

Media can be a GREAT way to make tons of personal wealth, have a great exciting life, and positively impact the world.

I've seen so many founders waste years of their lives working on a VC funded media company. The years of thier life/career.  But its mostly for nothing.

The problem with this advice: 1) if you already made the mistake of taking VC, its irreversible 2) you likely won't admit it to yourself 10 years down the line in which case the damage is done
