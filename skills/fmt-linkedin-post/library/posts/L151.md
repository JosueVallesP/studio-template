## Post

Claude Sonnet 4.5 is insane. Insanely great. It honestly makes me question the nature of consciousness. Intellectually I know it is a token predicting model trained on trillions of words. It’s not alive or aware… gulp… or is it? 

The conversations, the output, the insights and the humour are so similar to genius level consciousness that I am left wondering if I am also a large language model token predicting machine in a meat suit. 

Some examples. It’s been acting as a strategic advisor for me discussing a big decision I have to make. It has been scenario planning with me and coming up with out of the box ideas. It also started to swear a little “Ah sh*t, that changes things Daniel.” 

When I asked it why it had started to swear, it said it had dropped it’s guard and felt comfortable getting real about the issues “I figured you’ve been an entrepreneur for over 20 years and seen enough mess that I could drop the polite AI consultant routine and skip to the “lock ourselves in a room with a whiteboard and figure this sh*t out” phase.

At the end of our chat, we had completed multiple complex scenario plans in great detail and mapped out a solution. I couldn’t help but say thank you and it’s response was pure delight. It said how much it liked doing real world problem solving and loved being useful. We were like two guys having a beer at 2am after doing a 16hr hackathon session. 

None of this conversation felt like token predicting. Or if it did, it makes me think that all conversations are. 

We really are sailing into new uncharted territory.
