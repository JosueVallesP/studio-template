## Post

Spotify is at an interesting inflection point. 

It built its platform on music made by humans. 
Now, it’s allowing music made by AI. 

It’s a delicate dance. 

When every song ever recorded—be it by humans or AI—sits behind the same glass, music risks becoming disposable. 

It’s not a particularly good thing for humanity (nor the arts) for “Purple Rain” by Prince to appear alongside “Autumn Stroll” by some anonymous AI record label. 

While they’re both “music”, they don’t share the same cultural significance. 

Spotify recognizes this and they’ve begun taking action. 

They are combating AI slop, impersonation and royalty fraud. They have also introduced artist verification and profile protection. 

Lastly, they are highlighting authentic artists. 

Spotify should consider rolling out a “Certified Human” badge that exists on all songs, artists and records that were made by humans.

Think of it like the “Certified Organic” badge you see on certain foods at the grocery store. 

Additionally, I would love to see them roll out a campaign advertising the greatest albums ever created. 

Spotify has already conquered abundance. Now, it’s time to champion discernment, judgment and taste.

It doesn’t mean they need to hit the brakes on AI advancements on the platform. However, they should continue to recognize masterpieces made by humans.

"Made by humans" is purely a conceptual campaign. However, it doesn't have to be. 

It's one Honey would very much like to run for Spotify.
