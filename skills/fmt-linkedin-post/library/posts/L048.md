## Post

I ghostwrite for a whole bunch of LinkedIn accounts. And one of the most frustrating things I've had to learn:

Nobody wants your wise thoughts.

Posts that are basically "here is an interesting thing I think" will bomb. Pretty much every time. Even if you're very smart and your thoughts are very interesting.

My clients are brilliant, and I still can't save these posts. I've watched a clever industry take get 40 likes, while a simple "lesson learned" story got over 600. Same client. 

The posts that work consistently fall into three types:

1. Here is a good way of doing something difficult that I learned from experience.

2. Here is something that happened to me that you can learn from.

3. Here is how I solved a problem for someone else  - it might work for you too.

They're all tactical. They're all useful. There's something the reader can DO after finishing them.

Next time you're writing a post, ask yourself what you want the reader to do after reading your post.

If your answer starts with "I want them to think..." you probably need to keep working.

You want to give people something they can use.

Hope it helps!
