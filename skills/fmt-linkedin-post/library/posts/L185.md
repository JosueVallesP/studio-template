## Post

In 2015, a 15 minute conversation with a founder changed my life. I walked out of his penthouse suite, resigned as CEO of my startup, and went to work for him as a junior AE. Here’s why WHO you choose to work for matters more than any title:

Here’s what happened:

A friend of mine (Rob Mackenzie) had started a new job in sales at a tech company in Toronto.  I wasn’t job hunting or considering working for anybody else. I was an entrepreneur and had BIG DREAMS of building my own business.

Rob wanted me to meet his boss (Ross Andrew Paquette), so I walked over to meet him.

We met Ross at the Shangri La — swanky 5 star hotel downtown Toronto.  

He was completely bootstrapping his business with a small team. And, he was doing sales calls himself, ALL DAY, EVERYDAY.

I took a look at Ross’ calendar and my only thought was “THIS IS WHERE I WANT TO BE!” 

Ross was already successful and yes, I was blown away by his penthouse.

But more than anything, I was impressed by his work ethic.

As the CEO of Maropost, he was doing back to back sales demos from 9-5 and closing business on his own. He was literally a sales machine.  

I knew I needed to work for Ross to learn how to get to the next level. 

So I flat out asked him, “Can I work for you?” 

He sent me a contract that night and I started the next day.

I dropped my startup completely and never thought about it again.

Ross completely redefined “hard work” for me. 

I quickly became his top rep/right hand in the business and together, we closed millions of dollars.  We worked like crazy but had many surreal experiences together (like the day his first PJ was delivered).

What's the takeaway?

Sometimes you need to take a step back to take a step forward. 

Ross showed me what hard work and dedication it actually takes behind the scenes to build a company like Maropost.

And when I left, I was living in my own penthouse downtown Toronto 😊

Two years later, Adam Robinson and I started Retention.com and copied this playbook. 

My dream of a full calendar of sales calls from 9-5 came true. 

We built a lean, bootstrapped, sales machine and made it to $13M with a team of 6 people. 

Then we did $30M in 2024 with 40 people.

And we are just getting started.
