## Post

You should care about AI taking jobs from writers, musicians, TV producers, filmmakers, artists, cartoonists, and all the other creative fields. Even if you're not affected directly.

When those fields cease to become a career path - which is what's happening now - people will stop producing cool new stuff. AI will not be "just a tool" to create stuff, it will be the only thing creating stuff.

Imagine your favorite TV show or musician or writer never existing. That's basically the path we're heading on for future generations.

There are always people who will try to do this stuff in their spare time, but if you can't get paid for it, you can't truly dive in, get better, and produce something great.

This isn't some hypothetical future. It's already happening now. The job openings for those creative fields are plummeting, and the ones that remain are "prompt engineer" jobs geared around signing off on what AI produces.

You might say "this is simple economics." AI costs less. But no one seems to take into account the revenue side. AI is not bringing in money. It's embarrassing brands. But because "everyone is doing it," slop becomes normalized over time, just like low-quality clothing, electronics that break after a few months, and every other consumer good that's gotten worse with time. When AI writing or music or video is the only option, it's game over. And that's terrifying.
