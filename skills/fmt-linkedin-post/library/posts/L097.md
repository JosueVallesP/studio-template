## Post

A school crossing guard works 50 minutes every morning, then goes home and writes you a letter... an actual handwritten note about what she saw that day.

She called them "cloud reports," and people loved the personality of it, the physical experience of getting something real in their mailbox, and the fact that it felt like it came from an actual person in a world full of endless scrolling.

Now thousands of subscribers pay to receive her illustrated notes, and she makes around $14,000 a month doing it!

She didn't build an audience by cracking an algorithm (like most people). The "attention economy" keeps telling us to post more, optimize more, move faster, and this woman ignored all of it completely.

I love stories like these.
