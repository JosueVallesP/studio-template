## Post

Marketers, please stop making your ads look like ads..

Ads that look like ads don't perform.

Most ads look like freakin' flyers: 

• completely on brand
• super commercial
• call to action

But people don't like ads, and scroll right past them. 

Ask yourself, what do you do when you see an ad on LinkedIn/YouTube/Instagram? You scroll past it!

So if you want to grab the attention of your prospect, don't make your ad look like an ad. 

Make your ads look like native, organic content. Content people would engage with also if it wasn’t promoted.

Exception: traditional advertising. 
When you have a billboard, radio or television commercial you want it super branded. This is something you do to build authority and legitimacy.
