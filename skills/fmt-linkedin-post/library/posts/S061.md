## Post

I've lived in London for the better part of 10 years now. Never understood why cafes always shut so early - it's like your only option after 6pm is a pub.

Saw a post about Corgi Cafe on LinkedIn last week, open 24/7 and targeted at people building in tech, so went for a visit this weekend.

Every table had a laptop out, most running Claude (as you'd expect). Focused energy, people just getting stuff done.

Love the concept.
