## Post

Failing to do things at the right time… According to society:

-I didn’t find the right person for me until I was 39.

-I published my first book at 32. But I had to wait until I was 39 for my first bestseller. Sometimes, you just have to keep plugging away.

-I tried for 12 years to have children. I went through recurrent miscarriage and failed fertility treatment until making my peace with not being a mother in the conventional sense. And on the other side of all the trying and sadness...there was unexpected liberation.

-I was 39 when I launched a podcast that would change my life. I was 45 when I sold out the Sydney Opera House because of it.

-At 47, I’m getting better at handling debilitatingly low self-esteem, but it’s still a daily practice. It helps to acknowledge it - and knowing that almost everyone else struggles too.

-I was 43 when I discovered strength training. It changed my body...and my mindset.


I definitely don’t have it all figured out.
But I’ve come to understand that the richness of life comes not from having all the answers but from living in the question.
