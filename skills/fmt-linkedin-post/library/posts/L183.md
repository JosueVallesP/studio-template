## Post

A few years ago, I saw an Airbnb listing, called the owner out of nowhere, and said 'You seem fascinating. What's your story?' 

I know that sounds like a strange thing to do, but it was worth it.

This guy had had a lake with 6 Airbnb cabins. His About Us page said he was a 24 year old artist, accountant, and entrepreneur. 

I thought that was peculiar, so wanted to hear his story. 

His name’s Isaac French.

He was 25 at the time and making 6 figures profit from the property.

He's become my good buddy since then. We've gone to conferences together. He visits me in New York. I've been to his home for Easter mass.

And here's what's fascinating: I'm not religious and don't believe in God, but Isaac is. He's part of a religious community where 60 families live on one 500 acres. They grow their own food. They make a lot of their own clothes. They're pretty self-sufficient.

According to Isaac's religion, they get closer to God by being a really good craftsman. And so everything Isaac does is purposeful, intentional, and done really, really well.

When my daughter was born, he drew this amazing piece of work about her. When my dog passed away, he gave me another sketch of my dog. He’s looking to start a hotel and he’s traveling the world to get inspired by the best architecture and hotels.

I've been fortunate to talk to tons of entrepreneurs. It doesn't matter if you're Steve Jobs building a massive company, or if you're like Isaac making something highly crafted that provides an amazing experience.

What I love is people who are obsessed and incredibly intentional. People who pave their own path in the world and refuse to be put into a box.
I love business because it's just a really practical way to exercise that feeling.
