## Post

Today is the first morning in 1,735 days that I woke up without a WHOOP recovery score telling me whether I was “green, yellow, or red.” For almost five years I relied on a wearable to tell me how I should feel before I even had a chance to check in with my own body.

I learned a lot from it — yes, alcohol kills sleep quality and meditation helps — but somewhere along the way I outsourced too much of my self-awareness to technology. I stopped listening to internal signals and started waiting for external validation.

So I’m taking a break. My experiment for the new year is simple:

- ŌURA Ring (sleep only) for objective sleep trends
- Garmin (workouts only) for training data
- My own brain and body for everything in between

Not anti-tech — just pro-agency.

Curious if others are reassessing the systems that govern their day. Anything you’re modifying, testing, or taking back this January?
