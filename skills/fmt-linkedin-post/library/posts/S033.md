## Post

The biggest trap I see is that people try to find the perfect idea that will go ultra viral before they post.

Unfortunately, you will never know with absolute certainty when a post is going to perform well.

You just get better at making estimated guesses over time.

However, if you want to make better guesses as a beginner (to give yourself better chances at going viral), do this:

Go to Eden (https://eden.so)

Go to the Discover tab filled with outlier content

Find a viral post, any viral post

Hit the lightning icon, then "Reverse Engineer"

That will tell you exactly why the viral post worked in a chat.

Then, say "Interview me to pull content ideas out of me, then use the viral post principles and post structure to give me 5 content ideas with variations."

Now you don't have to start from scratch.

And you're off to a much better start than 99% of beginners.
