## Post

So much business advice comes from people who don't have kids. 

"Success requires long hours, late nights, weekends, etc".

If you have kids (or other caring responsibilities) stop comparing yourself to people who don’t.

While you’re on the school run, they’ve had an hour of deep work.

While you’re juggling dinner, bath time, and bedtime stories, they’re networking over dinner, posting on Instagram, or filming their ice bath.

If you’re running a business and raising a kid, you’re building two start-ups at once.

Give yourself credit for that.
