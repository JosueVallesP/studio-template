## Post

How much should you pay yourself as a startup founder?


I think you should pay yourself as much as you can afford (and probably more than you think).

For the first 2 years of running The Hustle (which I eventually sold for tens of millions), I paid myself $2,000/mo.

At the end of year 2 the company had like 800,000 in the bank account, and yet I was paying myself nothing and was incredibly cash poor.

I thought it was cool that the CEO should pay themselves nothing and everything should go back into the business.

But I made a lot of dumb defensive mistakes because I was always in a scarcity mindset.

I also wasn’t able to taste the dopamine rush when we were succeeding. 

Even at ~$10 million in revenue, I was paying myself 150k/year. Looking back, it would’ve been totally fair to pay myself 250-350k.

So my advice to first time founders is pay yourself as much as you possibly can because it’s going to make you more patient and better in the long run.

You’re also gonna taste the dopamine rush and want more of it.
