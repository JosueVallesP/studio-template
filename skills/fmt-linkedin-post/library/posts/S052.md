## Post

Apple’s new CEO posted on social for the first time today. It contained one word. 

“hello”

The post was viewed 50m times and gained him 600k followers.

Sometimes CEOs and their teams are guilty of overthinking what they need to say online.

You can imagine what this first post looks like from a lot of companies - written by a comms team, the equivalent of a press release, AI slop etc.

This broke all the “rules” and in turn got more attention than any comms launch plan would have ever achieved.

Unfortunately, the “rules” are never about great communication, they’re inherently about risk management. The outcome is to ensure nothing goes wrong, and as a result, nothing goes right either. 

CEOs who are engineered by comms teams to play safe are also engineered to be invisible. 

I love this because Apple understood that, in 2026, a CEOs first post isn’t to demonstrate their vision or values, it’s to prove there’s a real person behind the account. 

People follow people.
