## Post

Bon Jovi performed 1000 live shows in their first decade as a band. An average of 2-3 shows a week every week for 10 years. 

It’s not enough to have hit songs. You have to tour the album. 

I see so many entrepreneurs who have a hit idea but don’t tour it hard enough. 

They seem to think you can post a few times and then the customers come rolling in. 

They’re wanting the business to take on a life of its own after a smattering of networking events or proposals sent. 

Customers don’t get enrolled because of a quick exposure to a good idea. 

They respond to repetition and experience. 

If you want the business to succeed you can’t be just living on a prayer.
