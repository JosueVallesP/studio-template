## Post

Steve Jobs had a simple rule.

Only start a company if you feel pulled to do it.
The kind of pull that keeps coming back, even when it gets hard and inconvenient.

PS. this is OpenAI's early stage deck: https://lnkd.in/e5bVD5XT

A startup gives you no map and no guarantees.
You build something that does not exist, make decisions with partial information, and watch plans break in real time.

The upside is not money or fame.
The upside is putting something real into the world that would not exist without you.

If the urge is strong, you already know.
If it is not, there are better ways to spend your life.

Do you think obsession is required to build great companies, or can discipline replace it?
