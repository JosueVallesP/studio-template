## Post

Dear CEOs, Investors, Boards,

Take some time to understand the discipline of marketing.  

It's clear we're approaching 2026 and most of us are right where we were 15 years ago.  In fact, it might be worse.

15 years ago people had a deeper appreciation for brand.

Now, if people think about marketing at all, it's exclusively demand generation.  

SOME STORIES FROM MY COACHING CALLS

I've been doing 4-5 calls with Pavilion Members a day for the past few weeks as we near the holidays.  The goal is to help as many people as possible during what can be a high anxiety time.  

Negotiating EOY comp.  Getting fired.  Maneuvering for a promotion.  Taking on responsibilities of a recently fired exec.

What's become clear: marketing is under siege.

Some anecdotes:

- A job interview for a CMO that has a screening question blindly asking "How many MQLs did you generate last quarter?" with no additional context (!!??)

- CMOs being fired and all of marketing rerouted to the CRO with no CMO backfill

- Continued attacks on any budget line item not directly tied to hiring more reps or near term demand gen

- CMOs & marketing dept. being fired after crushing every number because the company needs to show improved profitability before an exit

The list goes on and on.

The #1 feeling most marketers are expressing right now: EXHAUSTION

BACK TO THE OPEN LETTER

So if you're a CEO or Founder or Investor reading this, a couple things:

1. Brand is long term demand generation.  If you want to drive revenue in 2026 and 2027 and 2028 and 2029 and onward you'll need a brand.  Some part of your marketing budget should be allocated towards it.

2. You don't need a "Chief Storyteller".  That's your head of Marketing.  You need a marketing department.  Stop being a moron.  Just hire a great marketer.  

3. We are in a world of commodity software products where people think they can vibe code what you've spent 10 years building in 30 minutes on the weekend.  You'll need marketing to differentiate yourself.  Your marketing can't just be "Ha ha they don't have any security and we're SOC-2"  

4. Your CHEAPEST path to demand generation is MARKETING.  Not tons of sales people.  You don't need more AEs.  You need program spend that puts  your story in front of as many people as possible.  That generates awareness and interest and your sales team converts that to revenue.  

5. You're in the business of driving efficient revenue growth.  That comes through MARKETING and, relatedly, through PARTNERSHIPS.  Hiring 50 SDRs with a 3 person marketing team isn't smart.  You look like a fool.  INVEST in the discipline of marketing.

BOTTOM LINE

I am having call after call with Pavilion Members tearing their hair out that you have no f-ing clue what you're talking about when it comes to marketing.

Take some courses through Pavilion if you need to or DM me.  

But you're doing it wrong and it's going to cost you results in 2026.

Thanks for reading.

Love,
Sam
