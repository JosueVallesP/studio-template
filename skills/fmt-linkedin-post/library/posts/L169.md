## Post

End of an era. 

The MTV music channels are shutting down end of this year.

Was a big source of inspiration to me back in the day. The music, videos, the design and branding played a big part in my early days as a designer.

Ironic fun fact: MTV launched in 1981 with "Video Killed the Radio Star" by The Buggles. That was basically their mission statement from day one.

And now a couple decades later, the internet/social media killed the video star.
