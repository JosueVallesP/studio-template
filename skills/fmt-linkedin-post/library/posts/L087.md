## Post

This the part of Linkedin I'm still in love with. A story in 4 arcs:

1. The CEO of Hubspot uses Linkedin to announce their new AEO tool

2. Jason M. Lemkin jumps on here same day to share his 2 cents on it, aka it's not that great

3. A relatively small, but awesome founder I know Melissa Kwan chimes in and fires some (fair?) shots at the CTO

4. Said CTO of Hubspot Dharmesh Shah PERSONALLY jumps into said comment thread to take responsibility and add context

Now that's awesome.

That's what Linkedin should be about. People sharing real POVs that are earned. CEOs and CTOs of legit companies being active. Real back-and-forth dialogue between users in the comments.

Not "comment for playbook" posts.

Not AI comments replying to other AI comments

Not Linkedin execs repeating that you HAVE TO use vertical video.

But legit leaders talking about legit things and responding to legit comments.
