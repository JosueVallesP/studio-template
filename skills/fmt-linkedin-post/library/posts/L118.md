## Post

Well, this is exciting: I’m building something new.

After 10 years as a partner at True, working with hundreds of extraordinary founders, it’s time to build again. There’s rarely been an opportunity like now to create in a way I never dreamed would be possible. Nothing to announce just yet, but you won’t be surprised to learn that I’ll be digging into the future of design in our new world of abundant intelligence. 

My connection with True goes back decades, to when I first met Tony Conrad as a client at Adaptive Path and then getting to know Jon Callaghan and Phil Black as we partnered on Typekit’s seed round. I learned then what remains true today: you can build a venture firm on deep alignment with founders, not just on outcomes but on values. The result is an unusually supportive community of founders that becomes a bedrock of encouragement in the often lonely entrepreneurial journey.

I couldn’t possibly step away from that community completely. I’ll remain connected as an Advisor to my partners at True and the platform we’ve built. And as ever, I’m always interested in hearing about what you’re building.

And a little teaser: My first step is a project I’ll talk about in a few days. Small, very human, and focused on connection. More on that soon.
