## Post

10 THINGS EVERY B2B CONTENT MARKETER SHOULD READ:

1. SHORT FORM CONTENT MATTERS MOST. 
90% of consumed content is short-form. Also in B2B!

2. DON’T WORRY ABOUT AI (YET). 
Most AI content is rubbish. AI just makes content creation a little faster.

3. NOBODY CARES ABOUT YOUR CHRISTMAS POST OR COMPANY EVENT POST.

4. PEOPLE ARE ON SOCIAL MEDIA TO LAUGH OR LEARN. 
So your content needs to do one of the 2.

5. ATTENTION DRIVES RESULTS PERIOD. 
Enough attention will make you president of the USA.

6. GOOGLE AD COSTS FOR B2B ROSE ~30% LAST YEAR. 
Dependency is dangerous. Content = the cure.

7. YOU ARE NOT MARKETING TO A COMPANY.
But to a person working in that company.

8. CONTENT IS STILL UNDERRATED IN B2B.
It is often handled by juniors or interns. Compete by involving seniors.

9. MOST B2B COMPANIES HEAVILY UNDERESTIMATE HOW OFTEN THEY NEED TO BE VISIBLE TO BECOME TOP OF MIND.

10 PRIORITIZE PERSONAL PROFILES OVER COMPANY PROFILES 
to distribute your content - ALWAYS

11. THERE IS NO SUCH THING AS B2B OR B2C CHANNELS. 
Instagram, TikTok and other “B2C” channels can also work for B2B.

That was it for my New Year's content rant. If you follow these principles in 2026, you'll have a very successful year!
