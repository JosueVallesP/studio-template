## Post

You can't build a business around expertise anymore.

But you can build it around authority.

Authority =

Expertise
+ 
Experience
+ 
POV
+ 
Distinctiveness
+ 
Charisma

We don't need human beings to give us information anymore.
But we do need them to lead us.

Adapt accordingly.
