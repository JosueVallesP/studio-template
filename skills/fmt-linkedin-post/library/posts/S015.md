## Post

The #1 underrated skill in business: long-form copywriting. The kind that people used to use for direct mail.

Most people think AI makes this stuff irrelevant. It doesn’t. It makes it MORE relevant.

Before the internet, advertising pros wrote long ads and ran them in magazines or mailed them to people’s houses. Just words on paper to convince a stranger to buy something.

David Ogilvy (the guy who behind the famous Rolls Royce ad below) called long-form, direct mail advertising “my first love and secret weapon.”

Why? Because longform writing, when done well, is the best way to sell stuff. The issue is that its really, really hard. But if you master it, you become an ATM. 

So many people think: I never read that longform stuff. You do. It works. A plain, ugly website with tons and tons of solid writing will outperform the prettiest website. 

Longform = almost always better.

Words work. There’s a formula for what makes people buy, and the long-form sales letter is the best place to learn it
