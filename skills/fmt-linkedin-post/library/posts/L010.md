## Post

You're still competing on execution, but AI has already turned that into table stakes.

When building was expensive, the bottleneck was getting things done.

You won by executing faster than everyone else.

That advantage is fading fast. When anyone can ship in days, speed stops being the thing that sets you apart.

The new bottleneck is taste.

Knowing which ideas are actually worth building.

The flood of cheap execution means your taste is now the constraint on the whole company.

The question is shifting from "can we build it" to "should we." Your instincts may not have caught up yet.
