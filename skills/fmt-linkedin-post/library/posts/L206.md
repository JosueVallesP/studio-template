## Post

Japan has created a World Cup tradition like no other.

After their brilliant 2-2 draw against the Netherlands, the Japanese players left their dressing room spotless.

Nobody to capture what they did.
No publicity stunt.

Just a habit they've followed for years.

This gesture tells you that respect isn't something you switch on for big occasions.

It's something you practise every day.

The Japanese team understands that better than nobody else.

They set high standards and hold themselves to them even if no one's watching.

It's why you'll often see Japanese fans staying behind after matches to clean the stadium too.

Small actions. Big message.

I've always believed that excellence leaves clues. And one of the biggest clues is this:

The way you do the little things often tells you how you'll do the big things.

Absolute class from Japan. 👏
