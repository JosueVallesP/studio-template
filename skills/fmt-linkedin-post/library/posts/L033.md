## Post

Personal Brands should be building worlds just like Lord of the Rings.

Most people don’t realize how powerful of a tool Worldbuilding actually is because they think it doesn’t apply to educational content.

But they couldn’t be more wrong.

Here are 7 worldbuilding elements that you can apply to your personal brand right now:

#1 - Named Concepts: Just like how there are named concepts like “The Force” in Star Wars, these concepts make it easier for people to take your teachings with them.

#2 - Re-defined Terms: Make the vague words in your industry or space actionable for your audience. Just like how before Tolkien, "elf" meant tiny Christmas helpers or mischievous Shakespearean sprites. He redefined elves for all future generations.

#3 - Sharing Weaknesses: People relate to superheroes not because of their powers, but because of their flaws and weaknesses. So don’t just share successes in your content.

#4 - Characters & Setting: When you think about your favorite movie or TV show, you most likely think about your favorite characters or the locations that make it feel like home to you in some way. How can you create a nostalgic element to your personal brand?

#5 - Interests and Values: Your favorite fictional character is probably your favorite because of the interests, hobbies, and values you have in common with them. Give your audience more opportunities to connect with you on a deeper level.

#6 - Lore and Legends: Game of Thrones, Lord of the Rings, Harry Potter, they all have this rich lore and backstories that make the world more real. You want to have a backstory that provides credibility to what you’re teaching in your content.

#7 - Common Enemy: Like all the villains in the movies, people rally AGAINST something much more than rallying FOR something. My advice though, do this much differently than the majority of gurus are teaching you.
