## Post

When I see copywriters say they don’t use AI it’s NOT a flex to me. It’s a sign that they are not keeping up with the times. 

(I’ve seen this multiple times, including from one of the biggest copywriting experts, who actually trains other copywriters 👀🚩)

As a marketing leader who hires marketing teams regularly, one of the things I’m looking for is people who are using AI to speed up and improve the quality of their work. 

I am not saying we should just be ripping copy straight out of ChatGPT, but if you are learning advanced ways of using it, it can actually sound exactly like you wrote it yourself. Or better.  

AI isn’t going away, and when I think about my clients budgets for hiring, my goal is always to maximize return, so if I can hire an incredible copywriter that has custom GPT’s that are trained on their writing style and frameworks, and they can get the work done in a fraction of the time — I will always choose that route. 

And honestly, if I were delivering marketing services right now, I would be all over AI integration. It would be my competitive differentiator: I will deliver excellent work to you in a fraction of the time for a fraction of the cost because of my AI systems that are trained on my IP. <<< THAT is the future. 

If you are a copywriter, creative or other marketing service provider who is absolutely killing it in this space please say HI 👋 because I am always hiring for my clients teams and this is something I’m not seeing enough of.
