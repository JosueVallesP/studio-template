## Post

If the world's biggest brands were 100% honest, what would their ads say?

1. Apple

"You will pay a 40% premium for this phone because of the color of text bubbles."

2. McDonalds

"Calorie-dense sustenance available in three minutes. You are here because you are drunk, broke, or in a rush."

3. Google

"We are an advertising firm that offers you free tools in exchange for a complete log of your location, curiosity, and private thoughts."

4. RedBull

"An elevated heart rate in a can. We are a marketing company that outsources beverage production."

5. Coca-Cola

"A mixture of carbonated water and high-fructose corn syrup capable of eroding tooth enamel and contributing to Type 2 diabetes."

6. Nike

"Marketing athletic excellence while outsourcing manufacturing to the lowest bidder in developing nations."

7. Zara

"Clothes designed to fall apart after six washes so you are forced to buy the next trend."

8. Louis Vuitton

"We sell coated canvas at a 2,000% markup to signal your social status to people you do not know."

9. Visa

"We take a 3% tax on almost every transaction in the global economy."

10. Starbucks

"A milkshake disguised as coffee, serving as a socially acceptable sugar addiction for adults."

What else would you add? 👇
