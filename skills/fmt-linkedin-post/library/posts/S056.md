## Post

We've rebranded every adolescent impulse as a virtue.

Impulsivity got rebranded as authenticity.
Having no filter got rebranded as “keeping it real.”
Emotional outbursts got rebranded as passion.
Dunking on strangers got rebranded as justice.
Oversharing got rebranded as vulnerability.
Self-obsession got rebranded as Main Character Energy.
Ghosting got rebranded as protecting your peace.
Rudeness got rebranded as radical honesty.

Meanwhile, self-control got rebranded as boring, uptight, fake, and "giving Boomer," as the kids say.

Yes, self-control. The thing the Stoics called power. The thing that samurai, monks, and your grandfather treated as the highest status signal there was. That’s no longer cool. Crying in your car for content is. 

It’s all downstream of social media’s clutch on our culture. The entire incentive structure of public life encourages the loudest, most unfiltered version of you, simply because the algorithm can only monetize the things you did—not the things you didn’t. 

Every impulse on that list got promoted for the same reason: 

It makes content.
