## Post

A content director I know was assigned to rewrite her company’s homepage. So she got to work. 

She did a listening tour: What did sales need from the homepage that they weren’t getting? Where were prospects getting hung up?

She pulled up the heatmap. Where were they rage clicking? How far did most people scroll and where did they stop?  

Then she did some audience research on Reddit. How did the target buyer describe their problems? What complaints did they have?

She met with the design team to discuss everything from UX elements to H1 character counts.  

She used all that to write, rewrite, and edit the page. Every stakeholder below the C-suite reviewed it.

Finally, the new homepage was staged and ready for final approval. 

She presented it to the CEO.

He copy/pasted the H1 into ChatGPT.

Asked for a "better" option. Approved ChatGPT’s version instead. 


Now, there are plenty of ways to use AI well for content production.

But this — outsourcing your judgement to AI and undermining weeks of research, customer insight, and UX analysis with a 5-second prompt — isn’t one of them. Come on.
