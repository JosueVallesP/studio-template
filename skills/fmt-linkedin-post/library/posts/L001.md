## Post

Everyone keeps asking me if SEO is dead.

NOPE!

That said, SEO has changed A LOT.

Specifically, SEO is now MUCH more than just Google.

Here's exactly what changed IMO:

Fewer and fewer people "Google" things anymore.

They search on Amazon. They search on YouTube. They browse Reddit to get people's opinions before they buy.

I learned this the hard way with my SaaS company, Exploding Topics.

When we asked customers how they found us almost NO ONE said they "googled and landed on your optimized landing page". 

Instead it was all over the place.

Someone would first hear about us in a YouTube video. Then they'd Google "trend research tools" to see what else was out there. Then go read Reddit threads and comparison posts to figure out if we were any good.

Only after ALL of that would they finally sign up on our actual website.

If we only optimized for Google, we'd have missed most of that journey.

The customer was already researching us (YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit etc) in places we that traditional SEO would completely IGNORE. 

So here's what "SEO" actually means in 2026:

Optimizing for Google is just PART of SEO now. 

SEO in 2026 is all about showing up wherever your audience searches.
