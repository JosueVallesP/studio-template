## Post

I cold DM 2-3 of my ICP every single week and ask them for 15 minutes on Riverside. The first 7 minutes are for me. The last 8 are a gift back to them.
This is how I never run out of content ideas..

The setup is simple. I find a project manager, a marketer, or a founder, somebody who maps to the ClickUp ICP on LinkedIn. I send them a short message:

"Hey, can I be selfish for 10 minutes on Riverside? First half I'll ask you a few questions. Second half I'll record a personal-brand clip you can use however you want."

About a third say yes. That's plenty.

In the first half, I ask the questions that fuel everything. Questions like:

What's the most ridiculous thing that happened in your office this week? 
What podcasts do you actually listen to?

What does your team complain about that nobody outside your role would understand?

That's where every script for ClickUp Comedy starts. Real situations from real people in our ICP, not ideas pulled out of a writers room with no real-world signal behind them.

In the second half, I flip the camera around. I ask them about their own work - wins, lessons, hot takes. I clip it up afterwards and send it back. Most of them post it on their own LinkedIn that week.

The math on this is hard to argue with. I get original material straight from my buyer. They get free content for their personal brand. The whole thing takes me 30 minutes including the cleanup.

If you're stuck for content ideas, you probably aren't talking to enough of your ICP.
