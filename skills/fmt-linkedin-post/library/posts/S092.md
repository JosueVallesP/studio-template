## Post

I think people would have a much easier time getting clients, building successful businesses and really just enjoying life if they focused the bulk of their energy on these two things:

1. Cultivating genuine, meaningful relationships
2. Thinking long term

Everything else (marketing, offers, content) is just table stakes. You can always tweak your offer or improve your marketing skills. But earning the trust of someone takes a lot of time, and there's a limit to how many people you can do it with.

Still, a few hundred people who trust you, who think your work is genius, and who would take your call will do more for you than having 100,000 followers on LinkedIn.  

And you can't cultivate genuine relationships if you're in a rush, trying to "convert" anyone who accepts your connection request. Which is why those two things aren't really two things. You can't be sincerely interested in someone and be running a 30-day close on them at the same time. People can tell. They can always tell.

If you focus on building genuine relationships without worrying about immediate transactional returns for the next 12-18 months, you’ll be way ahead of 99% of creators on this platform. 100% guaranteed.

P.S. This is especially true if you sell expensive stuff and can only work with 20-30 clients a year. If 25 clients is a full year for you, what the hell are you doing chasing 100,000 strangers?

P.P.S. There's an actual place for audience-building. I mean, I have 130k followers on here. But I think it should be downstream of the relationships, not a substitute for them.
