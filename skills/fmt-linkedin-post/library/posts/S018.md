## Post

I don’t understand this obsessive advice to “raise your prices”.

Price is a very simple concept:

There is some price which is the optimal price for what you’re selling - at which point it will earn you the most revenue.

-> Sometimes this is higher than what you're charging
-> Sometimes this is lower

Your job isn’t to “raise your price”.

Your job is to set your price at the level where you earn the most money.

One of the most basic things I do with my clients is to explore how advantage might be unlocked by making things "worse" as well as making them "better".

In a world where everyone is adding features, doing things quicker, charging more - you have to look for opportunities with removing features, doing things slower, charging less
