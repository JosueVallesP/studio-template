## Post

Just talked to LinkedIn's VP of trust about the algorithm and he revealed what will help your content get seen by more people on LinkedIn

For context:

There are no unique messages
There are only unique messengers

And now, LinkedIn is now matching the messenger to the message.

Your profile is no longer just a landing page.
Your profile and posts need to tell the same story.

When your posts match your expertise, people trust you more. They engage more. They reshare more. And that organic engagement is what drives your reach.

My take? Makes sense. 

Do this right now.

Copy your LinkedIn profile into ChatGPT and use this prompt:

"Based on this LinkedIn profile, give me the five topics you would expect this person to talk about."

Then compare those five topics with what you actually post about.

If they match, you win. Your content is positioned to travel further because people see you as credible and engage accordingly.

If they don't match, you have two options.

Option 1: change what you post about to match your profile

Option 2: use this follow-up prompt:

"These are the five topics I want to talk about: [list them]. Rewrite my LinkedIn headline and about section so my profile matches these topics."

The gap between your profile and your posts needs to close.

Most people haven't figured this out yet.

Which means if you do it now, your content has an unfair advantage over everyone who's still treating their profile like a CV.

Takes ten minutes. 
Could change your reach overnight.
