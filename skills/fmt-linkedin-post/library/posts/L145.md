## Post

Fatherhood doesn't stop when your kid turns 18. 

I'm 34 years old, and I still need my dad. 


Earlier this week, I was wrestling through some doubts & fears. 

I called my dad to chat about it. 

He told me to come over to his house. 


He smoked some ribs for us. 

We drank some good bourbon. 

Smoked some good cigars. 

Sat on the back porch for 2+ hours. 

Talked about life, work, dreams, & more. 


My dad will be the first to tell you that he wasn't the perfect dad:

→ He spent too much time in the office. 

→ He let work stress effect how he showed up at home.

→ He let his priorities get out of whack at times. 


And I'll be the first to tell you that my dad still showed up:

→ He never missed one of my games, no matter what. 

→ He quit a job that demanded he leave our family vacation.

→ He moved our family across the country to give us new opportunities.

→ He worked diligently to make sure I had everything I needed.


I was grateful for my dad as a kid. 

I think I'm even more grateful for him now. 

Because he's still showing up for me & my family. 

Love you, Pops [Rick Graham].


Dads,

I know the demands of life & work & family are great. 

Perhaps you're in a season where you're feeling like it's all too much. 

Like you can't be everything you want to be at work & at home. 

Find small ways to show up in the mundane moments. 

They're more powerful than you realize!


What's one small action you can take this week to show up for your kids?
