## Post

My theory about LinkedIn in 2026 is if you treat it like Twitter in 2021 you will win.

Make jokes, talk about what you do and what you're interested in, get involved with hyper niche groups of people.

Don't make everything hyper-polished.

Share an off-the-cuff opinion.

Learn enough about copywriting to be dangerous then forget the rest and write like yourself. Force yourself to share ~3x per week. Send DMs. Try to sell something. You'll get the hang of it and the fear of the cringe will slowly evaporate.

When everyone on here is desperate to appear polished and brilliant, you can build a lot of trust by being just a tad bit more real.
