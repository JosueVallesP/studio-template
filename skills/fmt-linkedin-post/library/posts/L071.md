## Post

I just launched JustaDesignList.com.

It started in 2023 as a private folder of bookmarks — studios I kept returning to, work that made me stop and look closely. The list grew well past the point of being useful to keep to myself, so I turned it into a small, slow public archive.

A few things it is not: it is not exhaustive, it is not scraped, and nothing on it is paid. Every entry is there because the work, at some point, made me pay attention. It leans toward small and medium studios, independent practices, and designers who publish their work clearly on the open web. Large network agencies are mostly absent — that part is on purpose.

If you know a studio whose work rewards close attention, I’d love to hear about it. You can also just say hello.
