## Post

7 years ago, we started our journey to become the #1 B2B Marketing Agency worldwide.  

Crazy to think how far we've come already:

• 70 team members in July 
• 20+ different nationalities
• HQ in Rotterdam, second office in Lisbon
• Awarded best European B2B Agency 2025
• 80+ clients throughout Europe and North America. 

But we have a looong road to go still. 

So somewhere it feels like we're just getting started. 

And yes, the road is full of hiccups, mistakes, pressure, chaos..

But I learned that a strong vision, combined with an even stronger willingness to make it happen, is what keeps you moving forward.

So freaking proud of our team. 

Let's do this!
