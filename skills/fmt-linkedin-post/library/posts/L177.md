## Post

Some people ask about success tips on LinkedIn. I can’t really give you advice on how to be successful, but I might have some thoughts on staying sane.

- Post and ghost. Make a post, maybe engage with it for a bit, then turn off notifications. Especially if a post takes off, after a certain point where your friends have mostly commented, most of the other comment threads devolve into weird attention-seeking or fight picking from randos. Ignore and move on.

- I delete the LinkedIn app from my phone, except when I’m traveling (some people message me on it). This prevents the constant “derp-scrolling” I find myself doing in agony.

- People want virality. It’s extremely hard to force vitality. Post something, and be at peace. If it goes viral, great. If not, the algo Gods aren’t with you this time. It happens. My most popular posts are usually spur of the moment blurting.

- Check LinkedIn once or twice a day. Trust me, you’re not missing out on much. It will still be here. You don’t need to live on the app (I know I don’t always do this). LinkedIn is basically a LARP for business people. It’s not real life.

- Move to Substack ;) The scene is way better there right now.
