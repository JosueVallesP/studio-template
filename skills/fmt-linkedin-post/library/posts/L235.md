## Post

Figma's stock is down 86% from its peak. Their revenue growth just accelerated to 46%.

Both are true. 

And the gap between those two numbers is maybe the best positioning lesson of the year.

When AI started generating interfaces, the market wrote Figma's obituary. Design tools are dead, code is a commodity, price the stock at $19.

Dylan Field didn't argue with the premise. He agreed with it. Code IS becoming a commodity.

Then he built the story on top: when anyone can generate software, craft and taste become the scarce thing. And Figma is the taste tool.

The numbers say buyers bought the story. 46% growth, second straight quarter of acceleration. Net retention at 139%, a two-year high. 

The AI that was supposed to kill them is now their biggest growth driver.

Wall Street is catching up late. Citi just initiated coverage calling Figma "built for AI-driven design chaos." The stock popped 6% on it.

Repositioning starts by conceding what everyone can already see. Then it tells you what that fact makes valuable now.

Admit the change/new reality and then reposition around what survives it.
