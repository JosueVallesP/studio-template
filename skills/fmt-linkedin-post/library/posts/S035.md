## Post

Is SaaS, you’re broke until you’re rich.
 
Most Founders celebrate big funding rounds while they’re getting paid peanuts. They dream of big secondaries, as Anthropic and OpenAI decimate exit multiples across the board.
 
Do you know what Anthropic and OpenAI can’t decimate the value of???
 
CASH FLOW
 
The reason I am standing here today is because the first book I read when I decided to become an entrepreneur was Rework by Jason Fried. Something about the idea of running a business that made lots of money and gave me the life I wanted made so much sense to me.
 
I followed in his footsteps.
 
I believe the number of successful bootstrappers will grow exponentially over the next decade because a) it’s so much cheaper and easier to start a SaaS now, b) the pipe-dream valuations of normal-ish software businesses are gone, so c) more people will start building them how they always should have - profitably, and to the scale they were meant to be … not pushed harder by VC.
 
How to shift from VC —> Bootstrapper mentality:
 
- Growth comes from disruptive product AND disruptive marketing
- VC money will not solve that problem, it will make it harder to solve
- Start charging people $$$ as early as humanly possible
- (we are two weeks into our MoltSets beta, $275k ARR)
- Work on niche problems with small TAMs first - sub-VC scale opps
- Once you have PMF, keep team tiny for way too long - $10m ARR and 3 people
- Expand TAM by throwing spaghetti at the wall and seeing what sticks
- Always be the leanest version of yourself you could possibly be
 
In summary, build products that generate profits.
 
Use those profits to build other products.
 
You’ll wake up some day and have total freedom, living the dream.
