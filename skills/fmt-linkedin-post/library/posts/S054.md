## Post

Nobody wants to hear this, but grinding harder to produce work that AI can produce faster and cheaper isn't a sustainable strategy.

Like the factory worker in 1970 working longer and grinding harder on the assembly line in the factory with the new robots. The work ethic was real, but the strategy was broken. Effort simply wasn't the variable that mattered anymore. 

Grinding harder didn't save the factory worker's career. It helped them compete to be the last one standing in the declining economy of skilled labor, instead of building the skills of the future for knowledge work. 

It's a tough pill to swallow, especially while the strategy is still working to some degree. Corporate cultures largely continue to celebrate and reward grinding, sacrificing, and overworking.
