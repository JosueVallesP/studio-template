## Post

One of the best things I’ve read on finding a job in a long time - How to Enter Side Doors 

Key points:

- jobs aren’t fixed objects waiting on job boards, they’re “a bundle of problems someone wants solved badly enough to pay for.” This means there are more ways in than it seems 

- the front door is broken: online applications are saturated, especially with resumes written and submitted via AI

- side doors work because the go around this and generate a differnt kind of signal that cuts through the nose 

- there are two kinds of side doors:

- 1/ outbound - proactively reaching out, in as specific and “expensive” a way as possible. You have to prove you’re a great fit for this role specifically. Write a memo, build a prototype, etc 

- 2/ inbound - building in public (essays, products) so the right people find you

This is the era of the side door; most of the best jobs will be found in this way
