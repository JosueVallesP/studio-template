## Post

One of my clients finished #1 at his company. Here's what his company did to reward him: 

On the day before his commission payout, his leadership team called and said they would be paying him $50,000 less than what was owed on his biggest deal of the year. 

They explained it was a low margin deal, and their was a clause in the comp plan which gave them the right to change the payout for big deals.  

Nobody had told him this would happen when he received pricing approvals to sell the deal, and only gave him notice the day before his annual commission payout. 

I told him to get the hell out of dodge as fast as possible! 

Unfortunately, this happens all the time in tech sales. I regularly hear horror stories of companies withholding large commissions because of some hidden clause.  

To these companies I say this: if you want to lose your top sellers, then mess with their pay. But you aren't just messing with their pay; it impacts their families and lives in ways you aren't even thinking about. 

Would you be okay if this happened to you? Of course not! So why is it okay to dehumanize sellers? 

Reps pour their blood, sweat, and tears into making you, your company, and your clients successful, and deserve to get paid what they are owed regardless of a clause in the comp plan. 

Do the right thing and take care of your people, or else they will leave just as this seller is doing! Karma is a bit&% and what goes around, comes around. 

You are so much better than this!
