## Post

This post on X is truthfully funny. 

Lots of takes just come from podcasts. Value-add is elusive in VC. Big exits are outliers. Being selected by the best founders is no easier than identifying the best people to back. Most VCs aren’t technical. And just as many never worked in a startup. 

So if I was a founder I’d ask 3-4 of these questions to filter for VCs who are light in ego but deep in the work:

- if your portfolio founders started a WhatsApp group chat about you, what would they roast you for?

- why did the last company you backed choose you over other investors?

- tell me about an investment you’re most proud of and why? how about one that dramatically failed?

- if I asked the founder you had the most impact on, what would they say about your contribution to their journey?

- what’s a now blindingly obvious thing you were wrong about?

- what books had the most impact on how you think about business? how about life in general?

- what’s a startup you’d love to join today and what role would you love to do there if you weren’t investing?
