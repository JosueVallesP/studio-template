## Post

The best brief I ever saw was one question long.

What does our customer actually resent about this category?

Everything else, the messaging, the angle, the campaign, followed from that. 
Nobody touched the product. Nobody changed the price. 
Just one question asked before anyone opened a laptop.

That question was the work.
