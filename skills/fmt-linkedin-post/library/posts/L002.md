## Post

LinkedIn has changed how carousel posts display in the feed. The first two slides now appear together as one combined block rather than two separate frames, which means cover text is smaller and harder to read at a glance, lending itself toward quick scanning rather than deep reading.

What this means for your upcoming carousel content:

1. Design the hook as a pair. Since slides one and two now show together, put the main headline on slide one and the supporting line on slide two, so they read as a single opening statement.

2. Switch to a 4:5 ratio (1080 x 1350px) rather than square, to make better use of the smaller display area.

3. Make every slide work alone. With scanning behaviour increasing, each slide should carry a complete thought or value point, even if someone only sees one before swiping away.

4. Go bigger and bolder. Larger type and strong colour contrast will help keep content legible now that everything appears smaller on mobile.
