## Post

Just so you know, having a stacked calendar of back-to-back meetings all day isn't a flex. 

Research shows it's actually the complete opposite. When you tell people how busy and stressed you are, it makes you look incompetent and less likeable. 

Even worse, bragging about your stress and busyness negatively impacts your co-workers. It's called the enhanced stress crossover effect. Stress, anxiety, and burnout are contagious and can be transferred from one person to another. 

What's actually a flex:
-Getting at least 7.5 hours of sleep every night
-Delegating effectively and empowering others 
-Having an efficient and profitable business 
-Working less and only focusing on the highest leverage activities 
-Having at least 2 days per week with no meetings and no Slack 
-Quiet, calm mornings for deep work. No meetings before 11am
-Creating the space to exercise daily & prioritize your health and wellbeing
-Having the time and mental space to be present with your spouse, kids, and friends
