## Post

I ghostwrite for a bunch of LinkedIn accounts. Which means I get a first-hand view of what actually gets leads here. Here's a framework I've started using because the new algo seems to love it: 

Step 1. Think of a difficult question that you've genuinely been asked by your ICP. (Please, no more of those "Founders always ask me" obvious BS posts.) I go through discovery call transcripts to get these. 

Step 2. Write down your best answer. 

Step 3. Articulate how you learned that answer. Did you get something wrong and learn the hard way? Did someone smart tell you? Did you do something and it worked really well? 

Step 4. How you learned the thing is the hook. 

Step 5. Then share the thing you learned. 

Save this, try it out, and see if it works for you too! (And if it does let me know :) I always want to know if I'm giving good advice or not.)
