## Post

In 1957, an investor gave a young Warren Buffett $100,000 to manage. When Buffett asked why, the man said: "Because you remind me of Charlie Munger."

Buffett had never heard of Munger, so the investor arranged a dinner for them to meet. 

That single introduction sparked one of the most famous partnerships in business history.

One of the most valuable things you can do for others is simply notice who should know each other. Making a good introduction takes like 2 minutes of your time, but it can completely change the trajectory of someone's life or business.

Who’s the last person you introduced to someone else, just because they needed to meet?
