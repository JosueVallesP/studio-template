## Post

I met a 26-year-old from Germany who bootstrapped an AI company to $5M ARR with 15 people in under two years, then sold it to Zillow.

Here's his story:

- At 15, he started blogging about microelectronics
- Self-published a book on Amazon to monetize it, ended up starting a publishing business
- Sold the publishing biz at 21 for $600k before starting college
- Co-founded a van rental business with friends during college
- Exited that for another $600k two years later
- Did a year abroad at Harvard and came up with the idea for Virtual Staging AI
- Scaled it to $5M ARR in under 2 years with 15 people, Zillow acquired it in 2024

His name is Michael Bonacina. Met him in Hampton. Pretty cool guy.
