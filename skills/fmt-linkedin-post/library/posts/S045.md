## Post

3 years ago, I was called crazy for posting here.

Now it's where 99% of my creative opportunities are generated from.

Despite everyone calling me cringe back then, I decided to keep posting anyways. I had this weird feeling that investing in LinkedIn would eventually pay off.

And that it has...

20,000+ followers later:

- I've been invited to LinkedIn events
- Went from selling my art for $500 → 5-figures
- Worked with Fortune 500's on content + brand partnerships
- Launched an art business from the ground floor (Casey Rickey Art)
- Met amazing entrepreneurs, creatives, and other creators around the world
- And so much more

All because I had the courage to keep pressing post, even when everyone around me was calling me cringe.

Some days (actually most days), I feel like giving up. 

"Is this even worth it?" I find myself constantly asking myself.

But then, some insane life changing opportunity or DM will pop in my inbox as a result of posting here. Then I'm reminded just how POWERFUL this place is, and why I continue to show up day-in and day-out.

If you're early on in your journey here... KEEP GOING.

I promise you, if you keep at it, eventually you'll catch your stride and LinkedIn will start to work its magic.
