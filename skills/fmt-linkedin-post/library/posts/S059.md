## Post

I'm incredibly bullish on the unscalable, high-touch, deeply human things AI can never truly replicate. Travel planning, matchmaking, restaurants and hospitality, hosting, coaching, therapy, live experiences, mentorship.

My bet for the next few decades: as AI makes digital content infinite and free, the most valuable businesses will be the ones that are deliberately, stubbornly human.

The scarcer AI makes the digital, the more important the human becomes. 

I'm long the unscalable.
