## Post

Well this is an interesting development: Business Insider accepted my pitch for a personal essay about my unusual living situation. But before we could move forward, the editor needed a video call to verify I have a pulse. 

The reason? 

They've received a number of AI-generated pitches from AI-generated people. And no publication worth its weight wants that.

So now, all human writers must prove their identity with a video call and an ID.

The world has changed so much since I was in the newsrooms fielding pitches. 

But my question is: How lucrative is this grift?
