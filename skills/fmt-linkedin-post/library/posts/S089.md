## Post

It pisses me off when so-called "experts" whose only skill is building an audience hand out advice on business, money, health, and relationships as if they really knew what they're talking about.

A big following proves they're good at getting attention, but be careful about mistaking attention for expertise.

I've seen people quit stable jobs, drain their savings, and burn months and years following a playbook from someone who has never run a business, managed a team, or sold anything one to one.

Yes, building an audience is a skill, but the problem is when people treat it as proof of every other skill.
