## Post

2 weeks ago we took our 4 month old on her first ever flight. We were preparing for the worst. Crying and all that.

But she was an absolute trooper!

Cried a bit as we got on the plane and waited for takeoff.

But during take off my wife fed her to help with the ear pressure stuff and while she was eating she fell asleep, so we popped her in the car seat and she slept...

FOR 4 HOURS. 

😂

Literally woke up with 30 minutes left then as we landed prepared to feed her again but we didn't need to.

She was just looking around all happy as we landed.

All the adults and kids around us were feeling sick (me included) from turbulance and ear pressure.

Not her. Absolute legend of a baby 👶
