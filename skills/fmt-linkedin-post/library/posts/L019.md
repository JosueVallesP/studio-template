## Post

This sounds lame but hear me out:

Whenever I want to make a transformation, I unfollow everyone and only follow the people I wanna be like.

Wanna get fit: my instagram feed is ripped dudes and healthy eating.
Dress better: people I find stylish

I think the easiest way to get better at something + change your life is to surround yourself with others like you and people you want to be like.

Yes...this is an easy plug for Hampton. If you wanna build a great company and live your rich life (bigger than just $$$)...surround yourself with people who want to get after it. Its contagious.

But regardless of Hampton -- the easiest thing you can do right now is write down who you wanna be, clean your social feed, and digitally surround yourself with people you want to be like.
