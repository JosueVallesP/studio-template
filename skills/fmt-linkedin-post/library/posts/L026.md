## Post

Overthinking builds problems, not solutions.

Here are 9 rules about overthinking:

1. You can’t think your way out of a feeling problem. Overthinking is underfeeling. Trying to think your way into feeling emotions is like trying to drink your way sober.

2. Trying to think your way out of overthinking is like trying to sniff your way out of a drug addiction.

3. “Overthinking invents more problems than it solves.” — Gurwinder Bhogal

4. Your brain ruminates because it hates uncertainty. It would rather fantasise a ca .astrophe than deal with not knowing what the future holds.

5. Unpopular opinion: your brain ruminates because you are getting something out of the rumination. Find out what that is.

6. You never get paralysed by overthinking positive outcomes, you only overthink about terrible outcomes.

7. Most people don’t think about things enough. You are probably not one of them.

8. The more you think about yourself, the harder it is to access happiness.

9. The amount you overthink is directly inverse to how much you live life. Do not trade the thing you want for the thing that is supposed to facilitate it.
