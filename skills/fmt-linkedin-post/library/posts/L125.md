## Post

“Why’s my engagement so low??”

Well… maybe because you never actually engage back.

LinkedIn isn’t designed to be your own personal billboard (even though some people do seem to think so...)

If you’re only showing up to drop your post and then vanish, don’t be shocked when nobody shows up for you.

1. Want comments? Go leave comments.
2. Want people to care? Go show people you care.
3. Want reach? Go earn it.

Stop treating this place like a megaphone. Start treating it like a coffee shop.
