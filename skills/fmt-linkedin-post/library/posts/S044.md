## Post

There is a a sentence every entrepreneur should have tattooed on their arm so they don’t forget this fundamental truth…

“If it was easy and required no risk, everyone would do it. If everyone was doing it, it wouldn’t be valuable. “

Things that are easy and risk free are rarely valuable. 

Sometimes you find yourself doing things that are hard. 

If you’re convinced that those challenging things will result in solving meaning problems at scale, then doing the that thing is you crossing the moat that makes what you are doing valuable. 

Some days you just need to hear it - doing hard or risky things is part of the process of success. 

Keep going.
