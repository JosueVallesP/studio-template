## Post

In the past 3 months, I’ve generated 6,700 saves and 1,690 new followers with what I call “Proof → Payoff” hooks. Here’s the exact formula I use (and why it’s so effective): 

The basic premise here is to establish credibility through firsthand experience and/or results, then promise a specific, valuable takeaway derived from said experience. 

As a 2-part hook, the formula is as follows: 

(1) Proof → “Why should I listen to you?” 

This should be a specific result, experience, or sustained practice that gives you the credentials to speak to the topic at hand. 

(2) Payoff → “What will I get from reading on?” 

With your credibility established, you follow by promising a framework, methodology, resource, or other actionable lesson that can help a reader achieve similar outcomes. 

The key is that the proof establishes the payoff’s credibility. 

Here are a few variations from recent posts of mine:  

→ “I’ve spent the last 3+ years literally studying content for a living. Here are 7 resources I can’t recommend enough:” 

→ “I’ve written or edited ~1,800 pieces of content in 2026 alone. Here’s the 9-step workflow I use to edit almost every draft:” 

→ “I’ve onboarded 18 ghostwriting clients in the past 1.5 years. Before I write a single piece of content, I run a 7-step research process to make the engagement as valuable, seamless, and efficient as possible:” 

With the Proof → Payoff hook, you're demonstrating that you've put in the work and offering readers an opportunity to benefit from what you've learned in doing so.  

That's why the stronger the connection between your proof and your payoff, the more compelling your hook (and thus your content) becomes.
