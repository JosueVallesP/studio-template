## Post

Slop doesn't pay. A 16-month study of 2,000 AI-generated articles on new websites found that, despite an initial surge, search performance collapsed to next-to-nothing after a few months, and then to nothing at all.

Topic somewhat affected the shape of the curve, but they all ended up in the same place. 

The authors attribute this collapse to the usual SEO reasons:

“No backlinks or external validation.”
“No authors, credentials, or real-world expertise.”
“Much of the content resembled what already exists.”
“No internal linking, topical organization, or clear hierarchy.”

However, an external expert who read the data summed up the real issue nicely:

"No one is going to bookmark, share, subscribe or revisit a site with average content."
