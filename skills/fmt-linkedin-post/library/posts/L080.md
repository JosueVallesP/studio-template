## Post

I don’t use AI writing because I like writing. I don’t get why people don’t understand that.

The glee with which some people tell writers their career is over and it's time to become a prompt engineer is such a bummer. Every day I am derided as a "horse and buggy driver" or something similar for saying I don't use AI.

You wouldn’t tell a musician to let a robot play piano for her, or an athlete to let a machine dribble the ball - especially when the evidence so far all suggests the robot doesn’t do any of that very well. But even if it did, why would those people be excited to see one of their favorite things in life taken away?

The problem with Optimization Culture is that it sucks all the joy out life. At some point you have to ask yourself, what are you optimizing for?
