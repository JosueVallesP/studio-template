## Post

Go to your LinkedIn company page right now. Then open your G2 listing. Then your YouTube profile. Then your homepage.

Read the tagline on each one.

If all four say different things, LLMs have no idea what you actually do.

This is the brand consistency problem most companies don't realize they have. 

Every third-party profile describing your product differently is a conflicting signal to the models generating recommendations about your category.

Here's how to fix it indefinitely in an afternoon:

Step one. Pull your current homepage headline and top-nav services. This is your source of truth. Whatever your homepage says you do is what every other surface should say you do.

Step two. List every third-party profile you own. LinkedIn, G2, Capterra, Clutch, DesignRush, Crunchbase, your Twitter bio, your Instagram bio. Most companies have 8-12 of these they've forgotten about.

Step three. Create a quarterly automation using Claude Cowork to rewrite every mismatched profile to align with your source of truth. Same core language. 

Same service framing. Same positioning. The image attached is an example. 

Brand consistency is easy, but it’s also easy to forget about, too. 

The power of tools like Claude make it so easy wins get executed, not forgotten. Setup the automation and improve your perceived positioning on a consistent cadence.  

The brands showing up consistently in AI-generated answers aren't doing anything exotic.

 They're just saying the same thing everywhere.
