## Post

I hear a lot of young people say, "I can't date right now, I gotta focus on my career."

I think that’s B.S. You're dating the wrong person. 

When I had my first company, there were so many nights I'd come home exhausted and lay on the ground, thinking, ‘I want to quit’. 

My wife would listen and let me complain for while. But then she'd give me a pep talk and tell me to get after it. She never made me feel like I was choosing between career and family. 

Early on, the right person will tell you: ‘I know you're trying to provide for our future family. I know you're trying to build a business. That's okay. Keep going.’ 

The right person doesn't ask you to slow down your career. It's the opposite. They’re the reason that you work harder.
