## Post

I told you 2026 will be the year of "I told you so." Here is more about the so-called "benefits" of "AI":

"One of the promises of AI is that it can reduce workloads so employees can focus more on higher-value and more engaging tasks. But according to new research, AI tools don’t reduce work, they consistently intensify it: In the study, employees worked at a faster pace, took on a broader scope of tasks, and extended work into more hours of the day, often without being asked to do so. That may sound like a win, but it’s not quite so simple. These changes can be unsustainable, leading to workload creep, cognitive fatigue, burnout, and weakened decision-making. The productivity surge enjoyed at the beginning can give way to lower quality work, turnover, and other problems."

I know, I know some will still answer with "But, but, but..." until the end of time... 

Because for some, it is easier to find a new excuse than to acknowledge the huge pile of limitations, pushback against, and impossibilities of "AI."
