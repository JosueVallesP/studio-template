## Post

I’ve spent the last 3+ years literally studying content for a living. Here are 7 resources I can’t recommend enough:

(1) Become a Dangerously Good Writer in 55 Minutes — Sam Parr with Alex Garcia and Brian Blum

One of the legends of modern copywriting, Sam packs an incredible amount of craft wisdom into this hour-long episode.
 
🔗 https://lnkd.in/ei734jRN 

(2) The Neuroscience of Addictive Storytelling (And How to Master It) — Kane K.

By far the best breakdown I’ve seen on how to use dopamine loops to make your content impossible to put down.

🔗 https://lnkd.in/e-k-Xjdt 

(3) 10,000 Hours of Studying the Best Viral Hooks in 51 Minutes — Mino Lee

You can’t spend too much time studying hooks, and this is an absolute masterclass.

🔗 https://lnkd.in/eTC26m5P 

(4) The Brand Expert Behind Gary Vee and The Hormozis’ 11.5M Followers — Caleb Ralston with Tiffany K. Guillen
 
Caleb’s approach to content and personal brand building is truly a breath of fresh air. And his results speak for themselves.

🔗 https://lnkd.in/e2s6XGVr 

(5) 2-Hour Youtube Masterclass From The World’s Highest-Paid Strategist — Paddy Galloway with Mark Mastrandrea

YouTube strategy has huge cross-platform leverage, and the way Paddy thinks about content is incredible. Highly, highly recommend this one.

🔗 https://lnkd.in/eZGadjPi 

(6) The Future of Social Media (Do This Before 2027) — Alex Garcia on The Grant Owen Podcast

Alex’s second appearance on the list, but this one is too good to leave off. If you want to know where social is headed in the next 6-12 months, tune in.

🔗 https://lnkd.in/eSv77c_H 

(7) how to fall down a curiosity rabbit hole & reconnect to your creativity — Anna Corinne Howard

Cultivating curiosity is an extremely underrated part of the content creation process, and I really love Anna’s approach here. One of many episodes of her pod I’d urge you to check out.

🔗 https://lnkd.in/eK8VyB6F 

P.S. If it wasn't painfully obvious, I’m always looking to add to my “Watch Later.” Please drop any recs in the comments.
