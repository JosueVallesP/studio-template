## Post

We built the company I wish existed when I was a CMO.

TLDR:
- After two years in stealth, we’re officially launching Science x Story, a brand driven growth consultancy where business strategy is brand strategy.
- Brands we’ve worked with include Rocket, Intuit, Scopely, Kraken, Soul Cycle and UpWork to name a few. 
- Clients have called our work “transformational” (see our website for more testimonials).
- Doing this with my childhood friend Tim Jones, one of the smartest people I know (Plus two British accents are better than one!).

Tim and I started Science x Story with a simple observation from our own experiences on both sides of the table. Agencies were often brilliant creatively but struggled to understand my business. Consultancies understood the business but didn’t fully unlock the role brand could play in driving it. That gap meant less focus, more cost/time, and ultimately less impact for clients.

Science x Story is closing that gap. Our belief is straightforward: brand should not describe a business, it should help accelerate where that business is going. So we start with the future of the business model and build brand strategy to support and drive that trajectory. Over the last two years, we’ve had the chance to test and refine this methodology with some of the smartest leaders I know (Jonathan Mildenhall, Hayden Brown, Walter Driver, Evelyn Webster, Thomas Ranese, Mayur Gupta) and their companies Rocket, Upwork, Scopely, SoulCycle, Intuit, and Kraken.

It is particularly relevant now. AI is making it easier than ever to build and scale, which means the real challenge is deciding what is worth doing in the first place. The answer sits at the intersection of business and brand and becomes a company’s system prompt, shaping how it shows up across people, partners and increasingly, agents.

I’m grateful to be building this with our clients and one of my oldest friends and the smartest human I know, Tim Jones, and thankful to everyone who has and will support us along the way.

We built this for CEO's and CMO's who believe brand should not describe a business, it should help accelerate where that business is going.

If this is you then we should talk. If this isn’t you, we should talk sooner.
