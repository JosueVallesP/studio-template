## Post

When Herb Kelleher ran Southwest Airlines, he made new employees clean planes...including the toilets.

Even senior hires.

Because he wanted everyone to understand the business from the ground up.

No exceptions.

Here’s what happened:

1. Flight delays dropped
2. Employee turnover stayed low
3. Southwest became the most profitable airline in history

Kelleher believed you can’t lead what you don’t understand.

So he built a culture where even the execs rolled up their sleeves.

Most companies teach culture in a PowerPoint.

Herb taught it in a mop bucket.
