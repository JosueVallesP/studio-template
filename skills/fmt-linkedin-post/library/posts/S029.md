## Post

One of the most important questions my mentor Steve regularly asks me has nothing to do with business. “How are you doing with Linda?” Linda and I celebrated 44 years of marriage. When we got married, we said we’d give it 50 years and then decide whether we were going to stick with it. I guess I’d better be on my best behavior with contract renewal coming up! 

Joking aside, Steve’s question matters. I’m a goal-oriented person. I work long hours. I travel too much. There will always be another meeting, another problem to solve and another goal to pursue. 

Sometimes when Steve asks about Linda, my instinct is, “Yeah, yeah, we’re doing great. Now here’s what I really need your help with…” But his persistence has been a gift. He reminds me that the most important relationship in my life deserves the same intentionality I bring to everything else. 

We all need someone willing to ask us the questions we might otherwise avoid. This includes making sure you have someone in your life who challenges you to keep your most important relationships among your most important priorities.
