## Post

12 years ago, Paul Graham (famous investor & entrepreneur) published an essay on the importance of being kind.

His point: to succeed in the startup world, you must not be mean. Mean people fail.

His reasoning:

1. Mean people fight, and fighting is stupid. It's a poor use of your mental and physical resources.

2. Mean people have insufficient networks - they can't get the best people to work with them.

3. Mean people are usually extrinsically motivated while kinda people are intrinsically motivated. And the individuals who are motivated by more than money typically keep going longer than others.

I loved this piece.
