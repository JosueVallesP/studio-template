## Post

An expert is an idiot who didn't quit.

Trust me, it takes one to know one.

To quote the great philosopher of our time, um, Seth Rogen, "If you don't quit, you might make it. If you quit, you definitely won't."

This post serves as a reminder to all my creative friends... to create for yourself, not just for agencies and brands. 

Keep chipping away on an idea, even if it takes a few years to get there.

There are so many examples of talented, creative professionals who finally started creating for themselves and have become stupidly successful.

And I think there are a lot more of us out there.
