## Post

Ranking the best business models:

S Tier

- Marketplace: hardest to start but super hard to kill once you get critical mass

A Tier

- Software: public markets are bearish on SaaS right now but anything that’s hard to get into is a good business

- Agency / Services: you’ll need to deal with a lot of people issues, but it’s very easy to start, super profitable, and you can pivot and build more stuff based on what you learn from clients

B Tier

- Media: I did this with my company The Hustle which I sold for tens of millions. Valuations are very low and if you raise VC it’s an F, but if you own the whole thing and are willing to run it for a long time, it’s great.

- Events: Alot of people will disagree with me, but I think events businesses can be super super profitable, especially B2B ones (tradeshows)

- Info / Courses: I own one (copythat.com) and courses can be great for cashflow, but won’t be worth a lot if you want to sell; also difficult to scale past a certain size

C Tier

- Community: I’m doing this right now with Hampton. People can be a pain to deal with, but this type of business when done right can last a long time (50+ years).

D Tier

- Middleman: My dad owns a produce brokerage company selling $10M/year in onions, but it’s tough because margins are razor thin

E Tier

- E-commerce: one of the worst, not a lot of cash flow, insane competition

Anyone else have thoughts on this?
