## Post

Tackiest compliment I've ever received as a copywriter. 

“I love your writing so much!!” she tells me.
 
“Aww, thank you, I love hearing that, writing is so much fun for me.” I respond.

“Yeah, I love your writing so much I take every email you send and load it into AI and ask it to learn and copy the way you write. Then when I want to write something for my business I tell it to do it in Camille’s voice. So smart, huh? Then I never have to hire you, but I get your exact voice!!”
