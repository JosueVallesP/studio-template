## Post

The Amazon River just wrote its first ever brand logo. Literally. 

They studied satellite images of the Amazon and its tributaries and found letter shapes hidden in the curves of the waterways.

Every single character in the logo comes from real coordinates of the river system itself.

The typography is shaped by the geography of the region.

25,000 kilometres of navigable waterways turned into a living typeface.
And it goes further than just the logo.

The brand was co-created with residents, workers, artists and representatives from all nine states that make up the Brazilian Legal Amazon.

Illustrators, photographers, letterers, all from within the territory.

A "Made of Amazon" seal was created for local products and entrepreneurs to certify their Amazonian origin.

This is the first time the nine states have ever shared a single unified brand.

All under one identity while each keeping their own.

The idea being that the brand doesn't just bring visitors in. 

It generates real income for the 28 million people who live there and directly funds the preservation of the region itself.
