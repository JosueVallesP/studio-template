## Post

I don't consult for brands any more, but if I did, here's the 2026 Substack playbook I'd suggest—

-You or someone on your team needs to spend a considerable amount of time on the platform every day. you need to get familiar with the landscape, the drama, and the tone of letters (new + old)

-A PR pitch does not work for a successful Substack writer. These are entrepreneurs keenly aware of their audience + impact. Treat them as such. 

-Notes are the new event Instagram story. Include this in your recap and bake this in as a KPI. 

-Longterm partnerships are gold. Contract inclusions in multiple letters over the course of multiple quarters for alignment that lasts. 

-Reallocate the budget you thought you were going to spend on launching a Substack of your own on partnering with writers in your category, particularly to activate their communities IRL, powered by your brand. 

-A great way to get on the radar of a writer is to be a Paid subscriber of their newsletter. A bad way? To say you're a fan but not be subscribed. 

-Last idea: pay Substackers to come talk to your team about what works from their POV + how you can best partner. A mention (organic or paid) in the right letter can shift sales/consumer sentiment in ways I guarantee you're underestimating.
