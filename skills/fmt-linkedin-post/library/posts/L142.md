## Post

I've been consulting on and off for 13 years (mostly B2B tech). Here’s what I do *now* that I NEVER did as a junior:

- Mull over an idea for weeks (sometimes months) before knowing it's ready
- Send my book to filter out leads who aren't ready
- Share findings without a slide deck
- Send 3-line proposals over email
- Deliver as little information as possible
- Make things simpler (and not more complex just to appear smarter)
- Know clients hire me for a clear direction, not a buffet of options
- Stop working when my energy drops
- Work in bursts of 2h max
- Build in public
- Know the difference between what clients say and what I find
- Say "I don't agree" if I don't agree
- Get paid before I start (50% minimum)
- Wear cosy onesies on client calls
- Go undercover before giving advice
- Don't take things personally
- Crank ideas to 100% intensity (20% execution blends the f*ck in)
- See other positioning/GTM consultants as online friends (not competition)
- I don’t sell. If anything, I’m looking for reasons to say no.
- Stay true to who I am and what I believe
- Trust my gut
- Obsess over little weird details and pull the thread

In short, junior-me thought most of this was "unprofessional." Current-me knows that's what makes me (and my clients) stand the f*ck out.
