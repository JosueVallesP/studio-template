## Post

I hate where content is going right now…

Too many Personal Brands outsource their thinking to ChatGPT which makes them look and sound like everyone else.

AI should be used as a multiplier of your expertise, not the source of it.

As someone who’s been building personal brands for 17 years…

I encourage you, start with your own thought/opinion/experience. THEN use AI to improve/refine/clarify it.

Keep what makes you unique…you.
