## Post

Ahrefs created a fake luxury brand and spent 2 months manipulating LLMs with misinformation…and it worked!

They tested a fake Elon Musk endorsements on a blog post. A Reddit AMA from a "former employee." A Medium "investigation" that debunked some obvious lies while slipping in new ones.

The Medium article won. AI trusted fake journalism over the brand's actual FAQ.
Fascinating times!
