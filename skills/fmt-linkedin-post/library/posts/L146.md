## Post

I was playing a game with our whole family last night. As we started, I made a couple (thinly veiled) sarcastic comments that were negative in nature. I didn't think much of it. 

But as the game went on, I noticed the mood had taken a turn. 

What started as a fun, goofy time together shifted into frustration and tension.

And it was 100% on me. 

My disposition to start the game created a negative tone that carried throughout the rest of the family. 

As a dad, I am the thermostat, not the thermometer. 

The way I show up with my family will set the atmosphere and tone. 

I set it poorly last night and it was amazing how quickly it spread. 


The good news in these moments: just as easily as I set a negative tone, I can shift to a positive one instead. 

Once I recognized what was happening (with the help of a few call outs!), I apologized and did a reset. 

I brought a new energy, we played another round, and it was a blast. 


Dads, your presence carries tremendous influence in your family. How you show up matters. 

So how will you show up today?
