## Post

The top 10 habits that block your potential:

1. Alcohol. By far the worst. Sorry not sorry. I went from rarely drinking to not drinking at all and it changed everything. Clarity, energy, and emotional regulation all transform when you stop. Most people don't realize the cost because they've never experienced life without it.

2. Excessive Screen Time. Staring at a screen all day for work or pleasure (doesn’t matter the intent) will suppress your power. The longer you’re plugged in, the harder it is to tune into your clarity and intuition. 

3. Societal Conditioning. Blindly following the “rules” nobody agreed to (e.g. bachelor/bachelorette parties, get married by X age, suffer and grind for success, etc.). These aren’t facts. They’re made up rules. Think for yourself! 

4. Limiting Beliefs. These sit deep within your subconscious and often you don’t realize they’re there or just how much they’re actually capping your life and potential. When I started intentionally identifying and reprogramming limiting beliefs in 2020, my life completely changed. 

5. Self-Doubt. Not trusting yourself and doing what you know is right for you moves you further and further away from your power. Trust yourself. Act on it immediately. Watch the momentum in your life build fast. 

6. Sitting Too Much / Lack of Movement. Sitting at your laptop all day is the opposite of “productive”. Moving your body, taking a walk, getting outside in nature - these activities set you up for the clarity and creativity that actually moves the needle. 

7. Complaining / Victimhood. Constantly focusing your attention on problems will keep you stuck and blocks you from everything above it. 

8. Excessive Media Consumption. Doomscrolling news, stocks, and other propaganda. You’re not “staying informed”. You’re being programmed. 

9. Trying to Control Everything. Being so set on controlling and forcing your plan actually gets in your way. All of the amazing possibilities you could’ve never dreamed of - gone! Because you tried to control everything lol

10. Low Frequency People & Relationships. It’s true - you inherit the beliefs, intentions, and habits of the people around you. This includes companies, intimate relationships, friends, influencers, and social circles - choose wisely. 

Every single one of us has far more talent and capability than we give ourselves credit for.

This is an invitation to honest look at this list. Make a few adjustments. And watch your clarity, confidence, and performance go through the roof.
