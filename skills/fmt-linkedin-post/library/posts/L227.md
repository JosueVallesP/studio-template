## Post

This was a few years ago now. 

My wife spotted a Tweet from a major influencer with 15 million followers. The influencer was looking for a business coach or advisor to help with his business and asked for recommendations.

Without hesitation, my wife responded:

"My husband could help you with this." She went the extra mile, did her research, and got in touch with the influencer's girlfriend. As a result, I made it to the final selection of candidates.

When it was my turn to pitch, I took a different approach. I said:

"I don't know if you need me as a business coach or not, but take this assessment I created called '24 Assets.' Answer these 48 questions, and then I'll tell you whether you need me, or if I should recommend someone else."

The influencer completed the assessment, and they ended up signing a huge contract with me. We did incredible work together for over 12 months, and we are still great friends to this day. It was a wonderful client relationship.

Later, I asked why they chose me out of all the applicants. They said:

"Everyone else was just telling us about how amazing they were and who they had worked with in the past. You didn't do any of that. You simply asked us to take the assessment. When we did, we realised we had all these problems we weren't even aware of. We knew we had to go with you because you were the one who identified those problems."

Standing out in the marketplace means offering real value and insights instead of just talking about past achievements.
