## Post

Last July, my co-founder took €100K and nearly my home. I was f*cked. But this year I took LinkedIn and built something NO ONE can steal:

Wtf, I’d helped build this startup. When I left, they took the money and blocked my number.

My ADHD brain went into full practical mode: "I can't lose my house too."

So I opened LinkedIn. Not to look for a job, but to survive:

- I led with the person I am, not just what I know
- I started posting mid-mess, not post-victory
- I showed my enthusiastic labrador energy in comments and DMs 

I won't pretend I had a plan. I didn't. I almost sh*t myself every time I hit publish.

But a few people believed me before I had any reason to believe myself. Then a few more showed up. Then it got LOUD:

> 7 days in, I closed a Netflix client
> 30 days in, an Uber exec DM'd me
> 5 months in, I'd made €220K from LinkedIn
> 8 months in, I helped +50 people do the same

Today, I help founders and consultants turn 10+ years of expertise into personality-first brands.

But the wire transfers aren't what rewired me.

When someone takes everything, you become OBSESSED with never doing that to someone else.

So I refuse to deliver bullsh*t and empty promises. I've been on the receiving end. I know what it tastes like.

Startup life taught me what NOT to do. LinkedIn taught me who I REALLY am.

What did your worst year build?
