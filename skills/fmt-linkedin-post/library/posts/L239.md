## Post

You're pitching to the wrong person.

We asked 101 B2B SaaS CMOs how they actually select vendors.

The finding that might change your entire sales strategy: 82% of CMOs enter after the team has narrowed the list down to the final few. 35% of CMOs only enter the buying process at the very final stage. 

• They're not doing research. 
• They're not comparing vendors. 
• They're not sitting through your discovery calls.

Their team is.

One CMO told us: "My team does the first two rounds. I come in for the final 2-3 vendors when it's time to make a call."

That's the gate. Make the shortlist or go home.

Here's what this means for your go-to-market: Your CMO-targeted ads and cold outreach might be a waste of money and effort. 

If 35% of CMOs aren't even involved until the end, you're paying to reach someone who won't see you until their team has already built the shortlist.

The people who actually decide if you make the cut? Directors. Managers. Individual contributors who'll use the tool daily.

They're the ones asking their Slack groups. Querying ChatGPT. Reading G2 reviews. Building the long list of 5-7 vendors that gets narrowed to 3.

By the time the CMO shows up, you're either on the shortlist or you're not. The CMO isn't discovering you. They're validating a decision that's mostly already made.

Stop optimizing for the CMO. Start optimizing for the team that decides if the CMO ever sees you. This goes for website messaging too.

(This is finding #2 from Wynter's 2026 CMO Software Buying Survey. Full report link in comments and Wynter website).
