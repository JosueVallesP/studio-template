## Post

$17,000,000
That's what I sold my company for in 2001.

Ten years later, I walked into Google.

Day 1. Age 46.
I noticed the age difference. 
So did everyone else.

Most people talk about feeling young in the room.
I felt old. Out of place. Was I now an imposter?

But my backpack was filled with years of experience.

Post "dot-com crash". I'm in Germany.
I ran an MBO with my redundancy cheque.
6 people who looked to me to keep their jobs.
Gave everyone equity from day one. Let's do this!
We grew the business 4X in 2 years (we didn't sleep)

Sold it for $17M. Everyone got a payday.

That's what I reminded myself of in that induction room.

I wasn't the owner anymore.
But I brought an owner's mindset.

And that's the shift nobody had told me about.
From building your own thing to corporate life.
You can still carry ownership in your bones.

What became of that owner mindset?
It built a $1 Billion+ project at Google.
That evolved into Meta. And Microsoft.

Age isn't your anchor. It's your arsenal.
