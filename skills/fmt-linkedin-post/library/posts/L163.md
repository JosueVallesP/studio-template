## Post

Arguably the hardest marketing pill to swallow (and people have been fighting it for years):

Sometimes marketing isn't measurable 🤷🏽‍♀️ 
Actually, A LOT of times marketing isn't measurable. At least not in the way executives want. 

Hear me out with these 2 real-life examples:

Example #1: I ghost-write LinkedIn posts for a lot of leaders. One came to me just last week and told me that our posts are working, despite their relatively low (yet consistent) engagement. All within 1 week, this client received 4 new leads and 2 new asset downloads from our LI posts, confirmed by her clients.

Example #2: 90% of my inbound LinkedIn leads have NEVER commented on ONE of my posts. Not one. 

Sometimes marketing works in silence. Silent lurkers, silent results...until they're not so silent. 

Sometimes marketing isn't measurable. Sometimes there's no attribution. It doesn't mean it's not working. 

And any leader who doesn't get that in 2026 will fail.
