## Post

Mike Gallardo (Sales Director at Deel) posted that an AE on his team closed an SMB deal for $1M in under 3 months 🤯 They’re an Aligned customer, so I had to interview her. Here’s a full breakdown of how she did it:

BACKGROUND:

Morgan Shelly is a superstar AE who truly GETS IT.

Deel is a $12B HR-Tech giant and leader in its space.

So transactions of this size are not uncommon.

And yet, NO buyer takes that kind of decision lightly.

Especially as an SMB.

More so when buying in <3 months.

Here’s how she did it:

1. She Sold on Investor-Level Priorities (Not Benefits)

Morgan recognized that her prospect’s recent investment brought pressure to meet certain strategic milestones. Instead of just selling Deel’s benefits, she was laser-focused on positioning the project as a catalyst for these investor-driven goals. Selling a transformation (vs benefits) is the only way to unlock big $$$.

2. She Made Team Selling a Priority on Day 1

Most of these deals die off early (or close at small ACV) simply because not enough trust is built for buyers to feel confident about this level of investment. Instead of trying to be the ‘lone wolf hero’, Morgan looped in Mike, and C-levels from day 1. This showed how committed Deel’s team was to their success and ensured they received the best buying experience possible.

3. She Planned Every Move Like a Chess Player

Morgan approached each interaction with the highest attention to detail. Before every call, she built the agendas, questions, and defined desired outcomes. She then shared these plans internally and gathered feedback to fine-tune her strategy and ensure her Team Selling is as effective as it can be. You can’t wing it in high-stakes situations. It’s all about precision.

4. She Built ‘7-Fig Trust’ Through Extreme Honesty

When her client pushed for a Sep 1st start date, Morgan didn’t overpromise. She candidly told them it wasn’t feasible and recommended Oct 1st. “It’s all about them”; That was her mindset throughout the deal. This honesty was refreshing in a world full of empty promises. It not only set her apart from competitors but built a foundation of trust that allowed them to start with such a high commitment.

5. She Turned Decision-Making Chaos into Structured Progress

Instead of drowning buyers in endless emails and attachments, Morgan used Aligned to create a single collaborative space (Deal Room). She designed a simple buying experience that helped build consensus with >8 people in only 2 months. The decision group spent 83 hours in the room, commented, and really appreciated the experience. To quote Morgan: “No way I could have used email. It’s a deal killer”.

TAKEAWAY:

Stop selling.

Start facilitating.

Morgan didn’t just close a $1M deal;

She redefined what’s possible in SMB.

(And earned a promotion to Senior AE!)
