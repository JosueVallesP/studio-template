## Post

If you’re a founder selling to B2B, read this:

This is the playbook to guarantee your first 100 customers right now

It starts with "founder-led media"

Start a simple podcast in your niche
Make it specific to the people you want as customers
It can't be generic and broad, but distinct and valuable

Invite people you want as customers
Interview them all and have a great conversation
Tell them about your product after

Then upload the long form to YouTube
Share the clips on LinkedIn, Twitter, Shorts
Use AI to turn each episode into posts, threads, carousels, lead magnets
Run thought leadership ads - boost the ones that outperform the others
Plug your product

Take the topics that do well - turn them into newsletter posts with AI 
Write about your podcast guests and make them look awesome
Share it with your guests - “It’s about you,” you tell them
Ask them to share with their network
Sponsor the newsletter with your product 

Record ONCE, publish a HUNDRED times

After you've done this 6-12 times, invite your podcast guests to a private dinner
Run it like a "salon dinner"
Good food, thoughtful group, moderated discussion about your industry
Save a few seats for prospects
Bring non-competitive sponsors who sell to the same audience
Run the dinner at net zero
Be known for hosting the best gathering in your industry 
Share the themes and insights in your newsletter after

After 10 dinners, host your FLAGSHIP summit
A full-day event for customers, prospects, podcast guests, dinner attendees
Use it to share everything you've learned through the podcast series + salon dinners
Bring in speakers from your growing first- and second-degree network
Make it informative, entertaining, and fun
Teach people things, give away prizes, build human connection
Get your sponsors to fund it
Reinvest any profits into paid ads to drive more attendance
Put your brand front and center

You end up with trust, credibility, and a media engine that pays you to acquire customers.

Run this flywheel and get new customers with almost zero marketing spend.
