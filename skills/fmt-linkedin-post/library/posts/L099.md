## Post

How I'm using AI to run a $2M MRR startup with 130 employees:

> Built Claude skills to analyze 100+ dashboards, cross-reference our database, Slack channels, meeting transcripts, etc. to surface any emerging risks and opportunities.

> Leverage several scheduled tasks to automate dozens of recurring items, nudge employees to submit their weekly priorities (petty, I know), and prepare for recurring meetings. 

> AI Show & Tell: Friday sessions where employees demo what they're building with AI. 

> AI Club: our most active Slack channel where employees share what they're building, discuss the latest capabilities, ask questions, and help each other improve their AI competence. 

And admittedly I still feel like a massive n00b. 

My least favorite part of being a founder is having very little down time to explore and experiment outside of core work. I'd love nothing more than to irrationally buy a Mac Mini and spend 36 hours straight building personal agents. 

But we're pushing VERY hard on AI competence and adoption. We're burning $700K /mo atm. The only way to escape the venture flywheel of trading control for capital is to radically transform how we operate. 

It doesn't feel optional anymore; it's the only way forward.
