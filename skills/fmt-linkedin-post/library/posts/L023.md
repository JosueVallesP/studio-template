## Post

As the CEO of a 150-person company (and a father of four), people often ask me how I "do it all." The honest answer? I don't. To survive (and actually enjoy) both, I rely on two things:

1. A true team effort at home. My wife, Nasia, is the CEO of our household logistics. She manages the schools, the schedules, and the chaos. Without her, my work wouldn't be possible.

2. Aggressive outsourcing. I have a demanding job running TestGorilla, and I have a large family. To make that equation work without burning out, I decided to stop doing chores entirely. If I can hire someone to do the gardening, cooking, or cleaning, I do it. I pay people to run my house so I can just be a Dad.

I want my kids to remember me playing with them, not stressing over a pile of laundry. 

It allows me to keep my weekends sacrosanct. 

From Friday evening to Monday morning, I am not a CEO. I am just a father.

That is the best investment I make every month.
