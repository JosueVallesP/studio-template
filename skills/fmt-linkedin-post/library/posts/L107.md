## Post

We’re doomscrolling through the best era in human history.

Global poverty is at historic lows. Violence is down. Literacy is up. Life expectancy keeps climbing.

And yet, everyone's convinced the world is ending.

Put a human in paradise and they'll find a reason to be miserable by Thursday. Land your dream job and you'll be doom-spiraling about your 401k by lunch. Get the perfect relationship and suddenly you’re annoyed at how they chew.

I've written about this before—after Subtle Art landed on the bestseller lists, I had everything I thought I wanted and still couldn't get out of bed. What I didn't understand at the time was why I felt so depressed.

Now I realize: I was out of problems—and I needed one.

Humans have this weird thing where we can't just be OK. We're addicted to feeling like things are wrong.

Because if everything's fine, there's nothing to hope for. 

So we invent problems, we manufacture crises, and we find something—anything—to worry about. Just so we have hope. 

Welcome to being human.
