## Post

I spent 2 hours with an actor who said no to $14.5M.

Here are 7 lessons from Matthew McConaughey that will change how you think about yourself and your choices:

1. We forgive ourselves too quickly. We hop up and dust ourselves off without sitting in the guilt long enough to make sure we never do it again.

2. The courage to keep going is easy. The harder courage is stopping to figure out why you keep stepping in the same pothole.

3. Don't study the result. Study what the person was doing when they got there. Model the rise, not the result.

4. So many people have a renter's mentality. They never give the relationship a chance to become a friendship, or the house a chance to become a home.

5. Be quick, but don't be in a hurry. It's like a Lego set. Rush it, skip the directions, and you'll finish with 12 pieces left over.

6. Humility shouldn’t make you shrink. It’s realising you’re not done learning.

7. Success without profit is a trap. You can have the most money and the most toys but still can't sleep, your relationships are broken, and none of it means anything. Success is the quantity. Profit is the quality. You need both.
