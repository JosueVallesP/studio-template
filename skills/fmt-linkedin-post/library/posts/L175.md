## Post

The best writing tip I’ve received since I became a content writer 3.5 months ago was to play a game. 

Here are the 3 rules. 

1/ Before you click the post button, highlight all the verbs in your draft. 

2/ Stop at each verb and ask the following question: Is this as vivid as possible? (I bet the answer’s no.)

Just look at this example: 

“The chandelier is on the ceiling.” 

The verb in this sentence = “is.” The word “is” doesn’t describe action -- what the object’s doing at the moment. In this case, the chandelier just happens to exist on the ceiling. Pretty boring. 

3/ Replace the old verb with a new verb. One that’s specific and colorful. 

Attempt # 2: “The chandelier hangs from the ceiling.” 

Your brain draws a better picture. The chandelier acts. It hangs down into the room, swoops over everyone’s heads and leaves just enough space for them not to scratch their heads on the crystals. All because of one verb. 

What 85% of writers don’t realize is that readers think in images, not ideas. As a result, writers spend hours reordering sentence structure and refining their message when they should be painting a picture with their writing. 

Not the end of the world. Just a missed opportunity. 

Especially when this drill only takes 4 mins.
