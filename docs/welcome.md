**Welcome to your content studio.**

The studio turns what you believe, what you've done and how you talk into posts, essays and pictures that sound like you. It learns about you from what you've already made and from short interviews, and it keeps what it learns in a set of files in this folder. It never makes up a story, a client or a number about you. When it doesn't know something, it asks. You make the call on every piece.

**The order to do things in.** Each step gives you something you can use the same day, so you don't have to finish setup before it pays off.

1. **Answer the setup questions** (about five to ten minutes). Say "set up my studio". A few questions about facts: what you do, where you publish, how much time you have. You get a one-page guide to your own studio.
2. **See how your ideas connect** (none of your time while it reads, then about 20 minutes). Hand over what you've already made (posts, essays, talks, call transcripts: paste them or attach the files), then say "/map". The studio reads all of it in one sitting and shows you what you can't see from inside your own work: the few claims the rest hangs from, the same idea said many ways, two ideas that make a new one, what you tell clients and have never published. Then you go through the strongest findings and say what's true.
3. **Set up Figma and Notion, if you use them** (about 10 minutes each). Say "set up my Figma" for a kit to make the pictures for your posts, and "set up my dashboard" for one page in Notion that shows what's waiting on you. Both are optional.
4. **See your libraries** (about 10 minutes). Say "/libraries". Your studio came with hundreds of real LinkedIn posts, real Substack essays and proven ways to draw an idea, all sorted by the job each one does for the reader. The studio shows you the few that fit what you already have.
5. **Make your first post** (about 15 minutes). Paste something you've already made into this chat, like an old post, notes from a talk or a call transcript, or attach the file to your message, and say "write a post from this". The studio picks one of those moves for it.
6. **Do the interviews** (20 to 30 minutes each, about eleven or twelve over a few weeks). Say "start my brand tree". Each one covers one layer of what you believe, starting from what your map found. The studio asks about things that happened in your work, not how you'd describe yourself, and nothing it writes down gets used until you say it's right.
7. **Build your strategy** (one session). Say "build my strategy". What to be known for, for whom, where and how often, with the reason for each choice.
8. **Write your manifesto, if you want one** (one session, once your thesis is confirmed). Say "/manifesto". Why you do what you do, in one piece.
9. **Make it yours** (one session). Say "build my look". The studio builds your look from what the interviews found.
10. **Run the check** (about 20 minutes). Say "/check". One piece goes through every step with your own files, so you see the whole thing work once.

You can skip any step except the first, and come back to it later. If you skip one, the studio tells you what you lose.

**What you can do any time, even before the interviews:**

- "/post" or "/essay" makes a LinkedIn post or a Substack essay from something you hand over.
- "/visual" works out the right picture for an idea.
- "/shape" finds the ideas worth posting in a call, a memo or a transcript.
- "/capture" saves an idea the moment it happens.
- "/journal" is a private journal with questions that help you find out what you think.
- "/refine" takes a draft that feels flat and makes it better.
- "/adapt" turns an essay into posts, or a talk into an essay.
- "/teardown" breaks down why someone else's post works, so you can learn the move.
- "/think" shows you what your files add up to. "/think about" and a topic works one idea through with you.
- "/scout" finds real cases, evidence and the best argument against a point of yours.
- "/recall" finds what you've already said about a topic.
- "/strategy" gives you a draft plan from your material; the interviews confirm it later.

A few things wait for the interviews: a strategy that rests on what you've confirmed, your manifesto ("/manifesto"), and having the studio run your whole week.

**Handing things over.** Paste text straight into the chat. For a file (a PDF, a Word document, your logo), drag it into the box where you type and send it; the studio puts it in your inbox, a folder in your studio where things you hand over wait until it reads them. You never need to find that folder or sort anything.

**Three to remember.** "/start" gives you the next step during setup. "/status" shows what's waiting on you after it. "/end" wraps up the session before you leave. You never have to save anything; the studio saves after every reply.
